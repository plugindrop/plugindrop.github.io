What Is Surge XT?

Surge XT is a free, open-source hybrid synthesizer. Originally a commercial synth by Vember Audio, it was open-sourced in 2018 and has since been rebuilt by a community of developers. The “XT” version (2022) was a major overhaul — new UI, new features, new oscillator types. It covers subtractive, wavetable, FM, and additive synthesis in one plugin. Comparable commercial synths cost $150-$250.

Key Features

  • 3 oscillators — Each can run in Classic (subtractive), Wavetable, Window, FM2, FM3, Sine, or Twist mode
  • 2 filters with 14 algorithms — Ladder, SVF, Comb, S&H, and more, with serial/parallel routing
  • 1000+ factory presets — Organized by category, covering pads, leads, basses, FX, keys, and more
  • Built-in FX section — Reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, rotary, distortion, EQ, conditioner, and more
  • Deep modulation — Extensive modulation matrix with LFOs, envelopes, macros, and MIDI sources
  • MPE support — Full MIDI Polyphonic Expression support for expressive controllers
