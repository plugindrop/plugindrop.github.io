What Is Plural?

Plural is a free pitch-shifting sequencer plugin from Urban Kits, a developer focused on building tools for bedroom producers and beatmakers. It lets you run audio through a step sequencer that shifts pitch up or down by an octave per step — creating rhythmic, tonal movement from any loop or sound source. Think of it as an instant way to inject motion and texture into static audio. Urban Kits previously released Bedroom Guitar (a free sampled nylon guitar plugin) in October 2025, and Plural continues their streak of putting genuinely useful tools out at no cost.

Key Features

  • Up to 16-step sequencer — Program pitch shifts across a customizable sequence length for rhythmic variation.
  • ±1 octave pitch range per step — Shift each step up or down a full octave, sticking to octave intervals to avoid dissonance.
  • Variable playback speed — Adjust from quarter speed to 4x speed, letting you sync the effect to different tempos and grooves.
  • Smooth control — Shape how pitch transitions between steps, from hard jumps to gliding shifts.
  • Mix and Master controls — Blend the wet/dry signal and dial in the output level without leaving the plugin.
  • AU and VST2 formats — Works in most major DAWs on both macOS (10.13+) and Windows (10+).
