What Is The Very Loud Indeed Co.?

The Very Loud Indeed Co. is a UK-based sound design company focused on cinematic scoring tools for composers and producers. Their catalog spans Kontakt sample libraries, Omnisphere 2 soundsets, Serum presets, and u-he Zebra2 patches — all built around dark, textural, and hybrid-organic sound design. Think thriller underscore, horror atmospheres, and moody cinematic textures.

Key Features of Their Catalog

  • Kontakt Sample Libraries — Deep-sampled instruments covering everything from mutated cellos (Oblique series) to dark scoring drones (Outland series), all running in Native Instruments Kontakt.
  • Omnisphere 2 Soundsets — Collections like Corvus and Mirai featuring exclusive sound sources recorded and designed specifically for Spectrasonics’ Omnisphere 2.
  • Dark Explorations Series — The Oblique line (cello, bass, violin) is built around haunting, expressive string textures processed through heavy sound design.
  • Hybrid Percussion & Textures — Libraries like Chimaera (mutated pitched percussion), Thump, and Domus bring unconventional rhythmic elements to scoring sessions.
  • Serum & Zebra Support — Not just Kontakt — they also cover Xfer Serum and u-he Zebra2/Dark Zebra for producers working outside the sampler world.
  • Bundle Options — Curated bundles (Shift, Rhythm, Horror) package multiple libraries at additional savings even beyond the 60% sale price.
