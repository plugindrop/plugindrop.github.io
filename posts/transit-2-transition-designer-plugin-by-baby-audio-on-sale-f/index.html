What Is Transit 2?

Transit 2 is a motion effects plugin from Baby Audio, developed in collaboration with YouTuber and producer Andrew Huang. It’s designed to handle transitions, risers, drops, sweeps, and rhythmic motion — the kind of stuff that normally requires stacking multiple plugins and drawing tedious automation curves. Instead, Transit 2 lets you link multiple effect parameters to a single macro control and sweep them all at once. It runs as a VST, VST3, AU, and AAX plugin (64-bit) on macOS 10.11+ (including Apple Silicon) and Windows 10+.

Key Features

  • 28 Effect Modules — Reverb, delay, distortion, filters, pitch shifting, plus newer additions like Loop, Warp, Reverser, and Retroverb, all available across 7 effect slots.
  • 6 Motion Modes — Macro, LFO, Follower, Sidechain, Gate, and Sequencer give you different ways to animate your effects beyond simple automation.
  • 968 Presets — A massive library designed by Andrew Huang and professional sound designers, covering everything from subtle movement to full-on transition chaos.
  • Randomization Engine — Dial in controlled randomness to specific parameters for quick inspiration and happy accidents.
  • Tempo-Synced Modulation — Speed, depth, and range all lock to your project’s BPM, keeping transitions musical rather than messy.
  • Macro Knob Workflow — Link any combination of parameters to a single knob. One sweep = instant riser, washout, or breakdown.
