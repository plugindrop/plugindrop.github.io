What Is The Very Loud Indeed Co.?

The Very Loud Indeed Co. is a UK-based developer specializing in cinematic sound design tools for composers and sound designers. Their catalogue spans Kontakt sample libraries, Omnisphere soundsets, Zebra 2 patches, Serum presets, and more — all geared toward film, TV, and game scoring. Think dark textures, hybrid percussion, mutated guitars, and eerie vocal explorations. They’ve built a focused niche in the tension-and-atmosphere corner of scoring.

Key Features of Their Catalogue

  • Kontakt Libraries — Covers horror, percussion, pads & textures, strings, transitions, vocals, guitars, and bass. Many run on the free Kontakt Player.
  • Omnisphere Soundsets — Titles like Mirai, Corvus, Cobalt, and Altura deliver cinematic patches for Spectrasonics’ flagship synth.
  • Zebra 2 & Knifonium Presets — Patches for u-he’s Zebra 2 and Plugin Alliance’s Knifonium extend their scoring palette into deeper synthesis territory.
  • Signature Instruments — Standouts include SHIFT (hybrid scoring transitions), Mobilis Ultimate (cinematic percussion), BANSHEE (dark vocal textures), and COIL (mutated scoring guitars).
  • Bundles Available — Multi-product bundles like the “Everything So Far Bundle” offer additional value on top of sale pricing.
