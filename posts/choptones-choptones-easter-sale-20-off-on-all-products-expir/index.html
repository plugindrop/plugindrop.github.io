What Is ChopTones?

ChopTones is a developer specializing in guitar amp modeling profiles and impulse responses (IRs) for platforms like Kemper, Fractal Audio, Line 6 Helix, Neural DSP Quad Cortex, and other popular amp modelers. Their catalog covers a wide range of amp tones — from classic vintage cleans to modern high-gain — captured from real amplifiers. They’ve built a following among guitarists who want studio-quality amp tones without hauling a full rig.
