What Is Rhodes Software?

Rhodes — yes, that Rhodes — makes official virtual instrument and effects plugins modeled on their iconic electric pianos. Their software lineup includes the V8 and V8 Pro virtual instruments (hyper-realistic recreations of the Rhodes MK8 electric piano) and the V-Rack multi-effects plugin (based on the MK8’s analog preamp and FX circuit). These are the only officially licensed Rhodes plugins on the market, built from samples and modeling of actual Rhodes hardware.

Key Features

  • 30,000 samples from the MK8 — The V8 is built from recordings of Rhodes’ flagship MK8 piano, capturing its full tonal range with remarkable detail.
  • Up to 127 velocity layers — Fine-grained dynamic response across the keyboard for expressive, realistic playing.
  • 14 articulations — Sustains, staccatos, portamentos, legatos, note-offs, sympathetic resonances, and mechanical noises are all included.
  • Modeled MK8 FX board (V8 Pro) — VCA Compressor with sidechain, 4-Stage VCA Phaser, Bucket-Brigade Chorus, and Bucket-Brigade Delay.
  • V-Rack standalone effects — Seven classic analog effect emulations including 3-mode Drive, resonant 3-band parametric EQ, Vari-Pan, and the full MK8 effects chain.
  • Cross-platform support — Available as VST2, VST3, AU, and AAX for both Mac and PC.

What Producers Are Saying

The Rhodes V8 generated significant buzz as the first official Rhodes plugin. On forums like KVR Audio and Reddit, producers frequently highlight the authenticity of the sampling and the expressive velocity response. The V-Rack has drawn attention as a versatile effects unit that works well beyond just electric piano duties. Community discussions commonly note the large install size (~22 GB) but agree the sonic quality justifies it.

Who Is It For?

  • Neo-soul, jazz, and R&B producers — The Rhodes electric piano sound is foundational to these genres, and the V8 delivers it from the official source.
  • Singer-songwriters and session keyboardists — Designed for expressive, dynamic playing with deep velocity sensitivity and multiple articulations.
  • Lo-fi and chill beat makers — The built-in effects chain (chorus, phaser, compression) in the V8 Pro is tailored for warm, textured keys.

FAQ

Q: Do the Rhodes plugins require iLok? A: Check the official Rhodes or Plugin Boutique product page for current authorization details.

Q: Can I try before I buy? A: Yes — Rhodes offers a free 14-day trial for both the V8 and V8 Pro.

Q: What’s the difference between V8 and V8 Pro? A: The V8 Pro adds the modeled MK8 FX board (compressor, phaser, chorus, delay) and per-note controls for timbre, damper, level, and tuning. The standard V8 focuses on the core piano engine.
