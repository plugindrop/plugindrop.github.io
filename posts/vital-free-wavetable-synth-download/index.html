What Is Vital?

Vital is a wavetable synthesizer created by Matt Tytel. It launched in 2020 and immediately drew comparisons to Xfer Serum ($189) due to its visual modulation system and wavetable engine. The free tier is not a trial — it’s a permanent, fully functional version of the synth. Paid tiers (Plus at $25, Pro at $80) unlock additional presets and wavetables.

Key Features

  • 3 wavetable oscillators — Each with independent wavetable position, spectral warping, and phase randomization
  • Visual modulation — Drag any modulation source (LFO, envelope, macro) directly onto any parameter
  • Spectral warping — Bend, smear, and reshape wavetables in real time
  • 75 presets in free tier — Enough to start producing immediately
  • Advanced LFOs — Drawable LFO shapes with up to 32 points
  • Built-in effects — Chorus, compressor, delay, distortion, EQ, filter, flanger, phaser, reverb
