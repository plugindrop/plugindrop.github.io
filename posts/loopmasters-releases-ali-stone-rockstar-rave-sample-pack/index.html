What Is Ali Stone – Rockstar Rave?

Ali Stone – Rockstar Rave is a new EDM-focused sample pack from Loopmasters, built around the sonic world of Ali Stone’s albums Pandora and A Través Del Espejo. Recorded in Ali Stone’s Los Angeles studio using Roland hardware, UAD plugins, and Ableton Live 12, the collection sits at the crossroads of industrial rave, rock, and Latin-influenced electronic music. Think distorted guitar riffs tangled with analog synth textures, bass growls colliding with Colombian-rooted percussion, and bilingual English/Spanish vocal elements throughout.
