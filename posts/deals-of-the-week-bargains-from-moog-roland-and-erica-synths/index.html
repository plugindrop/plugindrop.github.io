What Is the SP-404 MKII?

The SP-404 MKII is Roland’s flagship portable sampler, billed as the most powerful entry in the SP-404 series to date. It’s a hardware sampler built for beat-making, live performance, and on-the-go production. Roland has continued to expand its capabilities through firmware updates since launch.

Key Features

  • 16GB Internal Memory — room for large sample libraries without constant file management
  • 32-Voice Polyphony — handle complex layered beats and chops without voice-stealing
  • OLED Display — improved visual feedback over previous SP-404 models
  • VariPhrase Time Stretching — manipulate sample tempo and pitch independently
  • Internal Resampling — bounce effects and layers internally without external gear
  • Lo-Fi Effects Suite — vinyl simulation, cassette textures, and other character effects built in

What Producers Are Saying

The SP-404 MKII has built a dedicated community since its release. On Reddit’s r/SP404 and various beat-making forums, users commonly praise the workflow improvements over the original MK2 and the ongoing firmware support from Roland. It’s frequently recommended as a go-to portable sampler for lo-fi, hip-hop, and experimental producers.

What Is the Moog Messenger?

The Moog Messenger is a monophonic analog synthesizer released in 2025. It combines Moog’s classic ladder filter design with modern modulation capabilities, including dual oscillators with wavefolders, a sub oscillator, an arpeggiator, and a built-in sequencer.

Key Features

  • Two Oscillators with Wavefolders — expanded tonal shaping beyond standard Moog waveforms
  • Classic Ladder Filter — the signature Moog low-pass sound
  • Sub Oscillator — added low-end weight
  • Built-in Arpeggiator and Sequencer — melodic and rhythmic tools without external gear

Who Is It For?

  • Moog enthusiasts — a newer entry in the Moog analog lineup

What Is the Zen Delay?

The Zen Delay is a desktop effects unit developed by Erica Synths in collaboration with the Ninja Tune label. It’s designed as a creative delay processor for studio and live use, combining digital delay with analog filtering and tube saturation.

Key Features

  • Five Delay Modes — multiple algorithms for different textures
  • 5-Second Maximum Delay Time — extended delay for ambient and experimental work
  • Analog Multi-Mode Filter — shape the delayed signal with resonant filtering
  • Tube Overdrive Stage — analog warmth and saturation on the output

Who Is It For?

  • Sound designers and ambient producers — the long delay times and filter open up experimental territory
  • Live performers — designed to sit on a desk or in a live rig
