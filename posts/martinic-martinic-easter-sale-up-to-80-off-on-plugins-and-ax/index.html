What Is Martinic AX73?

Martinic is a developer known for meticulously modeled vintage instrument plugins. Their flagship product, the AX73, is a dual-layer virtual analog synth based on the classic Akai AX-73, AX-60, and AX-80 hardware synthesizers. It uses Martinic’s proprietary Advanced Circuitry Emulation (ACE) technology to recreate the character of those original analog circuits. Beyond the AX73 synth itself, Martinic also offers effect plugins like the Colorsound Tremolo, Scanner Vibrato, Lem Echo Music, AXFX, Elka Panther, and the Pianet T electric piano — all of which are included in this Easter sale.

Key Features

  • Dual synth layers — Two full layers that can be split across the keyboard or stacked, each with independent oscillators, filters, and arpeggiators
  • Four analog-modeled oscillators — Two oscillators plus sub-oscillator and noise per layer, processed through ACE circuit modeling
  • Deep modulation — Four envelopes and four LFOs per layer (eight total of each), with flexible assignment routing
  • Eight built-in effects — Compressor, Phaser, Flanger, Chorus, Delay, Reverb, Distortion, and EQ with customizable signal routing
  • 600+ presets — Includes recreations of the original AX-73 factory patches plus a large library of modern sound designs
  • Wide format support — Available as VST, VST3, AU, AAX, and CLAP on both Windows and macOS
