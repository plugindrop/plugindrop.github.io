What Is Analogue Micro House?

Analogue Micro House is a sample pack from Thick Sounds, released on Loopmasters. It’s built around analogue melodies, forward-thinking drum programming, and mellow synthetic basses — the DNA of the micro house genre. The pack draws direct inspiration from artists like Ricardo Villalobos, Kenny Larkin, Akufen, and Tiefschwarz. Everything is delivered in 24-bit / 44.1kHz WAV format across 852 MB of content.

Key Features

  • 139 Loops across multiple categories — 50 drum loops, 20 bass loops, 48 top loops, 9 percussion loops, 8 synth loops, and 4 noise loops covering the 125–128 BPM range.
  • 122 One-Shots — 68 drum hits, 25 bass hits, 19 synth hits, and 10 FX samples for building patterns from scratch.
  • 10 Xfer Serum Presets — Playable synth patches designed to match the pack’s analogue-leaning aesthetic.
  • 24 MIDI Files — Drop the melodic and bass phrases straight into your DAW and tweak freely.
  • 3 Construction Kits — Full song starters with 6–9 stems each, broken down for easy remixing and arrangement.
  • Modular purchasing — Individual sections (drums, bass, synths, etc.) available separately starting at £3.95 if you don’t need the full pack.
