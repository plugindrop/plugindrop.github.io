What Is Topos?

Topos is a saturation, amp simulation, and speaker modeling plugin developed by Forever 89. Rather than a traditional preset-based approach, it’s built around a pair of interactive “Map Views” — one for the amp section, one for the speaker section — that let you visually morph between different tonal characters by dragging a point across a 2D space. The idea is topographical tone exploration: blend between classic hardware voicings instead of toggling through static presets. It’s available in VST3 and AU formats (64-bit).

Key Features

  • 35 Saturation Models — Covers vintage tube amps, distortion pedals, tape machines, and classic preamps in a single plugin.
  • Interactive Map Views — Two visual maps (Amp and Speaker) allow seamless morphing between multiple models, making it fast to audition and blend tones.
  • Speaker Simulation Section — Shape tone and perceived space with Fidelity, Size, and Presence controls for detailed speaker design.
  • Input Filter — Low/high-pass filtering with adjustable slopes to tailor the tonal range before it hits the amp stage.
  • Output Stage Controls — Flux and Dynamics parameters for tightening transients and smoothing peaks after amplification.
  • Zero-Latency Processing — CPU-friendly, anti-aliased processing designed for real-time use without system strain.
