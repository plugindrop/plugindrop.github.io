What Is ACE Studio 2?

ACE Studio 2.0 is an AI-powered singing voice synthesizer and music production suite developed by Timedomain Tech. It generates realistic vocal performances from MIDI and lyrics input — no vocalist required. Version 2.0 expanded well beyond vocals, adding AI-driven virtual instruments, generative composition tools, and audio processing features like stem splitting and vocal-to-MIDI conversion. It integrates into standard DAW workflows via the ACE Bridge plugin (VST3, AU, AAX).

Key Features

  • 140+ AI Voices in 8 Languages — Covers English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, Italian, French, and Portuguese with styles ranging from Pop and Rap to Chanson and Kids Choir.
  • Verse25 Vocal Synth Engine — The core synthesis model behind ACE Studio 2’s vocal generation, handling pitch, timing, vibrato, dynamics, and emotional intensity.
  • AI Instruments (Chorus25 Model) — Realistic string, brass, and woodwind performances from MIDI input, including violin, cello, saxophone, trumpet, and duduk.
  • Voice Cloning & VoiceMix — Train custom voice models from your own recordings, or blend existing voices together without any training data.
  • Generative AI Kits — Three tools: Inspire Me (idea generation), Music Enhancer (arrangement assistance), and Add a Layer (loop/layer generation).
  • DAW Integration via ACE Bridge — VST3/AU/AAX plugin with ARA-based tempo sync for seamless in-DAW editing.
