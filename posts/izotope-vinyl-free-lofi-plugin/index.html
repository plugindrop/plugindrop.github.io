What Is iZotope Vinyl?

iZotope Vinyl is a free lo-fi effect plugin that simulates the sound of vinyl records. Originally released in 2001, it’s one of the longest-running free plugins in the industry. iZotope is known for professional mastering (Ozone), audio repair (RX), and mixing tools (Neutron). Vinyl gives you their signal processing quality in a focused lo-fi tool at no cost.

Key Features

  • Year selector — Dial from 1930 to 2000 to shift the overall lo-fi character from extreme to subtle
  • Dust control — Adds vinyl crackle and pop artifacts at adjustable intensity
  • Scratch simulation — Mimics record surface scratches and imperfections
  • Warp effect — Simulates wow and flutter from a warped or uneven record
  • Electrical noise — Adds 50Hz/60Hz hum and electrical interference
  • Simple one-page interface — All controls on a single vintage-styled panel
