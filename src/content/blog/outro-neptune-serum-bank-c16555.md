## Where it fits in a session

A Serum preset bank is most useful early in a track, when you're auditioning sounds rather than committing to a signal chain. Load patches during the sketch phase to find a lead or bass tone that sets the direction for a section.

It also works well as a sound-design shortcut for arrangement filler: sequenced or arpeggiated patches can seed a secondary melodic line or a rhythmic texture layer without much extra programming. From there, you'd still shape the patch further with your own effects chain, automation, and mix processing to fit the rest of the track.
