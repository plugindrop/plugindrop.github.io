## Where it fits in a session

Raw drum samples like these sit early in a bass-music arrangement, right where you build the beat before layering bass and sub elements on top. Producers typically load individual hits into a drum sampler or chop them across pads to build a dubstep groove from scratch. Because the samples are unprocessed, they also work as a starting point for further sound design: pitching, saturating, or parallel-compressing a kick or snare to match a track's energy.

They're also useful for reinforcing weak spots in an existing beat. A producer with a half-finished drum bus can pull in a single transient-heavy snare or clap from the pack to sit on top of a softer original hit, rather than rebuilding the whole pattern.
