## What Is VISION 4X?

VISION 4X is a multi-channel spectral analyzer plugin developed by Excite Audio, built for frequency visualization during mixing and mastering. Unlike a standard single-channel analyzer, it allows you to monitor multiple audio streams simultaneously in one display — making direct frequency comparisons a visual task rather than a switch-and-listen guessing game. It sits firmly in the utility category: no processing, no coloring, just analysis.

---

## Key Features

- **Multi-channel simultaneous display** — View up to four separate audio streams at once, color-coded in a single frequency window for direct comparison between tracks or buses.
- **High-resolution FFT analysis** — Fine-grained spectral visualization across the full audible range, suited to precision decisions at the mixing and mastering stage.
- **Real-time spectrum monitoring** — Continuous, low-latency visual feedback as audio plays, giving an accurate picture of frequency content without display lag introducing confusion.
- **Reference track comparison** — Designed specifically for workflows where a mix needs to be held against a reference, letting you see frequency relationships rather than just hear them.
- **Readable multi-channel interface** — Channel differentiation by color and layering keeps the display functional even when all streams are active simultaneously.
