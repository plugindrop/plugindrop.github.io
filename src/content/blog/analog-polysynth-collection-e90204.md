## Where it fits in a session

These engines suit pad, bass, and lead duties in any session built around analog-style synthesis — think synth-pop basslines, cinematic pads, or classic house chords. The JUNO-60 and JUNO-106 cover chorus-laden pads and basses, while the JUPITER-4 and JUPITER-8 lean toward richer, more layered patches for leads and sustained chords. The JX-3P adds a brighter, more digital-leaning analog tone that can sit well in a mix next to the warmer Junos without masking them.

Because the collection includes an advanced arpeggiator and onboard effects on each instrument, it can also work as a self-contained sound-design station rather than something that depends on external processing chains.
