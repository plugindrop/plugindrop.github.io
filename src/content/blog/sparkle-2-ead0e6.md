## What Is SPARKLE 2?

SPARKLE 2 is a virtual electric rhythm guitar plugin developed by UJAM, the Berlin-based studio behind a wide range of virtual instruments and effects. Described as "a true team player," it covers everything from funky clean tones to indie rock crunch, making it practical for chord work, octaves, and riff-based parts. The sample library was recorded through a customized vintage single-coil pickup guitar, giving it a grounded, realistic foundation rather than a purely synthesized feel. It runs fully inside your DAW — no real guitar, amp, or external pedals required.

## Key Features

- **30+ guitar styles with 350+ playable phrases** — spans funk, brit pop, indie rock, and contemporary electronic pop out of the box
- **Instrument mode** — step beyond preset phrases for freer, more expressive real-time performance
- **Open Chords** — adds voicing variety to standard chord patterns, reducing the mechanical sameness that plagues some sample-based guitar plugins
- **30 Stomp Box emulations** — pre-amp effects covering common guitar pedal types so you can shape tone before it hits the amp stage
- **60 Finisher FX modes** — post-amp processing for texture, atmosphere, and creative sound design without chaining additional plugins
- **Internet-based authorization via the UJAM App** — no iLok dongle required; activates online through UJAM's own app
