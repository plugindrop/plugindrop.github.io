## FabFilter Twin 3: Core Purpose and Controls
<!-- role:intro -->
<!-- pick:FabFilter Twin 3 -->

FabFilter Twin 3 is built around analog-modeled oscillators, four filters, and an effects section. A fourth oscillator and built-in, analog-modelled drift give the instrument its own character, and both oscillator and filter sound quality have been refined this version. Each filter adds panning along with adjustable slope and shape, moving between low, high, and band pass, bell, low and high shelf, and notch. Reverb, delay, chorus, phaser/flanger, drive, and compression round out the toolset. Ring modulation for oscillator 1+2 and 3+4 brings another way to combine the oscillators.

For building and tweaking sounds, there is Easy drag-and-drop modulation with 100-slot modulation matrix, backed by 16-step XLFOs, XY controllers, envelope generators, envelope followers, and MIDI sources. 64-voice polyphony and unison covers stacking and layering notes. The window supports Resizable interface, full screen mode and customizable scaling, letting it fit different screens and setups. Factory presets cover sound design needs from the start.

## Sound Shaped By Four Controls
<!-- role:character -->

Twin 3 leans on its oscillators and filters, but several controls set how far you can push them. Easy drag-and-drop modulation with 100-slot modulation matrix lets you link nearly any control to any source without digging through menus, so sound design stays fluid rather than fiddly. Ring modulation for oscillator 1+2 and 3+4 adds another way to combine the four oscillators beyond basic mixing. With 64-voice polyphony and unison, dense layered patches and thick unison stacks stay playable at once. The High Quality option, enabling internal oversampling for the best results, switches on for patches that call for it, while the standard mode keeps things light for everyday work. Together these controls turn the four-oscillator core into something you can shape in detail rather than just tweak at the surface.
