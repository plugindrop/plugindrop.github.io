## What Is the UAD Little Labs Voice Of God?

The Little Labs Voice Of God Bass Resonance is Universal Audio's official plug-in recreation of Little Labs' acclaimed 500-series hardware module. Originally built to add weight and chest resonance to voiceover recordings, Voice Of God has become a go-to for tuning and shaping low-end frequencies across kicks, bass, 808s, and vocals. Previously, the UAD version required Apollo hardware or a UAD Accelerator. Now it runs natively on macOS and Windows — opening it up to any DAW setup.

## Key Features

- **Two-Knob Simplicity** — Dial in the target frequency and adjust the effect amount (amplitude) without menu-diving.
- **Switchable Frequency Centers** — Two buttons set the sweepable range center to 40 Hz or 100 Hz. Press both for 200 Hz.
- **Bass Resonance Tuning** — Goes beyond standard EQ by letting you tune the exact bass frequency a track needs, adding focused resonance rather than broad boost.
- **Vocal Authority** — Adds chest resonance and "heft" to vocal recordings, faithful to the original hardware's voiceover roots.
- **Low-End Cleanup** — Roll off muddy bass frequencies to improve overall mix clarity.
- **Native + Hardware Modes** — Runs natively in your DAW or accelerated on Apollo interfaces and UAD Accelerators. Supports VST, VST3, Audio Units, and AAX.

## Who Is It For?

- **Hip-Hop & Trap Producers** — Designed for tuning kick drums and 808s with surgical bass control that goes beyond a parametric EQ.
- **Voiceover & Podcast Engineers** — The original hardware was built for vocal work; this plug-in adds chest resonance and low-end authority to spoken word.
- **Mix Engineers** — Useful for tightening muddy low-end across full mixes, bass buses, or individual tracks.
- **UAD Users Going Native** — Producers who previously needed Apollo hardware can now use Voice Of God in any DAW setup on macOS or Windows.
