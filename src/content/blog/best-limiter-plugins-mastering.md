## W1 Limiter

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:W1 Limiter -->

W1 Limiter — Reliable, free brickwall that earns its place

- **Developer:** Jeroen Breebaart

- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS

- **Formats:** VST

W1 Limiter is a free, open-source brickwall limiter built on the same conceptual foundation as the Waves L1 Ultramaximizer. Its interface is deliberately minimal — a threshold, a release, and an output ceiling — which removes decision paralysis and produces consistently clean results on most material. On acoustic and melodic content, it competes with plugins at many times its price. It won't give you true peak metering or multiple algorithm modes, so treat it as a starting point rather than a permanent solution once you're delivering to multiple streaming platforms.

**Best for:** Anyone who wants a no-cost, no-fuss brickwall for streaming targets without sacrificing result quality on acoustic material.

## FabFilter Pro-L 2

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Pro-L 2 -->

FabFilter Pro-L 2 remains the benchmark true peak limiter for engineers who want one tool that covers every genre and delivery format without compromise. It offers eight distinct limiting algorithms — Transparent, Aggressive, Bus, Dynamic, Allround, Surgical, Smooth, and Safe — each carrying its own character and purpose, so the plugin suits mastering and mixing work across mono, stereo, or surround material, including external side-chain triggering for stem mastering.

The plugin pairs a genuinely transparent sound with maximum achievable loudness, built on true peak limiting and up to 32x linear-phase oversampling. Output and gain reduction metering, including true peak metering, is highly accurate, and an optional DC offset filter rounds out the signal path. Adjustable look-ahead, attack, and release settings let each of the eight algorithms shape the limiting curve to suit the material being treated.

It runs on macOS 10.12 or later, including Apple Silicon Macs, as well as Windows 7 through 11, in 64-bit form on both platforms.

Best for: any engineer who wants a single limiter that handles every genre, every target loudness, and every delivery format without compromise.

**Q: What are the best limiter plugins for mastering in 2026?**

W1 Limiter is the best free option, particularly for acoustic and melodic material.

**Q: What's the difference between a limiter and a maximizer?**

A: Both prevent signal from exceeding a set ceiling, but "maximizer" implies an aggressive loudness-increase focus alongside the limiting. A plugin like FabFilter Pro-L 2 can operate transparently or act as a maximizer depending on which algorithm you select. Standalone maximizers — like the Waves L2 — are tuned specifically for loud output.

**Q: What LUFS should I master to in 2026?**

A: For Spotify and YouTube: target -14 LUFS integrated. For Apple Music: -16 LUFS. Club and electronic releases often push -7 to -9 LUFS. Always confirm with a metering plugin like Youlean Loudness Meter 2 — perceived loudness and integrated LUFS don't always align with what your eyes tell you on a limiter's gain reduction meter.

**Q: Do I actually need true peak limiting?**

A: Yes, for any streaming delivery. MP3 and AAC encoding can introduce inter-sample peaks even when your output reads -0.1 dBFS. Set your true peak ceiling to -1.0 dBTP as a general rule, or check each platform's specific recommendation. Most modern limiters on this list support true peak mode.

A: If you master frequently and release professionally, yes — the algorithm variety and output transparency justify the cost quickly.

**Q: Which mastering limiter plugin is best for beginners in 2026?**

A: W1 Limiter paired with Youlean Loudness Meter 2 is the best starting combination — both are free, both are simple enough to learn in an afternoon, and the results are genuinely usable on final masters, especially for acoustic and singer-songwriter material.

**Q: Can I use a mastering limiter on mix buses?**

A: Yes, and it's common practice on drum buses and lead vocal chains. The difference is that mix bus limiting typically benefits from faster attack times and a degree of coloration — whereas mastering chain limiting prioritizes transparency and true peak protection. If using a mastering limiter on a mix bus, engage faster release settings and monitor for pumping.

## How to choose

<!-- role:choose -->

For producers starting out with no budget to spend, pairing W1 Limiter with Youlean Loudness Meter 2 is the sensible starting point: both are free, both work reliably, and working with them teaches more about limiting and loudness than following a paid tutorial would. For those who want real multi-stage limiting capability without spending heavily, Limiter 6 GE offers control that stands up against far more expensive tools; the interface takes some time to learn, but the results justify the effort. When mastering acoustic, classical, or jazz material, AOM Invisible Limiter G2 or FabFilter Pro-L 2 set to its Transparent algorithm are the better choices, since transparency matters more than loudness on this kind of material, and tools built for aggressive loudness can cause audible damage instead. For louder genres such as EDM, hip-hop, pop, and club music, FabFilter Pro-L 2 set to Dynamic or Aggressive, or iZotope Ozone Maximizer using its IRC IV algorithm, handle complex low end and fast transients cleanly while reaching typical commercial loudness levels. When delivering to broadcast or other strict compliance standards, Sonnox Oxford Limiter V3 is built specifically to meet the inter-sample peak requirements and dithering precision that broadcast delivery demands. For dense masters with problematic low end, DMG Audio Limitless stands out as the option built specifically to solve per-band and mid-side limiting problems that a single-band brickwall limiter cannot address.

Q: What are the best limiter plugins for mastering in 2026?

Q: What is the difference between a limiter and a maximizer?
A: Both prevent a signal from exceeding a set ceiling, but a maximizer implies an aggressive loudness-increase focus alongside the limiting. Q: What LUFS level should mastering target in 2026?
