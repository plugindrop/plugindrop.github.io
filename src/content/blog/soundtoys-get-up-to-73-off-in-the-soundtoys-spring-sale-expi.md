## What Is Soundtoys?

Soundtoys is a Vermont-based plugin developer known for building effects processors with heavy analog character. Their catalog spans delay, saturation, pitch, modulation, and filtering — all built around the idea of adding real texture and movement to a mix. The company has been around since the late 1990s and their plugins are a regular fixture in professional studios. The flagship offering is **Soundtoys 5**, a bundle containing their full effects suite.

---

## Key Features

- **EchoBoy** — a delay plugin modeled after vintage tape and analog echo units, widely used for adding vibe and dimension to tracks
- **Decapitator** — an analog saturation plugin based on five classic hardware drive circuits, used to add grit and harmonic color
- **Little AlterBoy** — a vocal transform tool for pitch shifting and formant manipulation, capable of subtle doubling or extreme effect
- **PanMan** — a panning modulation plugin inspired by classic auto-panner hardware, useful for creating rhythmic stereo movement
- **PhaseMistress** — a phaser modeled on vintage stomp-box-style hardware, offering deep modulation control
- **Crystallizer** — a granular pitch-shifting echo based on the Eventide H3000 algorithm, designed for textural and ambient work
