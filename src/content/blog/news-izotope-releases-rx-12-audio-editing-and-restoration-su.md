## What Is iZotope RX 12?

iZotope RX is the go-to audio repair and restoration suite used across post-production, music, podcast, and broadcast workflows. RX 12 is the newest major version, building on an ecosystem of more than 50 dedicated tools for noise reduction, dialogue cleanup, source separation, and surgical audio editing. iZotope — now part of the Native Instruments family — has developed RX into one of the most recognized names in professional audio restoration. This release continues that trajectory with machine learning upgrades and workflow refinements throughout the platform.

---

## Key Features

- **Expanded Machine Learning Tools** — RX 12 adds new ML-driven processing modules, pushing automated repair capabilities further into tasks that previously required manual intervention.
- **Enhanced Source Separation** — Improved algorithms for isolating individual elements (dialogue, music, effects) from mixed audio, useful for restoration and remixing workflows.
- **50+ Dedicated Modules** — The suite spans the full repair workflow, from spectral repair and noise reduction to dereverberation and ambience matching.
- **Workflow Refinements** — Version 12 introduces interface and workflow updates designed to speed up repetitive tasks in demanding post-production pipelines.
- **Broad Format and DAW Support** — RX integrates as a standalone application and as plug-ins compatible with major DAWs; check the product page for the full compatibility list.

## Who Is It For?

- **Post-Production Professionals** — RX is widely used for dialogue editing and cleanup in film, TV, and streaming production, where clean audio is non-negotiable.
- **Podcast Producers and Broadcasters** — Anyone dealing with inconsistent recording environments benefits from RX's noise reduction and voice restoration tools.
- **Music Producers and Engineers** — Useful for restoring archived recordings, cleaning up samples, or salvaging otherwise unusable takes.
- **Game Audio Designers** — Source separation and repair tools are directly applicable to building clean, isolated audio assets for interactive media.
