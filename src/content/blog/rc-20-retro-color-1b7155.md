## What Is RC 20 Retro Color?

RC 20 Retro Color is a multi-effect plug-in from XLN Audio built to add aged, degraded, sampled, and analog-inspired character to audio. It places several effect types in one interface, allowing producers to combine texture, pitch instability, saturation, digital coloration, space, and magnetic-style effects.

It is commonly used in DTM workflows where clean virtual instruments, drums, vocals, or sampled material need more texture. Rather than functioning as a single saturation or noise plug-in, RC 20 organizes multiple character processes into adjustable modules.

## Key Features

- **Noise module:** Adds selectable noise and texture layers to help create vinyl, tape, room, or device-like backgrounds.

- **Wobble module:** Introduces pitch variation and instability for tape-style movement or detuned character.

- **Distort module:** Provides drive and degradation controls for adding harmonic coloration and roughness.

- **Digital module:** Adds digital artifacts intended for sampled, bit-reduced, or older digital-device flavors.

- **Space module:** Applies reverb-based ambience as part of the overall texture chain.

- **Magnetic module:** Adds magnetic-media-inspired processing associated with tape-like coloration and movement.
