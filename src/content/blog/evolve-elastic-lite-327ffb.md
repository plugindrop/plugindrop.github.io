## Four Layers, One Elastic-Inspired Instrument
<!-- role:product -->

Four sound sources—A, B, C, and D—form the core of Evolve Elastic Lite’s quad-engine. Each layer can use sample or Synth OSC engines, giving producers a direct way to combine contrasting material before moving between combinations with the XY Pad. The maker describes the instrument as a “bold sample-based synthesizer” that morphs sound into unexpected forms, with its concept inspired by the playful, artificial, and unpredictable character of elastic.

That plain-language idea translates to a layered sound-design workflow: choose sources, blend them, then change the balance or processing through the XY Pad. The supplied sound library includes 125 sound sources across Chords, FX, Impacts, Instruments, Multisample, Synth, and Textures. There are also 75 genre-spanning presets for starting points, alongside the option to create sounds from scratch.

The instrument’s controls support detailed synth building as well. The Synth OSC section offers sine, triangle, saw, square, and pulse shapes, plus pitch, fine tuning, unison, detune, 1–32 voices, legato, glide, pitch bend, and global pitch. Sample-oriented controls include forward/backward playback direction, keytracking, pitch, tone, pan, velocity sensitivity, and volume.

One-click Randomise Sounds is particularly relevant to the product’s stated aim of sparking ideas. Rather than requiring every layer to be manually programmed before anything happens, it provides a supported route to unexpected combinations inside the same four-layer framework. The supplied demo, **“The Sound of the Future | Evolve Elastic,”** is the named video reference for the product.

## Melt, Bounce, Stretch, and Gloss Define the Maker’s Direction
<!-- role:sound -->

Melt, Bounce, Stretch, and Gloss are the four elastic-inspired macro effects used to shape and polish the output. The maker’s description ties those controls to a futuristic synthesis concept: four layers can be blended with the XY Pad, then altered through the macro effects. Macro routing can be set pre- or post-FX, while the XY Pad offers separate Sounds and FX modes.

The maker characterises the underlying idea with the words “playful,” “artificial,” and “unpredictable,” all in reference to elastic. It also cites experimental electronic artists Flume, SOPHIE, and Aphex Twin as inspiration for the preset direction. Those are stated influences, rather than claims that the instrument reproduces any particular artist’s sound.

For producers, the practical distinction is that the central controls are designed around movement between layers and macros, not just a single oscillator path. The included Sounds Browser, four sound-source layers, and randomisation feature keep the focus on selecting, combining, and changing material. Eco Mode and tooltips are also included.
