## What Is Excite Audio?

Excite Audio is an audio plugin developer behind a growing catalog of effect plugins and virtual instruments. Their lineup spans creative mixing tools like the VISION 4X multiband visual analyzer, the Lifeline series of mixing processors (compressor, EQ, spatial effects, and saturation), the Motion series of harmonic effects, and the Bloom family of sample-based virtual instruments. All products are available in VST, VST3, AU, and AAX formats, with Apple Silicon (M1/M2) native support added in recent updates.

## Key Features Across the Excite Audio Catalog

- **VISION 4X** — A real-time visual analysis suite combining a spectrogram, bar graph, waveform analyzer, and phase correlation meter in one window. Includes genre-based reference curves for mix comparison.
- **Lifeline Series** — Four dedicated mixing modules: Lifeline Comp, Lifeline EQ, Lifeline Wear (saturation/character), and Lifeline Space (spatial effects) for streamlined mix processing.
- **Motion: Harmonic** — A creative distortion effect with features like Speed Points control and Clean Mode for parallel processing flexibility.
- **Bloom Instruments** — Sample-based virtual instruments covering orchestral textures, ensemble strings, and more. All Bloom instruments support custom sample importing.
- **Royalty-Free Content** — Every Bloom instrument ships with royalty-free sounds, ready for commercial productions out of the box.
- **3-Device Activation** — All Excite Audio plugins can be activated on up to three devices without requiring iLok.
