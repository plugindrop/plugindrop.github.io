## What Is iLoud Sub?

iLoud Sub is a powered studio subwoofer from IK Multimedia, engineered to extend the low-end response of compact near-field monitor setups. It sits within the iLoud product family — the same line that includes the widely-used iLoud Micro Monitor and iLoud MTM — giving producers a matched bass extension option rather than a generic third-party subwoofer. The result is a tightly integrated monitoring chain designed for home studios and desktop setups where physical space is a constraint.

---

## Key Features

- **DSP-Controlled Amplification:** Onboard digital signal processing manages driver behavior for controlled, accurate bass output rather than bloated low-end.
- **Compact Footprint:** Built to sit under or alongside a desktop monitoring setup without overwhelming the workspace.
- **Integrated iLoud Ecosystem Compatibility:** Designed to pair tonally with the iLoud Micro Monitor and iLoud MTM, maintaining spectral consistency across the full range.
- **Stereo Pass-Through Connectivity:** Routes signal from your audio interface through the sub and out to your satellite monitors, simplifying your signal chain.
- **Boundary EQ Controls:** Onboard switches adjust bass response based on room placement — corner, wall, or free-standing — to reduce low-end buildup from acoustic boundaries.
