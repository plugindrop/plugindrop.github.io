## What you actually get

Complete Drums 2 is a sample pack bundle built around drum and percussion recordings, packaged as 16 individual packs according to the product listing. The collection reportedly includes around 34,000 drum and percussive hits in total.

A portion of that content is tape-processed material, with roughly 15,000 samples run through analog tape machines, specifically a Studer A80 MK1 and an Ampex ATR-102 according to the source listing. There's also a smaller set of vinyl-processed samples, put at over 1,700, recorded through a Neumann VMS 70 vinyl mastering lathe.

That combination of dry hits, tape-saturated versions, and vinyl-run versions means the pack functions less like a single drum kit and more like a layered sample source. Producers can pull a clean hit for transient definition and blend in a tape or vinyl-processed version of the same hit for character, without needing separate outboard processing.

## Where it fits in a session

This kind of library sits earliest in the signal chain, at the sample-selection and drum-programming stage rather than the mixing or mastering stage. It's built for producers assembling kits inside a sampler or drum machine plugin, whether for hip-hop, lo-fi, electronic, or hybrid production styles that lean on textured drum sounds.

The tape and vinyl-processed variants are useful when a track needs vintage character without routing individual hits through real tape or a lathe. Because the packs separate clean and processed versions, they also work well for layering: a modern, transient-forward kick or snare stacked against a warmer, saturated duplicate.
