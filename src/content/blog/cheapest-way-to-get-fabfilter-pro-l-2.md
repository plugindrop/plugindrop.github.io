## What Pro-L 2 is, based on its published specs

FabFilter describes Pro-L 2 as a mastering and mixing limiter. Its published feature set is the main reason to consider it over a stock limiter:

- **True peak limiting:** This helps prevent inter-sample peaks that can cause clipping after lossy encoding or digital-to-analog conversion.
- **Multiple limiting algorithms:** FabFilter ships eight distinct styles, ranging from transparent to more aggressive settings, so one plugin can handle different genres and material.
- **Oversampling:** Higher oversampling settings can reduce aliasing at the cost of more CPU.
- **Loudness metering:** Built-in metering lets you check levels against streaming-platform loudness targets without a separate meter plugin.
- **Surround support:** The plugin can be used in multichannel formats, which matters for film or immersive-audio work.

Consensus across producer forums and plugin reviews is that Pro-L 2 is a widely used reference limiter in mastering chains. That reputation is part of why it rarely drops far below its sale price.

## Practical examples

### Example 1: finishing a stereo mix

You have a finished mix and need a competitive level for streaming. Place Pro-L 2 last in the master chain, set the output ceiling, and raise gain until the limiter engages. Use the loudness meter to compare against the target for your platform. This is a simple use case and is the one most people buy the plugin for.

### Example 2: bus limiting on drums

A drum bus with unruly peaks can be tamed with a fast, punchy algorithm setting. The same plugin that masters your track can also act as a peak catcher on individual buses, so one purchase covers both jobs.

### Example 3: client deliverables

When you hand off masters to a label or distributor, true peak compliance matters. A limiter with a true peak mode lets you set a ceiling that keeps files within common delivery specs.
