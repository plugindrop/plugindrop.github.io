## What Is Zynaptiq Morph 3?

Morph 3 is a sound design plugin from Zynaptiq, a company known for spectral audio processing tools like PitchMap and Unmix. It performs real-time cross-synthesis, blending the spectral characteristics of two audio signals into a single hybrid sound. Producers use it to create textures and tones that don't exist in either source signal on its own.

## Key Features

- **Spectral morphing engine** — blends two input signals in real time, combining their tonal and timbral characteristics into new hybrid sounds.
- **Dual signal input** — routes two separate audio sources through the plugin so their spectral content can be cross-synthesized.
- **Formant control** — helps preserve vocal-like or recognizable character in a sound as it morphs between sources.
- **Real-time processing** — designed to work within a live signal chain rather than requiring offline rendering.
