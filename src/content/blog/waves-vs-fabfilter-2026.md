## Waves API 2500

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Waves API 2500 -->

Waves API 2500 brings the sound of the famed API hardware bus compressor into a digital plugin, built in close collaboration with API to capture the character of the original unit. It offers a choice between vintage feedback and modern feedforward compression designs, letting producers pick the circuit behavior that suits the material. A proprietary Thrust control shapes low end for a gut-punching bass response, while New/Old and Soft/Hard knee switching give additional flexibility over how the compression behaves. A Mix control is included for easy parallel compression, blending compressed and dry signal without extra routing.

This compressor is well suited to gluing mix elements together, whether on drum buses, instrument groups, or an entire stereo mix. It has become a favorite tool for adding a punchy character to drums and grooves, and producers across hip hop, pop/R&B, dance, and rock use it for that reason. It works on Mac systems running Windows 10 or later, and is available in VST, AU, and AAX plugin formats.

## Waves H-Delay

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Waves H-Delay -->

Waves H-Delay blends digital-precision timing with an analog-flavored character, giving it a rich, smooth sound while still offering lo-fi options for grittier textures. Delay times run from about 1 millisecond up to 3500 milliseconds, and the delay can be synced to tempo for rhythmic repeats that lock in with a track. Users can pick between four different analog-style characters or a cleaner, contemporary digital sound, depending on the tone they're after.

Built-in filtering lets you shape the delay's tone further, carving out highs or lows to fit the mix. A modulation section, also syncable to tempo, adds movement such as chorusing or vibrato to the repeats. Every control supports MIDI Learn, making it easy to map knobs for hands-on tweaking or live performance effects.

With its approachable interface and a wide range of presets, H-Delay is built to get a creative delay sound going quickly, which is part of why it has earned a reputation as a go-to, everyday delay plugin. It fits naturally into rhythmic delay sends and vocal slap-back duties, and generally any situation where straightforward digital timing is paired with a touch of vintage warmth.

H-Delay is available for Mac in VST, AU, and AAX formats, and runs on Windows 10 and later.

## Waves Kramer Master Tape

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Waves Kramer Master Tape -->

Waves Kramer Master Tape, developed with studio legend Eddie Kramer, is a tape saturation emulation plugin. Kramer engineered classic recordings for Led Zeppelin and Jimi Hendrix, and that pedigree carries directly into this plugin's design.

The emulation models the American tube recorder originally used at Olympic Studios, which captured much of the sound that defined early rock and roll. Users can shape the character of the effect with dedicated controls for speed, bias, and flux, letting them dial in anything from a subtle vintage sheen to a heavily colored, saturated tone.

Beyond the core tape modeling, the plugin includes a built-in tape delay with up to 500 milliseconds of delay time, along with time, feedback, and filter controls for shaping the repeats. For added realism, users can layer in wow, flutter, and hiss, recreating the imperfections of physical tape machines.

At the heart of the processing is a tube saturation stage, which is especially useful for warming up instruments or mixes that sound harsh or overly digital. Together, these elements make the plugin well suited for adding analog-style tape compression and harmonic coloring to full mixes, drum buses, or individual tracks where digital hardness is the problem to solve.

## FabFilter Pro-Q 4

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Pro-Q 4 -->

FabFilter Pro-Q 4 is an equalizer plugin that offers up to 24 bands of processing, with Spectral Dynamics as its standout addition for handling problem frequencies with precision.

It's built for mixing and mastering engineers, whether working in stereo, surround, or Dolby Atmos sessions. The 3 dB and 6 dB display range options are suited to mastering work, while the 12 dB and 30 dB ranges are better suited to mixing tasks.

On the sound side, Spectral Dynamics targets problem frequencies more precisely and efficiently than traditional EQ approaches. Dynamic EQ is also included, with Attack and Release controls and optional free side-chain filtering. The plugin's dynamic EQ processing has been refined to produce less distortion and a cleaner overall sound, and this holds true across its zero latency, Natural Phase, and linear phase modes.

## FabFilter Pro-L 2

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Pro-L 2 -->

FabFilter Pro-L 2 is a true peak limiter built for mastering and mixing engineers, suited to mono, stereo, or surround work. External side-chain triggering also supports stem mastering.

It offers eight distinct limiting algorithms, each with its own character and purpose, so engineers can choose the behavior that fits the material. The plugin delivers transparent sound while still achieving maximum loudness, and true peak limiting is combined with up to 32x linear-phase oversampling to keep peaks under control. Output and gain reduction metering, including true peak metering, is highly accurate, and an optional DC offset filter is included as well. Adjustable look-ahead, attack, and release settings let users shape how the limiting behaves.

A true peak limiter like this is considered an essential tool for virtually every mastering or mixing engineer, making it well suited for final limiting and loudness normalization ahead of streaming, broadcast, or release masters.

## FabFilter Saturn 2

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Saturn 2 -->

FabFilter Saturn 2 is a multiband distortion and saturation plugin with a large library of distortion styles, ranging from clean tube and tape warmth to full guitar amp drive and bit crushing. Processing can be split across as many as six frequency bands, with crossover slopes adjustable between 6, 12, 24, and 48 dB per octave, and an optional linear phase mode for cleaner mastering use. Each band carries its own controls for drive, mix, feedback, dynamics, tone, and output level, giving independent shaping across the frequency spectrum.

The interface centers on an interactive multiband display paired with live modulation visualization, so modulation sources and targets can be seen moving in real time. For sound quality, two optional high-quality oversampling modes are available: a "Good" setting at 8x and a "Superb" setting at 32x.

It suits a wide range of uses, from subtle tube or tape-style warmth on individual tracks to aggressive multiband guitar amp effects, and the redesigned interface with linear phase processing makes it workable for mastering contexts where phase coherence matters.

## FabFilter Total Bundle

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Total Bundle -->

FabFilter Total Bundle brings together all fourteen FabFilter plug-ins into one package, built as the complete pro and creative suite from the developer. It covers the full signal chain — EQ, compression, limiting, saturation, multiband dynamics, de-essing, reverb, gating and expansion, delay, and synthesis — so a studio can standardize on a single set of tools rather than piecing together plugins from different makers.

The bundle has picked up several new and improved features recently. A new ducking control makes it easier to separate dry and wet signals cleanly, while a new thickness control lets users dial in program-dependent density and saturation. An intelligent auto-gate feature has been added, along with new output level meters for monitoring signal levels. On the improvement side, the decay rate range has been widened to between 25 and 400 percent, giving more flexibility in time-based processing, and a mid/side option has been added to the post-EQ stage for more targeted stereo processing.

On the technical side, the bundle uses high-quality audio processing algorithms with 64-bit internal processing applied where needed. It's available in VST, VST3, Audio Units, and AAX Native formats, each offered in both 64-bit and 32-bit variants, and built natively for both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. On Mac, it requires macOS 10.13 or higher and runs on either Intel processors or Apple Silicon (M1) chips, in 64-bit only. On Windows, it's compatible with Windows Vista, 7, 8, 10, and 11, available in both 32-bit and 64-bit builds. Format-wise, the Mac version offers VST2, VST3, AU, and AAX Native, while the Windows version offers VST2, VST3, and AAX Native.

For anyone comparing suites, the bundle sits alongside other large all-in-one packages like Arturia V Collection 11 Pro, the Soundtoys 5.5 collection, and iZotope's Music Production Suite as a comprehensive alternative to buying plugins individually.
