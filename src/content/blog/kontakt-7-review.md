## Kontakt 7 or Kontakt 8?

If you searched for Kontakt 7, note that Native Instruments now sells Kontakt 8 as the current version. The Kontakt 8 page offers a discounted Update version for owners of an earlier Kontakt. That update is priced at checkout and its exact amount depends on your account, so we do not quote a number here.

## Kontakt 8 at a Glance

- **Developer:** Native Instruments
- **Price:** $299 (full version, as listed on the vendor's store)
- **Platforms:** macOS 14, 15 and 26; Windows 10 or 11. As of Kontakt 8.8, macOS 13 is no longer supported.
- **Formats:** Standalone, VST3, AU and AAX on macOS; Standalone, VST3 and AAX on Windows
- **CPU:** Intel Core i5 or equivalent, or Apple Silicon
- **Disk space:** 52 GB for the complete installation

## What Is New in Kontakt 8

Native Instruments describes these as the headline additions:

- **Tools.** A new layer of creative features that work with any Kontakt instrument, old and new, such as Humanizer (randomization), Scale Lock and Chord Builder.
- **Leap.** A loop-playback and manipulation engine, launching with a new Leap Expansions series. Twelve curated genre packs (for example 80s New Wave, Acoustic Drums, Afrobeats and Drum Breaks) come with the full version.
- **Conflux.** A sound-design instrument that layers organic and synthetic samples, using original PPG wavetables and FM, phase modulation and ring modulation. It ships with the full Kontakt 8, or can be bought separately for use inside the free Player.
- **Synth capabilities for instrument builders.** Real-time wavetable, frequency modulation and ring modulation oscillators.
- **Chords and Phrases.** Included in both the Player and the full version, along with combined presets in the Factory Library.

## Included Sounds

The vendor lists the full version as shipping with:

- The Kontakt Factory Library, over 900 instruments. Collections named on the page include Orchestral (taken from Orchestral Tools' Berlin Series), Acoustic (60 instruments), Band, Beats (288 grooves across 90 kits), Choir, Synths and Vintage (292 deep-sampled electronic instruments).
- Conflux.
- Twelve Leap Expansions.
- Hybrid Keys from the Play Series.

## How to Choose

- **Only using Native Instruments instruments or Player-compatible third-party libraries:** the free Kontakt 8 Player covers that. Check the library's own product page for Player compatibility first.
- **Own or plan to buy libraries that are not Player-compatible:** the Player does not play them, so you need the full version.
- **Building your own instruments or editing existing ones:** editing and the sample editor are limited to 15 minutes in demo mode in the Player, so the full version applies.
- **Already own an older Kontakt:** look at the Update price at checkout before paying the full $299.
- **Need more than a sampler:** Kontakt 8 is included in Komplete 26 from the Standard tier up. Compare that bundle's price against buying Kontakt 8 alone.

## FAQ
**Q: How much is Kontakt 8?**
A: $299 for the full version on the Native Instruments store at the time of writing. Owners of a previous version can choose the Update version at checkout at a special price.

**Q: Is there a free version of Kontakt 8?**
A: Yes. Kontakt 8 Player is free and is included in Komplete Start. It runs Native Instruments' Kontakt instruments and officially licensed third-party Player libraries.

**Q: What is the difference between Kontakt 8 and Kontakt 8 Player?**
A: Per the vendor's chart, the full version also plays non-Player libraries, includes Conflux, twelve Leap Expansions and the Factory Library, and has unlimited instrument editing and the integrated sample editor. The Player limits editing and the sample editor to 15 minutes in demo mode.

**Q: Does Kontakt 8 run on Apple Silicon?**
A: The system requirements list "Intel Core i5 or equivalent CPU, or Apple Silicon" and macOS 14, 15 and 26.

**Q: Does my Kontakt 7 still work?**
A: We do not have vendor information on how long Kontakt 7 remains supported. Check Native Instruments' support pages for your version.
