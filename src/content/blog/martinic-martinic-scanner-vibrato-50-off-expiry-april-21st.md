## What Is Martinic Scanner Vibrato?

Martinic Scanner Vibrato is a plugin that faithfully models the iconic scanner vibrato and chorus circuit found in vintage Hammond tonewheel organs. Martinic — a boutique Swedish developer known for meticulous hardware emulation — built this effect around the mechanical rotary scanner that gave classic Hammond organs their distinctive wavering, warm modulation. The result is a dedicated vibrato/chorus effect plugin, separate from a full organ emulation, designed to bring that same circuit to any signal in your DAW.

---

## Key Features

- **Scanner Vibrato & Chorus Modes** — Recreates both the pure vibrato (pitch-only modulation) and the chorus modes (mixed direct+modulated signal) from the original Hammond circuit, giving you multiple character options.
- **Six Preset Modes** — Modeled after the V1/V2/V3 and C1/C2/C3 settings of the original Hammond scanner, each with progressively deeper modulation depth.
- **Analog Circuit Modeling** — The modulation is produced by a simulated capacitor/inductor ladder network, replicating the non-linear behavior of the hardware rather than a simple LFO.
- **Stereo Output** — Outputs a spread stereo image, making it useful for widening mono sources in a mix.
- **Low CPU Overhead** — Check the product page for exact specs, but boutique circuit-modeled plugins of this type are typically light on resources.
- **VST/AU/AAX Support** — Compatible with major DAWs on both Mac and Windows; verify exact formats and OS compatibility on the product page.

## Who Is It For?

- **Keyboard and organ players** — The primary audience: anyone layering Hammond-style sounds or Rhodes/Wurlitzer patches who wants authentic scanner modulation rather than a generic chorus.
- **Lo-fi and vintage producers** — The warm, slightly unstable modulation character works naturally on guitars, bass, and synths in lo-fi, soul, and funk contexts.
- **Mix engineers** — A subtle scanner chorus can add gentle width and movement to a static pad or lead without the obvious sweep of a phaser or flanger.
- **Sound designers** — Routing unexpected sources (vocals, drums) through scanner vibrato produces textured, retro-flavored results that standard modulation effects don't replicate.
