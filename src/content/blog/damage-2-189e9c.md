## What Is Damage 2?

Damage 2 is a cinematic hybrid percussion instrument developed by Heavyocity, built for Native Instruments Kontakt. It's the follow-up to the original Damage — a library that became a go-to for film, TV, and trailer composers needing massive, processed percussion sounds. Damage 2 expands the palette with new content, deeper sound design capabilities, and updated performance tools designed for modern media scoring workflows.

## Key Features

- **Hybrid Cinematic Percussion** — Blends acoustic drums, metallic impacts, and processed rhythmic elements into a unified performance engine.
- **Damage Engine** — A built-in multi-effects processor with modulation and chaos-style randomization tools for evolving, unpredictable textures.
- **Loop Designer** — Allows rhythmic loop construction directly within the instrument, useful for building layered, sync-to-tempo beds.
- **Kontakt Integration** — Runs inside Native Instruments Kontakt (full version required; check the product page for minimum version compatibility).
- **Expanded Library Content** — Includes a significantly larger pool of samples and patches compared to the original Damage.
- **Performance Macros** — Assignable controls for real-time manipulation of tone, effects depth, and dynamics during playback.
