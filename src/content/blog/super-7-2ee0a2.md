## What Is Super 7?

Super 7 is a virtual instrument by UVI inspired by the Roland MKS-7, an eight-voice rackmount instrument associated with Juno-style analog architecture and TR-707 PCM sounds. It is designed to provide synth lines, basses, drums, arpeggios, phrases, and layered parts from one interface.

The instrument runs in UVI Workstation or Falcon. It includes a multi-instrument layout alongside standalone versions of its individual parts, so a project can use either the full arrangement-style setup or a focused sound source.

## Key Features

- **Six discrete layers:** Super 7 provides independent drum, bass, melody, and three general-purpose synth parts for layered arrangements.

- **Eight drum tracks:** The Rhythm section supplies separate tracks for programming drum patterns within the instrument.

- **262 factory presets:** The library includes synth, drum, arpeggiator, and phrase material for starting points and editing.

- **Multi-sampled source material:** UVI sampled MKS-7-derived patches in normal, chorus, and noise-switch variations.

- **Per-part sound shaping:** Non-drum layers include amp envelopes, multimode filtering, drive, phasor, EQ, and reverb/delay sends.

- **Arpeggiator and phraser:** Each applicable part includes sequencing tools for creating rhythmic patterns and moving phrases.
