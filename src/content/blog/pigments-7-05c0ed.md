## What Is Pigments 7?

Pigments is Arturia's flagship software synthesizer, combining several synthesis engines in one instrument. It's designed for sound designers and producers who want to layer virtual analog, wavetable, and sample-based sounds without switching between multiple plugins. Arturia has continued to update the Pigments line with new engines, modulation options, and effects across major versions. Check the product page for the full list of engines and features included in this release.

## Key Features

- **Multi-engine synthesis** — Pigments blends different sound-generation methods (analog-style oscillators, wavetables, and sample playback) inside a single patch.
- **Modulation matrix** — A visual, drag-and-drop modulation system lets you route envelopes, LFOs, and other modulators to nearly any parameter.
- **Built-in effects rack** — Includes onboard effects for shaping tone directly inside the synth, reducing the need for external processing.
- **Preset browser** — A searchable library of factory presets organized by category and sound design tags, useful for quickly auditioning starting points.
- **Arpeggiator and sequencer** — Built-in tools for generating rhythmic and melodic patterns without leaving the plugin.
- Exact engine count, CPU requirements, and version-specific additions should be confirmed on the official product page before purchase.
