- **SATB Coverage** — The choir products cover soprano, alto, tenor, and bass sections, enabling full harmonic choral writing directly within your DAW.
- **Solo Voice Options** — Individual soprano, mezzo, tenor, and bass/baritone solo instruments are available separately for intimate or exposed melodic lines.
