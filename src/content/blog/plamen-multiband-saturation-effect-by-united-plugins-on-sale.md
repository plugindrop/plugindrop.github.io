## What Is Plamen Multiband Saturation?

Plamen is a multiband saturation effect developed by SoundEvice Digital and published under the United Plugins umbrella. The name means "flame" in several Slavic languages — fitting for a plugin designed to add heat, drive, and harmonic character to your audio. Rather than applying saturation globally, Plamen lets you target specific frequency bands independently, giving producers and mixing engineers surgical control over where the grit lands.

---

## Key Features

- **Multiband saturation engine** — Apply saturation to individual frequency bands without smearing the entire signal.
- **Multiple saturation modes** — Different harmonic distortion characters to suit sources from vocals to drums to bass.
- **Band-specific drive and output controls** — Shape the intensity and level per band for precise tonal sculpting.
- **Blend/mix control** — Dial in parallel processing to preserve transient punch while adding harmonic richness.
- **Visual frequency display** — See what's happening across the spectrum as you work.
- **United Plugins ecosystem** — Lightweight, stable integration consistent with other United Plugins titles.
