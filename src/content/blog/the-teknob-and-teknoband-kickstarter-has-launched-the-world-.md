## What Is the TEKNOB (and TEKNOBAND)?

The TEKNOB is billed as the world's first touchscreen electronic music groovebox knob — a physical rotary controller with an integrated touchscreen display, designed for live performance and studio production. The TEKNOBAND is a companion wearable version. Both are developed by AllOneSun and launched on Kickstarter in April 2026.

The concept bridges the tactile feedback of a hardware knob with the visual and touch interactivity of a screen, targeting producers who want hands-on control without sacrificing screen-based workflow.
