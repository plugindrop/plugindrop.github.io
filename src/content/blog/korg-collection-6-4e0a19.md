## Inside the KORG Collection 6 Synthesizer Museum
<!-- role:product -->

KORG Collection 6 is a suite of software synthesizers built to methodically and accurately recreate the authentic sound and behavior of legendary KORG hardware. The line began with analog instruments — the MS-20, Polysix, and Mono/Poly — before expanding into digital synths such as the microKORG, the M1 and TRITON workstations, and the physical-modeling Prophecy.

It also includes the ARP ODYSSEY and ARP 2600, both originally built by ARP and later revived by KORG for modern systems. Digital models ship loaded with their original expansion content: the M1 carries its 34 original expansion cards, the WAVESTATION its 7, the TRITON its 8 expansion boards, and the TRINITY four expansion libraries plus the sounds from the rack-mount TR-Rack — content that was previously sold separately.

KORG describes the full set as standing as "a true synthesizer museum across generations," spanning instruments first released between 1971 and 2004. Plugins run as AU, VST, VST3, or AAX, 64-bit only, on Apple Silicon or Intel Core i5-class Macs and Windows 10 PCs with at least 8GB RAM and 16GB of free disk space.

## The Fat, Viscous Character Modeled Into the MS-20
<!-- role:sound -->

The MS-20 model is built around what KORG's own material calls a "fat, viscous sound," paired with flexible patching that carries over the original's semi-modular routing. Across the analog models — the MS-20, ARP 2600, and PS-3300 — KORG applies its Component Modeling Technology to reproduce the organic and unexpected behavior specific to each original circuit, rather than a single generic analog character. That modeling approach is the throughline for the collection: authentic recreation of behavior, not just waveform, is the stated goal for both the vintage analog instruments and the digital workstations layered in alongside them.

## Who the Piano, Organ, and Synth Models Serve
<!-- role:audience -->

- **Workstation keyboardists** — The SGX-2 piano engine from the KRONOS and the VOX Super Continental combo organ extend the collection into piano and organ parts alongside the synths.
- **Analog patch programmers** — The MS-20, Polysix, and Mono/Poly give access to patching-based analog synth sounds, with the microKORG covering analog-modeling duties.
- **Workstation-era producers** — The M1 and TRITON models carry forward the sample-and-synthesis workflow of their original 1988 and 1999 hardware.
