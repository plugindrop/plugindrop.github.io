### Decapitator — The bundle's most-reached-for plugin

- **Developer:** Soundtoys
- **Price:** Included in bundle ($199 standalone)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS

Decapitator models five different analog saturation circuits — from transformer-style warmth to full-on amp overdrive — with a Punish button that pushes any of them into aggressive, asymmetric clipping territory. The Drive, Tone, and Mix controls give enough precision to be useful on a mix bus without overwhelming the source, while the circuit styles span the full range from subtle harmonic glue to deliberate distortion.

Its real advantage over more complex competitors is speed: the five circuit modes cover 95% of saturation use cases without requiring deep menu-diving. It sounds right fast, which is why engineers reach for it first.

**Best for:** Adding analog harmonic character to drums, bass, synths, and vocals.

### EchoBoy — The most versatile delay plugin available

<div class="video-embed"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/CMxM4YDpWM8" title="EchoBoy — sound demo" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

- **Developer:** Soundtoys
- **Price:** Included in bundle ($199 standalone)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS
- **Formats:** VST3, AU, AAX

EchoBoy models 30 different hardware echo and delay units — from classic tape echoes and oil-can delays to digital bucket-brigade circuits — and the depth of emulation is genuine. Each style changes not just the tonal color of the repeats but how timing drifts, high frequencies degrade, and saturation accumulates at long feedback settings.

Four output modes cover everything from standard single echoes to ping-pong delays, but the Rhythm Echo mode is the standout feature: program custom rhythmic repeat patterns, synced to tempo, that go well beyond what any standard delay plugin offers. For producers who have only used their DAW's built-in delay, this alone is revelatory.

**Best for:** Any delay application, especially when clean digital repeats feel too sterile.

## Modulation & Pitch

### Little AlterBoy — The vocal transformation tool producers argue about

<div class="video-embed"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/dP5y0Jiuqb0" title="Little AlterBoy — sound demo" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

- **Developer:** Soundtoys
- **Price:** Included in bundle ($99 standalone)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS
- **Formats:** VST3, AU, AAX

Little AlterBoy handles pitch shifting and formant manipulation in a way that produces musically usable artifacts rather than transparent pitch correction. Drop the formant while pitching a voice up and you get an eerie, inhuman quality; push the pitch down while fixing the formant and baritone vocals develop an otherworldly depth. The Drive control adds harmonic saturation to the shifted output, setting it apart from clinical tools by making the processed sound sit naturally in a mix.

These artifacts are the point — producers use them intentionally rather than working around them. The plugin became a standard tool in modern pop, hip-hop, and R&B precisely because it creates a specific sonic identity no other pitch tool replicates.

**Best for:** Creative vocal processing, gender-bending vocal effects, and experimental pitch design.

## How to Choose

- **If you produce hip-hop, trap, or lo-fi** and need immediate character on drums and 808s, start with Decapitator standalone — it solves the "digital mix sounds too clean" problem faster than any other single plugin.
- **If you produce ambient, film score, or experimental music**, the full bundle is the correct call — Crystallizer, Little AlterBoy, and EchoBoy together cover sound design territory that free plugins simply cannot reach.
- **If you are primarily mixing**, EchoBoy + Decapitator + MicroShift standalone covers 80% of mix engineer use cases — or buy the bundle on sale and stop making per-plugin decisions entirely.
- **If budget is a concern**, wait. Soundtoys runs reliable 40–50% off sales multiple times per year. At $299 the full bundle is among the strongest per-dollar values in professional plugin software.
- **If you are comparing to competitor bundles** like iZotope's Music Production Suite: Soundtoys wins on creative character and analog feel; iZotope wins on technical repair tools and metering. They cover different ground and many professionals own both.
