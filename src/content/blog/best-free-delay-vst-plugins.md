**Do free delay VSTs work in Pro Tools?**
Most free delays ship in VST3 and AU formats, covering virtually every major DAW except Pro Tools, which requires AAX. Valhalla's free plugins — SpaceModulator and Freq Echo — both support AAX, making them the strongest free options for Pro Tools users. TAL-Dub-3 and Delay Lama are VST/AU only; verify format support before downloading for any AAX-only workflow.
