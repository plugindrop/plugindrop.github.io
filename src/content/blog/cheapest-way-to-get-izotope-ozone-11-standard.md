## What Ozone 11 Standard actually covers

For context on why this plugin routinely lands on "best mastering plugin" shortlists: Ozone is built as a full mastering chain in one plugin rather than a single processor. The Standard tier typically covers the core mastering workflow — EQ, dynamics/compression, an imager for stereo width, and a limiter for final loudness — along with iZotope's assisted-mastering tools that analyze a mix and suggest a starting point. That combination is why producers reach for it instead of assembling a mastering chain from several separate plugins: it consolidates the tools most mastering engineers use into one interface with a shared signal flow.

If your mastering needs are narrower — say, you only need a limiter, or you only need spectral matching — a single-purpose plugin might be cheaper than Ozone at any price point. Ozone earns its price when you want the whole chain in one place rather than piecing it together.
