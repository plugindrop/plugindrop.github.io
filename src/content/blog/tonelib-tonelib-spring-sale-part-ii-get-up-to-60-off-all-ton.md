## What Is ToneLib?

ToneLib is a software developer focused on guitar players and producers who work with guitar tones. Their catalog spans amp simulation plugins, multi-effects processors, and dedicated software editors for popular hardware units like Zoom guitar pedals. The company caters to both studio-based producers and live players looking to manage and design tones from their desktop.

## Key Features

Depending on which ToneLib product you're looking at, here's what their core software offers:

- **Amp & Cabinet Simulation** — Recreates the behavior of guitar amplifiers and speaker cabinets in a plugin format
- **Multi-Effects Processing** — Chains of modulation, delay, reverb, and drive effects in a single environment
- **Zoom Device Editor** — Dedicated patch editor and librarian for Zoom hardware guitar processors, enabling patch management from your DAW or desktop
