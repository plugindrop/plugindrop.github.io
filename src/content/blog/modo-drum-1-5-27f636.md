## What Is MODO DRUM?

MODO DRUM is a virtual acoustic drum instrument from IK Multimedia built on physical modeling rather than sampled audio. Instead of triggering pre-recorded hits, it calculates drum sound in real time based on parameters like shell material, head tension, and mic placement. That approach is meant to let producers reshape a kit's character without needing multiple sample libraries for different tunings or drum sizes.

## Key Features

- **Physical modeling engine** — drum sounds are synthesized, not sampled, so there's no fixed round-robin or velocity-layer library behind each hit.
- **Customizable kit building** — shells, heads, and hardware can be swapped and adjusted piece by piece to shape tone and character.
- **Mic and room modeling** — virtual mic placement and room ambience controls let you dial in the recording environment inside the plugin.
- **Groove and pattern content** — includes MIDI grooves for quickly auditioning kits in a musical context.
- **Plugin and standalone use** — runs as a VST/AU/AAX instrument in your DAW or on its own. Check the product page for exact format and host compatibility.
