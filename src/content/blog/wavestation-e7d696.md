## What Is WAVESTATION?

WAVESTATION is Korg's official software recreation of the original WAVESTATION hardware synthesizer, first released in 1990. The hardware became iconic for its unique approach to sound design — wave sequencing and vector synthesis — and shaped the sound of ambient, new age, and electronic music throughout the early 90s. The software version brings that same engine to modern DAWs, preserving the original presets and synthesis architecture in plugin form.

---

## Key Features

- **Wave Sequencing** — A defining feature: individual waveforms are chained in rhythmic sequences, creating evolving, morphing textures that static synthesis can't replicate.
- **Vector Synthesis** — Four sound sources are mixed dynamically using a joystick-style control, allowing smooth, animated transitions between timbres over time.
- **Original Preset Library** — Includes the classic factory patches from the hardware unit, covering pads, sequences, and atmospheric textures.
- **Multiple WAVESTATION Variants** — Recreates the original WAVESTATION, WAVESTATION A/D, and WAVESTATION SR models, each with their own sonic character.
- **DAW Integration** — Runs as a VST/AU plugin, bringing the vintage synthesis engine into any modern production workflow without external hardware.
