## What Is Mix Bundle One?

Mix Bundle One is a plugin bundle from **Slate Digital** that packages seven analog-modeled mixing tools into a single, ready-to-use signal chain. It's built around the FG series — four core modules designed to replicate the character of classic British and American hardware — plus three utility and enhancement plugins. The bundle targets producers and engineers who want a complete mix chain without piecing individual plugins together over time.

---

## Key Features

- **FG-401 Compressor** — Variable attack/release compressor with a British Class A transformer stage and an optional opto path for different compression characters
- **FG-S EQ** — Classic British console EQ inspired by 80s desk sound, with harmonic saturation built into the circuit model
- **FG-N EQ** — Class A British EQ with a 70s tonal character and an added mid band for more surgical control
- **FG-116 FET Limiter** — American-style FET limiting with fast, punchy timing and transformer-style saturation
- **Trimmer** — Precision gain staging utility for level-matching signals before and after processing
- **Monster + Revival** — Dynamics enhancer (punch/transient weight) and a sonic enhancer (high/low frequency air and warmth) to add finish without heavy processing
