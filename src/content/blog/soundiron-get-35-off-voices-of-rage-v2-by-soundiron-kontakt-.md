## What Is Voices of Rage v2?

Voices of Rage v2 is a Kontakt instrument library from Soundiron, a San Francisco-based developer known for deeply sampled, unconventional vocal and instrument libraries. This library captures raw, aggressive human vocal performances — screams, growls, shouts, and tension-filled extended techniques — and organizes them into a playable, expressive instrument. Version 2 represents an expanded and refined iteration of the original library, built for composers and producers who need genuine emotional intensity in their sound design or scoring work.

## Key Features

- **Aggressive Vocal Multisamples** — Captures a wide range of intense vocal performances including screams, yells, and guttural tones across multiple dynamic layers.
- **Full Kontakt Scripting** — Built with a custom Kontakt interface offering articulation switching, envelope controls, and performance shaping tools.
- **Multiple Mic Positions / Round Robins** — Designed to minimize machine-gun repetition, keeping repeated hits sounding natural in context.
- **Built-in FX Rack** — Soundiron libraries typically include an onboard effects chain (reverb, chorus, EQ, etc.) for shaping the sound directly within Kontakt.
- **Playable Pitch Range** — Vocals are mapped across the keyboard so melodic and atonal phrases are both possible within the same patch.
- **v2 Expanded Content** — The second version adds new articulations and samples beyond the original release; check the product page for the full updated content list.
