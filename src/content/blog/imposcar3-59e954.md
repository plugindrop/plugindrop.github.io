## What Is impOSCar3?

impOSCar3 is GForce Software's virtual recreation of the OSCar — a rare, monophonic British synthesizer from the early 1980s designed by Chris Huggett. The original hardware combined digital oscillator technology with analog-style filtering, producing a distinctive character that set it apart from contemporaries like the Minimoog. GForce, a UK-based developer with a long track record of vintage synth emulations, has developed the impOSCar series across multiple generations. Version 3 represents the most current and refined iteration of that lineage.

## Key Features

- **OSCar-modeled oscillators:** Digitally modeled oscillators that capture the hybrid character of the original hardware's unique tonal palette.
- **Analog-style filter:** The filter section models the OSCar's distinctive low-pass design, which contributes heavily to its recognizable sound.
- **Polyphonic expansion:** Unlike the original monophonic/duophonic hardware, the software version extends to full polyphony for modern production use.
- **Arpeggiator and sequencer:** Built-in arpeggiator and step sequencer aligned with the OSCar's original feature set.
- **Expanded modulation routing:** A modulation matrix going beyond the original hardware's limitations for contemporary sound design flexibility.
- **Preset library:** Ships with a curated factory preset bank covering the sonic range the OSCar is known for — bass, leads, and textural patches.
