## What Is Best Service Desert Winds?

Desert Winds is a virtual instrument library created by sample library designer Eduardo Tarilonte and published by Best Service. It features four solo wind instruments from the Middle East and surrounding regions — **Armenian Duduk, Persian Ney, Turkish Ney, and Zourna** — built from over 2,000 individually recorded samples. The library runs on Best Service's free Engine Player (1.5 or higher) and targets composers working in film scoring, world music, and cinematic production.

## Key Features

- **Up to 6 true legato types per instrument** — Enables fluid, realistic phrasing that avoids the "machine gun" effect common in sampled winds.
- **2,000+ high-quality samples** — Deeply sampled with multiple articulations and dynamics across all four instruments.
- **Microtuning module** — Includes 16 patches per instrument with authentic Arabic and Turkish microtuning tables, plus the ability to save custom presets.
- **21 evolving soundscapes** — Each soundscape has 2–6 layers for building atmospheric textures underneath the solo instruments.
- **Multi-format support** — Works as VST3, AU, AAX Native, and Standalone on both Mac (macOS 10.15+) and Windows (10+).
- **Lightweight download** — The full library is approximately 1.5 GB, making it quick to install and easy on drive space.
