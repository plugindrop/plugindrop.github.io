## What Is 808 Cooker?

808 Cooker is a dedicated virtual instrument plugin built around the iconic 808-style bass and kick sounds that define modern trap, hip-hop, drill, and related bass-forward genres. Rather than building 808 tones inside a general-purpose synthesizer, 808 Cooker is purpose-built for producers who want that sound fast and polished. It's available through Plugin Boutique as a standard virtual instrument.

The 808 bass — rooted in the Roland TR-808 drum machine — has been central to beat-making for decades. A dedicated plugin built specifically around it means less sound design overhead and more time on the arrangement.

---

## Key Features

- **808-Focused Sound Engine:** The core architecture is built to deliver the sub-heavy, singing character of the classic Roland TR-808 bass tone.
- **Pitch Slide / Glide Control:** Sculpt the signature 808 "cook" — the gliding, pitch-dropping bass slide essential to trap and hip-hop production.
- **Built-in Distortion and Saturation:** Adds harmonic content to help 808s cut through dense, layered mixes without heavy post-processing.
- **Velocity Sensitivity:** Responds to MIDI velocity for dynamic, expressive 808 performance in real time.
- **Preset Library:** Includes ready-to-use 808 patches designed for immediate integration into your sessions.

| Native Instruments Massive X | ~$149 | Powerful synth for bass sound design, not 808-specific |

If you just need a handful of static 808 hits, free sample packs cover the basics at no cost. For real-time pitch sliding, MIDI-driven performance, and a workflow built entirely around the 808 sound, a dedicated instrument offers meaningfully more control. Serum and Massive X can both produce 808-style bass, but they cost several times more and require substantially more sound design knowledge to get there.
