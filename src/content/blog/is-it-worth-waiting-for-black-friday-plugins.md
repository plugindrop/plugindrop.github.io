**TL;DR:** "Wait for Black Friday" is good advice for exactly one brand in our dataset — FabFilter, where BF is the *only* documented discount all year. It's bad advice for Waves and Plugin Alliance, both of which run deeper discounts on a random Tuesday than most brands run on Black Friday itself. For everyone else — iZotope, UAD, Softube, Native Instruments, Soundtoys, Arturia — Black Friday is one of several equally good windows, not a uniquely special one. Here's the brand-by-brand breakdown, built from our own price-tracking data.

## The brand-by-brand case

### FabFilter — yes, actually wait

This is the one brand where "wait for Black Friday" is unambiguously correct. Every FabFilter plugin we track — Pro-Q 4, Pro-C 2, Pro-L 2, Pro-R 2, Pro-DS, Pro-MB, Pro-G, Saturn 2, Volcano 3, Timeless 3, Twin 3 — shows the identical pattern: full price 364 days a year, roughly 25% off on Black Friday, and nothing in between. [Pro-Q 4's tracked history](/plugin-prices/fabfilter-pro-q-4/) shows exactly that: $199 regular, $149 at BF 2024, and as low as $105.25 at a reseller during BF 2025. If you can hold out until late November, hold out. If you can't, the full-price purchase isn't a mistake — it's just the only option outside that one window.

### Waves — no, and it isn't close

Waves is the brand Black Friday advice was written for, except the advice is backwards. Our tracked checks on the Platinum Bundle show it selling at $60–$199 across six separate dates from May to July (confirmed all-time low: $77) — nowhere near its $1999 list price, and with no clear pattern of November being any deeper than April. Waves' flash-sale cadence means a plugin can be full price on Monday and 80% off by Thursday, any month of the year. Waiting specifically for Black Friday just means missing four or five equally good sales in the meantime.

### Plugin Alliance — no, the promo already recurs

Plugin Alliance's "Any Plugin $29.99" promotion shows up repeatedly in our tracked history across seven different products — [bx_console SSL 4000E](/plugin-prices/plugin-alliance-bx-console-ssl-4000e/), bx_console Neve 88RS, Maag EQ4, SPL Vitalizer, Lindell 80 Series, Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor, and Elysia niveau filter — and it isn't tied to a single calendar date. It's a recurring promo mechanism, not a once-a-year event, which means holding out specifically for Black Friday buys you nothing you couldn't get a few months earlier or later.

### iZotope — somewhat, BF tends to be the deepest cut

This is the closest thing to a genuine "wait for it" case outside FabFilter. [Ozone 11 Standard's](https://www.pluginboutique.com/search?q=iZotope%20Ozone%2011%20Standard&a_aid=69cb95abe1763&chan=art&data1=is-it-worth-waiting-for-black-friday-plugins) tracked history shows a $99 Summer 2024 sale price, then a deeper $70 at Black Friday 2024 — roughly 30% lower than the mid-year discount. Music Production Suite follows a similar shape, with its all-time low ($362) recorded in August rather than November, which muddies the pattern a little. If you're buying an iZotope product and can wait until Q4, our data suggests it's usually worth it — just don't assume BF is the *only* time iZotope discounts.

### Universal Audio (UAD) — no, several windows tie

UAD's storewide "up to 80% off" events aren't exclusive to Black Friday. Our tracked data on the 1176 Classic Limiter Collection, LA-2A Collection, Neve 1073, Pultec Passive EQ Collection, Studer A800, and API Vision Channel Strip all show the same 60–80% off depth without a documented reason to believe BF specifically beats UA's other seasonal sales. Buy whenever the storewide promo banner is up — it doesn't have to be in November.

### Softube — no, seasonal sales match Black Friday's depth

Softube's named sale events — Summer Sale, "Releases Rewind," and Black Friday — all show the same 70–75% off ceiling in our data across Tube-Tech CL 1B, Trident A-Range, Weiss DS1-MK3, and Drawmer S73. There's no evidence BF specifically goes deeper than Softube's other seasonal events.

### Native Instruments (Komplete) — no, Summer Sale matches it

[Komplete 15 Ultimate](/plugin-prices/ni-komplete-15-ultimate/) shows roughly 50% off both at Black Friday and during other major PB promotions across our tracked checks. There's no documented gap between the two — whichever sale comes first is the one to buy into.

### Soundtoys — no, Summer and BF are the same depth

Soundtoys 5.5's confirmed $299 sale price (50% off $599) came from an April 2026 promotion, not a Black Friday one, and individual plugins like Decapitator show the same ~65% off ceiling regardless of season. Summer and Black Friday sales appear interchangeable in depth.

### Arturia — no, discounts recur too often to wait for one date

Arturia's V Collection 11, FX Collection 6, and individual synths (Mini V, Jup-8 V, CS-80 V, Prophet V, Piano V) all show 30–50% off recurring across Black Friday, Plugin Boutique's own Anniversary sale, and Arturia's seasonal promos. With that many sale windows per year, waiting specifically for BF adds delay without adding discount.

### Sonnox — no, the real event is Sonnox's Summer Sale

Sonnox Oxford Inflator's deepest documented discounts — a 75% off Summer Sale price and a one-off $16 (>90% off) "every Sonnox plugin on sale" event — both trace to mid-year promotions in our research, not Black Friday. A single unconfirmed reference to a $29 Black Friday 2024 price exists but isn't corroborated, so we're not treating BF as Sonnox's marquee event.

### Valhalla DSP — doesn't matter, the price is fixed

[Valhalla Room and the rest of the Valhalla line](/plugin-prices/valhalla-room/) sell at a flat $50 with no discounting, by the company's own stated policy. Black Friday changes nothing here — it's the same $50 in November as it is in March.

### Spectrasonics and Xfer (Serum 2) — no, there's nothing to wait for

Omnisphere shows no promotional sales at all in our tracking; Keyscape's deepest documented cut is roughly 5% through authorized resellers, not a BF-specific event. Serum 2's developer has stated publicly that Xfer doesn't run promotions on the perpetual license. None of these plugins reward patience — the regular price is the only price.

## FAQ

**Q: Which brand is it genuinely worth waiting for Black Friday for?**
A: FabFilter. Our tracked history shows exactly one discount window per year — around 25% off at Black Friday — with full price the rest of the time. It's the cleanest "wait for it" case in our entire dataset.

**Q: Which brands should you never wait for?**
A: Waves and Plugin Alliance. Both show deep discounts (75%+ off list) recurring often enough in our tracked history that a specific date doesn't matter — there's usually a comparable sale within a few weeks in either direction.

**Q: Does Black Friday guarantee the lowest price of the year on a plugin?**
A: Not according to our data. For iZotope, UAD, Softube, Arturia, and several others, Black Friday is one of multiple equally deep sale windows across the year (Summer Sale, anniversary promos, storewide events) rather than a uniquely superior one. Only FabFilter and, to a lesser extent, iZotope showed BF as clearly the single best time to buy.
