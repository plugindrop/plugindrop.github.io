## Key Features

- **VariAudio** — Steinberg's built-in pitch and time editor for precise vocal and instrument correction without requiring a separate plugin.
- **MixConsole** — A large-format mixing environment with channel strips, inserts, and advanced routing built into the DAW.
- **Score Editor** — Full notation editing integrated directly into the workflow, aimed at composers who work across MIDI and sheet music simultaneously.
- **Chord Track & Chord Pads** — Harmonic composition tools that sync chord progressions across MIDI and audio tracks for arrangement-focused production.
- **SpectraLayers Integration** — Stem separation and spectral audio editing built into the DAW for advanced audio repair and creative processing.
- **Expression Maps** — Articulation management for MIDI, particularly useful when working with orchestral sample libraries.
