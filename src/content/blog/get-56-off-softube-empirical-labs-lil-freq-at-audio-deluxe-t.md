## What Is the Softube Empirical Labs Lil FrEQ?

The Lil FrEQ is a parametric equalizer plugin developed in collaboration between **Empirical Labs** and **Softube**. Empirical Labs is the hardware company behind the legendary Distressor compressor, and their design philosophy — focused on musicality over clinical precision — carries into this EQ. Softube handles the software implementation, bringing their analog modeling expertise to the partnership. The result is an EQ that aims to feel more like a piece of hardware than a surgical tool.
