Expert Sleepers has announced a pair of new Eurorack modules at Superbooth: the NTX-8CV, a versatile audio and MIDI interface, and Forever, an audio freezer module derived from the disting NT's Freeze algorithm. Two distinct use cases, one focused on CV/MIDI connectivity, the other on ambient and frozen-audio textures.

## What Are the NTX-8CV and Forever?

**NTX-8CV** is an audio and MIDI interface module designed to bridge your Eurorack system with a DAW or external MIDI gear. The "8CV" designation points to multi-channel CV output capabilities, making it a routing powerhouse for modular setups that need to talk to the outside world.

**Forever** takes its core concept directly from the Freeze algorithm found in Expert Sleepers' disting NT module. It captures and holds audio input indefinitely — the kind of tool that turns a single chord stab or texture into an infinite pad. Simple premise, profound creative potential.

- **NTX-8CV multi-channel CV output** — Designed to send multiple CV signals simultaneously, ideal for sequencer-driven or DAW-synced modular workflows

- **Forever Freeze algorithm** — Based directly on the proven Freeze algorithm from Expert Sleepers' disting NT, a well-regarded multi-algorithm module
