## What Is the Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor Class A?

The Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor Class A is a plugin emulation of the legendary Shadow Hills Industries hardware unit — a mastering-grade outboard compressor that retails in the thousands of dollars in hardware form. The plugin is developed by Plugin Alliance, who hold the official license from Shadow Hills Industries. It's designed to bring the character and control of a high-end mastering studio rack into your DAW at a fraction of the cost.

---

## Key Features

- **Dual-Stage Compression** — Combines an optical compressor stage with a discrete transistor (Class A) stage, giving you two distinct compression flavors in a single signal chain.
- **Multiple Transformer Options** — Selectable transformer types (Steel, Nickel, and Iron) each impart different tonal coloration, from bright and punchy to warm and saturated.
- **Optical + Discrete Signal Path** — The two stages can be used independently or in series, allowing for subtle shaping or heavier bus processing depending on your needs.
- **Stereo and Mid/Side Processing** — Supports M/S operation, making it suitable for stereo mastering workflows where independent mid and side control is essential.
- **Gain Reduction Metering** — Dedicated meters for both compression stages let you monitor how much each section is working in real time.
- **Mix Bus and Mastering Focus** — Designed specifically for the final stages of the signal chain, not general-purpose dynamics work.
