## Introduction

<!-- role:intro -->

Neutron 5 is the current version of this mixing plugin suite, succeeding the earlier Neutron 4. It comes in two editions: a full suite containing the complete set of tools, and a lighter Elements edition that pairs a mix assistant with a smaller selection of processors.

The full suite contains eleven plugins in total: one central host plugin plus ten separate component modules, along with a visual mixing view and a routing utility for sending audio between tracks. The individual modules include a clipper, a compressor, a density processor, an equalizer, an exciter, a gate, a phase tool, a sculpting tool, a transient shaper, and additional processing modules beyond those.

Beyond the standalone suite, these tools are also sold inside larger bundles. One such bundle pairs a mastering suite with this mixing suite, a tonal-balance utility, a reverb tool, and a vocal-production suite. A larger collection bundles over a hundred tools spanning mixing, mastering, vocal production, audio repair, and creative effects.

Anyone who already owns an earlier version of the software may be eligible for a reduced upgrade cost once they sign into their account, though no specific amount is given here.

Anyone searching specifically for Neutron 4 should know that current product pages and documentation now center on Neutron 5 instead. There is no information here about how long support or availability for the older version will continue, so anyone still relying on it should check the documentation for that version directly.

This overview sticks to what is documented about the software itself: its editions, included modules, system requirements, and what has changed in the newest release.

## FAQ

<!-- role:faq -->

**Q: Is Neutron 4 still the current version?**

A: No. iZotope's current Neutron product page is for Neutron 5. We have no vendor information about how long Neutron 4 remains supported.

**Q: What is new in Neutron 5?**

A: Per iZotope: new Clipper, Density and Phase modules, an improved Mix Assistant, Transient/Sustain and Mid/Side channel modes, and Delta buttons with improved metering.

**Q: Is Neutron 5 good for beginners?**

A: iZotope says it is designed for both beginners and professionals, with the Assistant giving beginners a custom starting point. We have not tested it hands-on, so we cannot add our own verdict.

**Q: Which operating systems and formats does Neutron 5 support?**

A: macOS 14.7, 15.7 and 26.2 on Intel and Apple silicon, and Windows 10 or 11, in AU, VST3 and AAX (64-bit only).
