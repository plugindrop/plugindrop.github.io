## What Is Phase Plant?

Phase Plant is a modular soft synthesizer developed by **Kilohearts**. It combines multiple synthesis types — including wavetable, FM, and granular — inside a single, highly visual interface built around a flexible generator-and-modulator signal flow. Rather than locking you into one synthesis engine, it lets you stack and combine sound sources freely. The result is a synth that can cover everything from fat pads and evolving textures to aggressive leads and sound design territory that most synths can't reach.

---

## Key Features

- **Multiple Generator Types** — Combine wavetable, analog-modeled, sample, and noise generators in a single patch for layered, hybrid sounds.
- **FM Synthesis** — Built-in FM generator lets you dial in classic digital tones and complex harmonic content without a separate instrument.
- **Granular Engine** — Granular generator enables time-stretching, pitch-shifting, and textural sound design from any loaded sample.
- **Snapin Integration** — Phase Plant plugs directly into Kilohearts' Snapin ecosystem, letting you route modular effects inline within patches.
- **Deep Modulation System** — A visual modulation lane lets you connect LFOs, envelopes, and macros to virtually any parameter.
- **Macro Controls** — Assign up to eight macro knobs for performance control or preset browsing convenience.
