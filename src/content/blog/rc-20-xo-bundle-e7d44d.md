## Key Features

### RC-20 Retro Color
- **Noise Module** — adds vinyl crackle, tape hiss, and static textures to any signal
- **Wobble** — introduces pitch instability and subtle tape flutter for organic movement
- **Distortion** — drives the signal with vintage-style harmonic saturation
- **Space** — a reverb module tuned for adding depth with a lo-fi quality
- **Bit/Rate Reduction** — degrades digital resolution for classic lo-fi and lo-bit textures
- **Limit** — a soft-saturation ceiling that glues peaks naturally

### XO
- **Visual Sample Map** — machine learning plots your drum samples in a 2D scatter map by timbral similarity, so browsing is visual rather than file-tree based
- **Built-in Sound Library** — ships with a curated collection of drum one-shots ready to drag into the map
- **Pattern Sequencer** — built-in step sequencer handles beat programming inside the plugin
- **Import Your Own Samples** — load personal libraries and have XO remap them automatically
