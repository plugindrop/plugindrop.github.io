## What Is Neutron 5?

Neutron 5 is iZotope's flagship AI-assisted channel strip plugin, built for mixing and mastering workflows. It combines traditional mixing tools — EQ, compression, transient shaping, and exciter — with machine-learning features that analyze tracks and suggest settings automatically. iZotope is one of the most recognized names in professional audio software, and Neutron has been a staple in mixing-focused studios for years.

---

## Key Features

- **Mix Assistant** — AI-driven analysis that balances multiple tracks against a focus element, giving you a starting mix in seconds rather than hours.
- **Track Enhance** — Automatically suggests per-track settings based on audio content type (vocals, drums, bass, etc.).
- **Dynamic EQ** — A fully featured dynamic equalizer that responds to transient peaks and sustained tones independently.
- **Spectral Shaper** — Targets and tames problem frequencies in the spectral domain, useful for harsh resonances or cymbal bleed.
- **Sculptor** — Shapes the overall tonal profile of a track using instrument-specific spectral models.
- **Visual Mixer** — A session-wide mixer view that lets you adjust volume and panning for all Neutron instances from a single interface.
