## What Is New in RX 12

The vendor's own new-features table lists these changes. Which edition gets each one is noted in brackets.

- **Scene Rebalance** (Advanced). A new module for adjusting dialogue, music and effects levels independently in a finished mix.
- **Stems View** (Standard and Advanced). Splits audio into stems through Scene Rebalance, Music Rebalance or Dialogue Isolate, then lets you apply other RX tools to a stem.
- **Trim Silence** (Advanced). A new module that detects and deletes gaps in audio files based on a threshold you set.
- **Music Rebalance real-time plugin** (Standard and Advanced), with improved neural nets. Splits vocals, bass, percussion and other instruments, offline or in real time.
- **De-bleed** (Standard and Advanced). Rebuilt on machine learning, and now also available as a real-time plugin. The vendor says it can isolate competing instruments without a reference track.
- **Breath Control** (Standard and Advanced). Rebuilt on machine learning.
- **Dialogue Isolate** (Standard and Advanced). Improved neural nets across offline and real-time modes.
- **Repair Assistant** (all three editions). Improved neural nets. You pick the material type (voice, tonal/harmonic instruments, percussion or sound effects), and it proposes fixes at light, medium or aggressive intensity.
- **Interface changes.** A larger default spectrogram, a module search bar and a resizable History panel.

## What Each Edition Includes

RX has two parts: plugins that load in a DAW or video editor, and a standalone audio editor. According to the vendor's comparison tables, the standalone editor comes with Standard and Advanced, and Elements is a set of six plugins.

### RX 12 Elements ($99)

Six plugins: De-click, De-clip, De-hum, De-reverb, Voice De-noise and the Repair Assistant.

### RX 12 Standard ($399)

Everything in Elements, plus (per the plugin comparison table): Breath Control, Connect, De-bleed, De-crackle, De-ess, De-plosive, Dialogue Isolate, Guitar De-noise, Monitor, Mouth De-click, Music Rebalance, Spectral De-noise and the Spectral Editor (with ARA support for Logic Pro 10.7+ in Rosetta and Studio One 7 / Studio Pro 8). The standalone editor adds modules such as Spectral Repair, Loudness Optimize, Streaming Preview, Loudness Control, Time & Pitch, Dither and Resample.

### RX 12 Advanced ($1,399)

Everything in Standard, plus these modules the vendor lists as Advanced-only:

- Ambience Match, Azimuth, Center Extract, De-rustle, De-wind, Deconstruct
- Dialogue Contour, EQ Match, Leveler, Scene Rebalance
- Spectral Recovery, Trim Silence, Wow & Flutter
- Multichannel processing and Multiple Speaker Detection in Text Navigation

The vendor also notes that the Advanced version of Dialogue Isolate adds a high-quality offline mode and multi-band processing. Ambience Match, De-rustle and Scene Rebalance are marked as AAX AudioSuite only in the plugin table.

## How to Choose

- **Only need common fixes (clicks, clipping, hum, reverb, voice noise):** the vendor's Elements edition lists exactly those tools, plus the Repair Assistant.
- **Want the standalone editor, spectral repair, stem separation or loudness tools:** these start at Standard.
- **Working with dialogue, music and effects in a finished mix, or need Ambience Match, De-rustle or Spectral Recovery:** those are Advanced-only per the vendor's tables.
- **Already own an earlier RX:** sign in on iZotope's site to see whether a better price is offered for your account before paying list price.
- **Need more than repair tools:** the RX Post Production Suite 9 bundle ($1,799) includes RX 12 Advanced, Neutron 5, Equinox, Nectar 4 Advanced, Insight 2 and more. Compare its price against buying the pieces you need separately.

The vendor's product pages also show a "Try for free" button.
