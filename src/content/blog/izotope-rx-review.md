## Introduction

<!-- role:intro -->

The current version of this audio repair software is RX 12, not RX 11. It comes in three editions — Elements, Standard, and Advanced — along with a larger Post Production Suite bundle that pairs the Advanced edition with several other audio tools. The three editions differ mainly in how many modules and plugins each one includes, with the top tier offering the full set of more than fifty tools. This overview sticks to what's officially documented: the available editions, what's new in this release, and the system requirements. It has not been tested firsthand.

## RX 12 Elements ($99)

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:RX 12 Elements ($99) -->

Six plugins: De-click, De-clip, De-hum, De-reverb, Voice De-noise and the Repair Assistant.

## RX 12 Standard ($399)

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:RX 12 Standard ($399) -->

Everything in Elements, plus (per the plugin comparison table): Breath Control, Connect, De-bleed, De-crackle, De-ess, De-plosive, Dialogue Isolate, Guitar De-noise, Monitor, Mouth De-click, Music Rebalance, Spectral De-noise and the Spectral Editor (with ARA support for Logic Pro 10.7+ in Rosetta and Studio One 7 / Studio Pro 8). The standalone editor adds modules such as Spectral Repair, Loudness Optimize, Streaming Preview, Loudness Control, Time & Pitch, Dither and Resample.

## RX 12 Advanced ($1,399)

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:RX 12 Advanced ($1,399) -->

Everything in Standard, plus these modules the vendor lists as Advanced-only:

- Ambience Match, Azimuth, Center Extract, De-rustle, De-wind, Deconstruct

- Dialogue Contour, EQ Match, Leveler, Scene Rebalance

- Spectral Recovery, Trim Silence, Wow & Flutter

- Multichannel processing and Multiple Speaker Detection in Text Navigation

The vendor also notes that the Advanced version of Dialogue Isolate adds a high-quality offline mode and multi-band processing. Ambience Match, De-rustle and Scene Rebalance are marked as AAX AudioSuite only in the plugin table.

## FAQ

<!-- role:faq -->

**Q: Does RX 12 run as a plugin inside my DAW?**

A: Yes. The vendor lists AU, AAX, AAX AudioSuite and VST3 plugin formats, and ARA versions of the Spectral Editor for Logic Pro (10.7+ in Rosetta) and Studio One 7 / Studio Pro 8.

**Q: Does RX 12 run on Apple Silicon?**

A: The system requirements list macOS Sonoma, Sequoia and Tahoe on Intel Macs and Apple silicon (M-series) Macs, noting "Native & Rosetta".

**Q: Does my RX 11 still work?**

A: We do not have vendor information on how long RX 11 remains supported. Check iZotope's support pages for your version.
