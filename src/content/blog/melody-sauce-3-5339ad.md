## What Is Melody Sauce 3?

Melody Sauce 3 is a MIDI melody generation plugin built to help producers create musical ideas quickly inside a DAW. It sits in your project as a MIDI effect or instrument slot and generates melodic phrases based on the key, scale, and settings you choose. It's aimed at electronic music producers who want a starting point for a hook, lead line, or bassline without hand-programming every note. ## Key Features

- **Key and scale-aware generation** — melodies are built around a musical key and scale you select, so results stay in tune with your project.
- **Drag-and-drop MIDI export** — generated ideas can be dragged straight into your DAW's piano roll for further editing.
- **Mood and style controls** — adjustable parameters shape how simple or complex the generated melody sounds.
- **Chord and progression tools** — build a harmonic base to feed into the melody generator.
- **Randomization and regeneration** — quickly cycle through new melodic variations until one clicks.
