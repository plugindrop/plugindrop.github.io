## What Is The Very Loud Indeed Co.?

The Very Loud Indeed Co. is a UK-based developer specializing in cinematic sound design tools for composers and sound designers. Their catalogue spans Kontakt sample libraries, Omnisphere soundsets, Zebra 2 patches, Serum presets, and more — all geared toward film, TV, and game scoring. Think dark textures, hybrid percussion, mutated guitars, and eerie vocal explorations. They've built a focused niche in the tension-and-atmosphere corner of scoring.
