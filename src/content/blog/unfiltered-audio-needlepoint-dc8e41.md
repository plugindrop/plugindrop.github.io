## What Is Needlepoint?

Per the [Plugin Alliance product page](https://www.plugin-alliance.com/products/needlepoint), Needlepoint is "a revolution in vinyl simulation" from Unfiltered Audio. Rather than looping samples of vinyl noise, it uses physical modeling to synthesize record imperfections such as hair, dust mites, scratches and warping, distributed on a virtually rotating, algorithmic platter. It can be used subtly or pushed well past realism for warped sounds. (An earlier version of this page described it as a tape emulation; that was incorrect.)

## Key Features (from Plugin Alliance)

- **Physically modeled vinyl emulation** with a choice of classic and custom turntable speeds, including tempo sync to the DAW.
- **WOW, FLUTTER and PITCH** controls with continuous adjustment and automation support.
- **SPINDOWN button** for vinyl stop and start effects.
- **BROKEN mode** for looping, stutter and glitch effects.
- **Granular pitch shifter** for chopped-up effects without changing playback speed.
- **Single-knob compressor** with 6 modes, which Plugin Alliance says recreates algorithms from hardware samplers and can mimic distortion from the vinyl pressing process.
- Selectable color schemes and a set of handcrafted presets; a 14-day trial is offered.

## Formats and System Requirements (from Plugin Alliance)

- Formats: AAX AudioSuite, AAX Native, AU, AUv3, VST2, VST3
- macOS 13 to macOS 26 (Intel or Apple Silicon); Windows 10 and 11 (x64)
- 2 GB RAM; display resolution of 1280 x 960 or 1440 x 900 or higher
