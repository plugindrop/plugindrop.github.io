## What Is TONEX MAX?

TONEX MAX is the top-tier edition of IK Multimedia's AI-powered amp modeling platform. It uses neural network technology to capture the exact sonic behavior of real amplifiers, cabinets, and pedals — a process IK calls "Tone Modeling." The result is highly accurate digital replicas of physical rigs, usable inside your DAW as a plugin or as a standalone application. TONEX MAX sits above the standard and SE tiers, unlocking the platform's full feature set.

---

## Key Features

- **AI Tone Capture** — Neural modeling technology that analyzes and replicates the dynamic response of real amps and pedals at the hardware level.
- **ToneNET Access** — Connects to IK Multimedia's community library where thousands of user-created Tone Models are available to download and use.
- **Plugin + Standalone** — Runs as VST/AU/AAX in any major DAW and as a standalone application for direct recording or live use.
- **Expanded Tone Model Library** — MAX tier includes the largest bundled collection of factory captures across amp and pedal types.
- **Hardware Integration** — Compatible with the TONEX Pedal hardware unit, allowing the same captures to run on a pedalboard in a live setting.
- **Amp, Cab & Pedal Capture** — Supports capturing not just amps but also preamps, pedals, and full signal chains for a complete rig-in-a-box workflow.

## Who Is It For?
