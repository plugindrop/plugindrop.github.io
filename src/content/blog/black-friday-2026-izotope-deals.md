## iZotope VocalSynth 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope VocalSynth 2 -->

iZotope VocalSynth 2 suits vocalists and producers who want to reshape a voice with blendable effects like Vocoder, Talkbox, Polyvox, Compuvox, and the vocal-tract-modeled Biovox for smooth, textural treatments. They can work through Auto Mode, MIDI Mode, or Sidechain Mode, reordering the seven stompbox-style effects (including Chorus, Ring Mod, and Shred with sequencer) to shape vocals, and pair it with Neutron 2's Visual Mixer or Tonal Balance Control for a broader view of the mix.

## iZotope Trash
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope Trash -->

This plugin suits producers and mix engineers who want a flexible, often unpredictable distortion and texture tool for shaping specific elements like bass, bass guitar, guitar, or piano within a full mix. They can blend up to four distortion settings on the XY pad, layer up to four impulse responses (including their own loaded files) with the Convolve module, target low, mid, or high frequency bands separately, and use the dice feature or Envelope Follower to discover new combinations quickly.

## iZotope RX 12 Elements
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope RX 12 Elements -->

iZotope RX 12 Elements suits content creators and musicians who need to clean up recordings rather than do deep post-production repair work, since it packages six streamlined tools for restoration tasks like click, clip, hum, and reverb removal alongside voice noise reduction. They can use its Repair Assistant plugin to let the software analyze problem audio and apply fixes automatically, turning to the individual De-click, De-clip, De-hum, De-reverb, and Voice De-noise tools when they want to target a specific issue by hand.

## iZotope FXEQ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope FXEQ -->

FXEQ suits producers and mixers working with bass, drums, synths, vocals, or keyboards who want to add saturation, lo-fi texture, modulation, reverb, or delay without building complex routing or sends. They can dial in effects directly through each module's 6-band EQ and filter shapes, start from one of over 50 presets using the random picker, and use the zero-latency Limiter or "FX Only" mode when running it as a send effect.

## iZotope Ozone 12 Elements
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope Ozone 12 Elements -->

iZotope Ozone 12 Elements suits anyone doing mastering work who wants guided help shaping a final mix, including vocal-heavy tracks, without having to build a signal chain from scratch. Users can run the Custom Assistant flow to target a genre or reference, pull in specific modules, and hit desired loudness levels, while leaning on the Vocal Checker and Assistive Vocal Balance to keep vocals sitting correctly, and the IRC 5 Maximizer mode for louder, cleaner limiting at the final stage.

## iZotope RX 12 Advanced
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope RX 12 Advanced -->

iZotope RX 12 Advanced suits audio post and music professionals handling restoration-heavy work—film, TV, podcast, and music editors who need to repair flawed recordings and clean up dialogue, music, or ambient tracks. They can use it either as a standalone application for editing and restoring audio files directly, or by loading its plugins (AU, AAX, AAX Audiosuite, VST3, with ARA support for Logic Pro and Studio One) inside their DAW to apply its 50+ restoration tools within an existing session.

## iZotope Neutron 5
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope Neutron 5 -->

Neutron 5 suits mixers who want machine-learning-guided help shaping a mix at any skill level, from first mixes to more demanding sessions, rather than those who want to dial in every parameter manually. They can run the suite's mothership or its individual component plugins, lean on Mix Assistant to set a starting signal chain, use Delta buttons to hear exactly what each module changes, and apply mid/side or transient/sustain modes to target processing precisely.

## iZotope Insight 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:iZotope Insight 2 -->
