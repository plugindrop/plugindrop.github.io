## What Is Portatron?

Portatron is a virtual instrument by Robotic Bean that emulates the experience of a 4-track cassette recorder — think Tascam Portastudio vibes, but inside your DAW. It's part tape machine, part sampler, part Mellotron-style instrument. You load samples onto four independent tape tracks, dial in lo-fi character (wobble, dropout, saturation), and play back everything as drones, loops, or MIDI-triggered notes. It ships with a library of included samples spanning piano, vibraphone, vocals, synths, hydrophones, and shortwave radio recordings. Additional sound packs called "Portapacks" are available separately.

## Key Features

- **4 Independent Tape Tracks** — Each track has its own level, pan, EQ, and drive controls, letting you build layered soundscapes from the ground up.
- **3 Play Modes (Drone, Loop, Instrument)** — Play along with DAW transport for endless drones, sync loops to bars, or trigger samples monophonically from a MIDI keyboard like a classic Mellotron.
- **Full Lo-Fi Tape Control** — Adjust tape speed (0–200%), noise, dropouts, wobble, and start/stop lag. Switch between Normal and Chrome tape types for different tonal character.
- **Built-In FX** — Onboard delay and reverb keep things self-contained without needing external processing.
- **Tape Editor** — Manipulate samples directly with built-in editing tools for trimming and looping.
- **Auto Fade (v1.5)** — Moving one channel's level automatically nudges the others, making live-performance-style mixing easier.

## FAQ

**Q: What plugin formats does Portatron support?**
A: VST3, AU, and AAX. The AAX version requires Pro Tools 11.3 or later.

**Q: What are the system requirements?**
A: Windows 10 or higher, macOS 10.13 or higher, and approximately 500MB of disk space. Check the [official Robotic Bean site](https://roboticbean.com/products/portatron/) for the most current specs.

**Q: Can I load my own samples?**
A: Yes — Portatron includes a tape editor for importing and manipulating your own samples alongside the included sound library.

**Q: Is Portatron polyphonic?**
A: No. It's monophonic by design — you can layer up to four sounds across the tape tracks, but each track plays one note at a time, staying true to the tape-machine concept.
