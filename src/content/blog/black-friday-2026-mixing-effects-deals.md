## Waves CLA-2A
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves CLA-2A -->

The Waves CLA-2A suits engineers mixing vocal, bass, and guitar tracks who want the alluring, warm sound of the classic electro-optical tube compressor without hunting down the original hardware. They can use its Limiter mode to boost instrument sustain, its Mix knob for parallel compression, the included Chris Lord-Alge presets as a starting point, and the sidechain frequency filter for more targeted control, all with zero added latency in the studio or live.

## Waves CLA-76
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves CLA-76 -->

The Waves CLA-76 suits mix engineers and producers who want a fast, aggressive FET-style compressor for tracking transient-heavy sources like drums and vocals, offering a choice between the "Blacky" and "Bluey" tonal characters. Users can apply it with zero added latency in both studio and live mixing contexts, dial in sound using the ALL ratio mode or Chris Lord-Alge's presets, and use the Mix and Trim controls for parallel compression and level matching.

## Waves H-Reverb
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves H-Reverb -->

H-Reverb suits producers and engineers who want both natural-sounding spaces and more experimental, time-bending reverb textures, and who range from those wanting quick results to those wanting deep sonic control, since its interface can collapse to basic settings or expand for detailed shaping. Users can start from one of its over 240 presets or build a space from scratch by blending its algorithmic and convolution engines, then shape the result with EQ, compression, ducking, de-essing, modulation, overdrive, resonance, and pre/post delay (with BPM-synced pre-delay for rhythmic effects), in mono, stereo, or 5.0/5.1 surround.

<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Soundtoys Decapitator -->

Soundtoys Decapitator suits mixers and producers who want to add analog-style saturation character to vocals, drums, and synths without complex routing. Users can select from its five analog saturation models, shape the tone with the modeled tone control, dial in a parallel blend using the mix knob, and push the sound harder with the Punish button for more extreme results.

## UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator -->

The UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator suits producers and mix engineers who want the warmth and presence of a classic plate reverb on vocals or dense mixes, using damping and filter controls alongside plug-in-only Balance, Width, and Modulation settings to shape the sound. It runs as a VST3, AU, or AAX plugin in a supported DAW on recent macOS or Windows 10/11 systems with an Intel, AMD, or Apple silicon processor, authorized via a free iLok account with optional iLok USB hardware.

## Sonible smart:EQ 4
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Sonible smart:EQ 4 -->

smart:EQ 4 suits mixing engineers and producers who want AI-assisted spectral balance across vocals, drums, bass, and full mixes rather than manual band-by-band EQ work. They can use it by applying its smart:filter to individual tracks or busses to correct tonal imbalances, and by grouping up to ten instances together to build an unmasking hierarchy that keeps competing elements clear across an entire mix.

## Soundtoys Devil-Loc Deluxe
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Soundtoys Devil-Loc Deluxe -->

Soundtoys Devil-Loc Deluxe suits producers and engineers working with drums, room mics, vocals, acoustic guitar, or lo-fi loops who want an extreme, gritty compression effect capable of making tracks sound huge and dark. They can dial in the amount of grit with the Mix knob, shape tone with the Darkness control, and choose fast or slow release times to fit the source material.
