## What Is Drums Against Humanity?

Drums Against Humanity is a virtual drum instrument developed by Ugritone in collaboration with Zbigniew "Inferno" Prominski — the drummer behind Behemoth and Azarath. The library captures Inferno's signature sound, recorded at Tall Pine Records in Kolbudy, Poland by producer Haldor Grunberg (who also worked on Behemoth's chart-topping 2022 album *Opvs Contra Nvturam*). It runs on Ugritone's proprietary sampling engine and is built specifically for extreme and heavy metal production.

## Key Features

- **Massive drum kit selection** — Includes multiple bass drums, 5 snares, 8 toms, and an extensive collection of cymbals, covering a wide range of metal tones.
- **10,000+ multi-samples** — Detailed round robins, velocity layers, and multiple mic channels deliver realistic drum performances straight out of the box.
- **Built-in mixing tools** — A drum-specific compressor, pitch tuning, EQ, and humanizer are all baked into the plugin, reducing the need for external processing.
- **Integrated FX bus** — Reverb, saturation, and delay effects are included, so you can shape your drum sound without leaving the instrument.
- **Genre-specific kit presets** — Pre-crafted starting points tuned for different metal subgenres and styles, saving setup time.
- **Wide format support** — Available as VST, VST3, AU, and AAX on both 64-bit Windows and macOS.
