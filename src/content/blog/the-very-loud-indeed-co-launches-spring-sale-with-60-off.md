## What Is The Very Loud Indeed Co.?

The Very Loud Indeed Co. is a UK-based sound design company focused on cinematic scoring tools for composers and producers. Their catalog spans Kontakt sample libraries, Omnisphere 2 soundsets, Serum presets, and u-he Zebra2 patches — all built around dark, textural, and hybrid-organic sound design.
