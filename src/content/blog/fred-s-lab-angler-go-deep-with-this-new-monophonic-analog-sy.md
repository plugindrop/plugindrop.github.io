## What Is Fred's Lab Angler?

Fred's Lab is a synthesizer manufacturer known for building hands-on analog hardware aimed at producers and performers who want tactile control over their sound. The Angler is their latest monophonic analog synthesizer — a single-voice instrument built for those who want to go deep into bass lines, leads, and expressive analog tones. Monophonic analog synths occupy a specific, well-loved niche: one note at a time, pure analog signal path, and the kind of warmth that digital emulations spend years chasing.
