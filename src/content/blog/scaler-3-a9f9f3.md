## What Is Scaler 3?

Scaler 3 is a music-theory and composition application from Scaler Music. It is designed to help users build chord progressions, explore harmony, create melodic and bass parts, and send MIDI to instruments in a DAW.

The software works as both a standalone application and a plug-in. Officially supported formats include VST, VST3, AU, and AAX, so it is intended for common desktop production workflows.

It is particularly relevant for producers who start with chords and want to turn them into arranged MIDI ideas. The Arrange Page places chords, melody, bass, and phrase material into dedicated timeline lanes.

## Scaler 3 Key Features

- **Chord progression building:** Drag and drop chords to construct progressions, then explore related chords, substitutions, inversions, extensions, and voicing options.

- **Arrange Page timeline:** Dedicated lanes for chords, melody, bass, and phrases let users organize MIDI ideas into a song-oriented layout.

- **Motions library:** Scaler 3 groups phrases, performances, melodies, and basslines into “Motions,” with tags intended to help locate material by mood.

- **Modulation Suggestions:** The software includes pathways for moving between keys, including secondary scales, modal interchange, mediants, and Neo-Riemannian options.

- **Bass Follow Track:** This feature can generate a bassline that follows the root note of the chord track.

- **External MIDI and plug-in hosting:** Scaler 3 can route material to external instruments through MIDI output, and it can host compatible VST/AU instruments and effects within the application.

## Who Is It For?

- **Songwriters building progressions:** Scaler 3 is designed for finding chords, developing harmonic movement, and arranging progressions into structured ideas.

- **Electronic music producers:** Its chord, bass, melody, and phrase lanes suit MIDI-based workflows where multiple parts need to follow one harmonic source.

- **Composers learning or applying harmony:** Features such as the Circle of Fifths, chord substitutions, voicings, and modulation tools provide ways to explore harmonic relationships.

- **Keyboard-based creators:** Bind to Keyboard can map chord playback to a MIDI keyboard for one-finger chords, melodies, and arpeggios.

Users who only need a basic chord chart or a lightweight MIDI utility may not need Scaler 3’s standalone workflow, multi-lane arrangement tools, or internal sound set. Check the product page for full compatibility details before buying.
