## What Is SoundID Reference for Speakers & Headphones?

SoundID Reference is a professional monitoring calibration tool developed by Sonarworks. It applies correction curves to your monitoring setup — speakers or headphones — to compensate for acoustic coloration introduced by your room or transducers, giving you a more neutral and accurate listening environment. The combined Speakers & Headphones version handles both: speaker calibration via a measurement process and headphone correction through a library of pre-measured profiles. It runs as a standalone system-wide app and loads directly into your DAW as a plugin.

## Key Features

- **Speaker room calibration** — measure your room and speakers with a compatible microphone to generate a custom correction profile that targets a flat frequency response
- **Headphone profile library** — hundreds of pre-measured profiles for popular studio headphones, usable without any additional hardware
- **Individual headphone calibration** — optional mic-based measurement for tighter per-unit accuracy beyond the standard library profiles
- **Standalone + plugin operation** — works system-wide outside the DAW or as a VST/AU/AAX insert inside your session
- **A/B correction toggle** — instantly compare corrected and uncorrected monitoring to audit how your mix will translate
