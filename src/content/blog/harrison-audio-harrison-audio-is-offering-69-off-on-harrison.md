## What Is Harrison Multi-Band Compressor?

Harrison Audio's Multi-Band Compressor is a dynamics plugin designed to give producers and engineers independent compression control over separate frequency bands. Rather than applying a single compressor across the full spectrum, multiband compression lets you tame a boomy low end, control a harsh midrange, or glue a sibilant top end — without affecting the rest of the signal.

Harrison Audio brings its console engineering philosophy to this plugin, making it relevant for both mixing and mastering workflows.
