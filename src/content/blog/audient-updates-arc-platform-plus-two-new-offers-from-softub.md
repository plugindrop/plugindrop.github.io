## What Is Audient ARC?

ARC — the Audient Resource Centre — is Audient's centralized hub for hardware owners. It handles gear registration, firmware and software updates, and provides access to a growing library of free plugins and educational content from industry partners. Think of it as the command center for your Audient ecosystem. With the March 2026 relaunch, the platform has been overhauled with a more intuitive interface, smarter onboarding, and two major new software additions from Softube and Excite Audio.

## Key Features of the Updated ARC Platform

- **Centralized hardware management** — Register your Audient gear and handle all firmware and software updates from a single dashboard.
- **Intelligent onboarding** — A guided setup process designed to get you from unboxing to recording in minutes, not hours.
- **Expanding free plugin library** — Partners now include Steinberg, Softube, Excite Audio, Spitfire Audio, Strymon, Output, Sonarworks, and Produce Like a Pro.
- **Softube Flow Guitar Essentials** — A free perpetual license providing a collection of guitar amps, cabinets, and effects built for fast DAW workflow.
- **Excite Audio Lifetime Lite Bundle** — Includes Lifeline Expanse Lite and Lifeline Console Lite, offering 10 rearrangeable effects for production, mixing, and mastering. Combined value: $79/£79.
- **Learning resources** — Direct access to product tutorials, how-to videos, and educational content.

## Who Is It For?

- **Existing Audient hardware owners** — If you already own any Audient interface or console, these plugins are waiting for you at no extra cost. Just register on ARC.
- **Guitarists producing in a DAW** — Softube Flow Guitar Essentials provides amp modeling, cabinets, and effects without needing a separate guitar plugin purchase.
- **Producers looking for creative multi-effects** — Excite Audio's Lifeline plugins add texture, depth, and tonal character across production, mixing, and mastering stages.
- **Budget-conscious beginners** — Audient interfaces already sit at competitive price points. The growing ARC software bundle makes the total value proposition even stronger.
