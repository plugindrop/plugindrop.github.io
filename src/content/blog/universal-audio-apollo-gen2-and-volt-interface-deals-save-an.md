## What Are the Universal Audio Apollo and Volt Interfaces?

Universal Audio is one of the most respected names in professional audio hardware. The Apollo series is their flagship interface line, built around Unison preamp technology and real-time UAD DSP processing. The Volt series sits at the more accessible end of their lineup — USB-powered interfaces designed for home studios and mobile producers who want UA quality without the Thunderbolt requirement.

Both lines are manufactured to professional standards and pair tightly with UA's LUNA recording software and plugin ecosystem.

**Apollo Gen 2 Interfaces:**

- **High-Resolution Conversion** — designed for professional studio tracking and mixing workflows
