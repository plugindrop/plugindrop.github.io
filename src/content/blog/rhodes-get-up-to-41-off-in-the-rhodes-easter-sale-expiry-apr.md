## What Is Rhodes Software?

Rhodes — yes, *that* Rhodes — makes official virtual instrument and effects plugins modeled on their iconic electric pianos. Their software lineup includes the V8 and V8 Pro virtual instruments (hyper-realistic recreations of the Rhodes MK8 electric piano) and the V-Rack multi-effects plugin (based on the MK8's analog preamp and FX circuit). These are the only officially licensed Rhodes plugins on the market, built from samples and modeling of actual Rhodes hardware.

## Key Features

- **30,000 samples from the MK8** — The V8 is built from recordings of Rhodes' flagship MK8 piano, capturing its full tonal range with remarkable detail.
- **Up to 127 velocity layers** — Fine-grained dynamic response across the keyboard for expressive, realistic playing.
- **14 articulations** — Sustains, staccatos, portamentos, legatos, note-offs, sympathetic resonances, and mechanical noises are all included.
- **Modeled MK8 FX board (V8 Pro)** — VCA Compressor with sidechain, 4-Stage VCA Phaser, Bucket-Brigade Chorus, and Bucket-Brigade Delay.
- **V-Rack standalone effects** — Seven classic analog effect emulations including 3-mode Drive, resonant 3-band parametric EQ, Vari-Pan, and the full MK8 effects chain.
- **Cross-platform support** — Available as VST2, VST3, AU, and AAX for both Mac and PC.
