**TL;DR:** Track temporary price drops for 14 plugins and see [our evergreen comparisons](/brands/cherry-audio/) for longer-term research.

Cherry Audio has rolled out a wave of new plugins spanning tonewheel organs, electric pianos, filters, drum machines, and deep vintage synth emulations. This roundup looks at what each one brings to the table, from modeled circuitry and expanded polyphony to studio-quality effects chains and extensive preset libraries.

## Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ -->

Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ suits keyboardists and producers working in blues, gospel, rock, and jazz styles who want the classic tonewheel sound alongside modern studio tools. They can use its Main, Edit, Cabinet, and Perform sections to dial in drawbar settings, percussion, and overdrive, then shape the tone further with the built-in tape echo delay, reverb, and seven-band graphic EQ.

## Cherry Audio Filtomika
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Filtomika -->

Filtomika suits producers who want to push drums, guitars, synths, or vocals through a wild, unpredictable filter modeled on the Soviet Polivoks synthesizer rather than a clean, surgical EQ-style tool. They can load it as an insert or bus effect on mono or stereo sources, dial in the Starve, Filter Drive, and Amp Drive controls or the syncable modulator for extreme textures, or start from one of the over 30 presets and shape it further with MIDI learn and DAW automation.

## Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B -->

Wurlybird 140B suits pianists and producers working in classic rock and pop styles who want the distinctive electric piano tone heard on recordings by artists like Ray Charles, Queen, and Supertramp. They can load it as an AU, VST, VST3, AAX, or standalone instrument and shape the sound using the Vibrato, Tune, Hum, and Velocity Curve controls along with the onboard stompbox-style effects and 50 included presets.

## Cherry Audio Atomika
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Atomika -->

Atomika suits producers and synth enthusiasts drawn to gritty, characterful analog sounds who want the twisted vintage filter of the Polivoks without being limited to its original duophonic, two-oscillator design. They can load one of the over 350 presets or build patches using the extended filter modes, up to 16-voice polyphony with unison, the arpeggiator, and the four integrated effects to take the sound from faithful emulation into fuller, more modern productions.

## Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine -->

Cherry Audio's KR-55C suits producers drawn to post-punk and new wave drum sounds who want deep sound-shaping control, since it combines 240 factory patterns from the original KR-55A and KR-55B with 200 new patterns and kits, plus per-instrument EQ, tuning, and decay editing. They can program rhythms in real time or step-by-step on the 16-button X0X-style layout, chain up to 99 patterns in Song Mode, shape the mix with the per-instrument effects routing matrix (overdrive, flanger/chorus, delay, reverb), and route finished patterns straight into a DAW via drag-and-drop MIDI export, individual outs, and full automation.

## Cherry Audio Yellowjacket
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Yellowjacket -->

Yellowjacket suits producers chasing the buzzy, quirky tone of the 1978 EDP Wasp but who also want modern extras, since it reproduces the original oscillators, filter, and speaker emulation while adding 16-voice polyphony and an arpeggiator with probability and humanise controls. They can dial in that character through presets across categories like Leads, Arps, Pads, Chords, Strings, and Basses, or build chained-synth textures using the Multi voice modes and their adjustable "mini synth" panels for single-fingered chords and lush stereo effects.

## Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit -->

Crumar Spirit suits producers and sound designers who want a monophonic analog synth with deep modulation and multiple voicing modes, offering unison, polyphonic, and cycle voice modes alongside extensive routing through the Mod X, Shaper Y, and Matrix Z sections. They can use its multi-voice panels and quick-access presets to add per-note variation and subtle-to-extreme detuning, while leaning on the over 430 included presets, built-in arpeggiator, and dual effects chains to shape everything from vocal-like filtered tones to drones and complex triggered patterns.

## Cherry Audio DS 2 Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio DS 2 Synthesizer -->

Cherry Audio DS-2 suits producers chasing gritty, harmonically edgy "vintage digital" tones who want more flexibility than the 1978 original offered, such as layered or split synth/poly textures, unison and multi-voice stacking, and arpeggiated patterns. They can shape sounds using the expanded Synth and Poly sections, route modulation through the six-destination LFO mixer and three independent effects chains, and start from the included presets or MIDI-mapped controls to dial in sounds live or in the DAW.

## Cherry Audio SH MAX
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio SH MAX -->

Cherry Audio SH-MAX suits producers who want one plugin covering classic SH-series synth tones (SH-5, SH-7, SH-3A) for basslines, leads, and layered chords, whether playing mono, duophonic, unison, or full 16-voice polyphony. They can route any of its five sound sources through the dual filter sections and four independent effects chains, then shape sequences on the built-in step sequencer, which syncs to a DAW and can output MIDI directly to the timeline.

## Cherry Audio Ensoniq ESQ 1 Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Ensoniq ESQ 1 Synthesizer -->

Cherry Audio's Ensoniq ESQ-1 suits producers and sound designers who want the complex, evolving digital/analog hybrid textures of the 1986 original, especially those who already own ESQ-1 SysEx patches or hardware they'd like to bring into a modern workflow. Users can explore nearly 400 presets (including the original factory patches) across the Play, Edit, Modulation, Envelopes, and Sequencer pages, shape sounds with the three-oscillator voices, CEM3379-modeled filters, and four-slot modulation matrix, perform expressively via MPE and polyphonic aftertouch, and import or export patches directly via SysEx or MIDI to move between the plug-in and original hardware.

## Cherry Audio Mercury 8
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Mercury 8 -->

Cherry Audio Mercury-8 suits producers and sound designers who want the classic JP-8 analog synth sound with modern flexibility, including split/stacked layer work and vintage character shaping. They can use its dual-layer architecture with per-layer oscillators, filters, and envelopes, dial in analog drift and condition controls for a vintage or pristine feel, build patterns with the arpeggiator and polyphonic step sequencer, shape tone through the three independent effects chains, and start from the over 600 included presets such as the recreated JP-8 factory patches.

## Cherry Audio P 10 Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio P 10 Synthesizer -->

P-10 suits players who already know the classic dual-manual analog polysynth and want its layered/split architecture, modeled Curtis 3340 oscillators and 3320 filters, and factory P5/P10 presets in a plugin. They can configure it as two independent synths, one combined ten-voice synth, or a layered/split keyboard, then shape sounds using the Mono Mod, Poly Mod, Aux Mod, and Pressure Mod sections alongside the step sequencer, arpeggiator, and per-layer effects.

## Cherry Audio Trident Mk III Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Trident Mk III Synthesizer -->

Cherry Audio Trident Mk III suits producers and composers who want vintage synth, brass, and string textures without hunting for original 1980s hardware, as well as sound designers who want deep modulation and effects routing in a single multitimbral instrument. They can layer or split its Synth, Brass, and String sections across the keyboard, shape sounds with the dual arpeggiators and 16x2 step sequencer, and process them through its four customizable effects chains, drawing on the included preset library as a starting point.
