## Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ -->

Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ suits keyboardists and producers working in blues, gospel, rock, and jazz styles who want the classic tonewheel sound alongside modern studio tools. They can use its Main, Edit, Cabinet, and Perform sections to dial in drawbar settings, percussion, and overdrive, then shape the tone further with the built-in tape echo delay, reverb, and seven-band graphic EQ.

## Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B -->

Wurlybird 140B suits pianists and producers working in classic rock and pop styles who want the distinctive electric piano tone heard on recordings by artists like Ray Charles, Queen, and Supertramp. They can load it as an AU, VST, VST3, AAX, or standalone instrument and shape the sound using the Vibrato, Tune, Hum, and Velocity Curve controls along with the onboard stompbox-style effects and 50 included presets.

## Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine -->

Cherry Audio's KR-55C suits producers drawn to post-punk and new wave drum sounds who want deep sound-shaping control, since it combines 240 factory patterns from the original KR-55A and KR-55B with 200 new patterns and kits, plus per-instrument EQ, tuning, and decay editing. They can program rhythms in real time or step-by-step on the 16-button X0X-style layout, chain up to 99 patterns in Song Mode, shape the mix with the per-instrument effects routing matrix (overdrive, flanger/chorus, delay, reverb), and route finished patterns straight into a DAW via drag-and-drop MIDI export, individual outs, and full automation.

## Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit -->

Crumar Spirit suits producers and sound designers who want a monophonic analog synth with deep modulation and multiple voicing modes, offering unison, polyphonic, and cycle voice modes alongside extensive routing through the Mod X, Shaper Y, and Matrix Z sections. They can use its multi-voice panels and quick-access presets to add per-note variation and subtle-to-extreme detuning, while leaning on the over 430 included presets, built-in arpeggiator, and dual effects chains to shape everything from vocal-like filtered tones to drones and complex triggered patterns.

## Cherry Audio SH MAX
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio SH MAX -->

Cherry Audio SH-MAX suits producers who want one plugin covering classic SH-series synth tones (SH-5, SH-7, SH-3A) for basslines, leads, and layered chords, whether playing mono, duophonic, unison, or full 16-voice polyphony. They can route any of its five sound sources through the dual filter sections and four independent effects chains, then shape sequences on the built-in step sequencer, which syncs to a DAW and can output MIDI directly to the timeline.

## Cherry Audio P 10 Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio P 10 Synthesizer -->

P-10 suits players who already know the classic dual-manual analog polysynth and want its layered/split architecture, modeled Curtis 3340 oscillators and 3320 filters, and factory P5/P10 presets in a plugin. They can configure it as two independent synths, one combined ten-voice synth, or a layered/split keyboard, then shape sounds using the Mono Mod, Poly Mod, Aux Mod, and Pressure Mod sections alongside the step sequencer, arpeggiator, and per-layer effects.

## Cherry Audio Trident Mk III Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Trident Mk III Synthesizer -->

Cherry Audio Trident Mk III suits producers and composers who want vintage synth, brass, and string textures without hunting for original 1980s hardware, as well as sound designers who want deep modulation and effects routing in a single multitimbral instrument. They can layer or split its Synth, Brass, and String sections across the keyboard, shape sounds with the dual arpeggiators and 16x2 step sequencer, and process them through its four customizable effects chains, drawing on the included preset library as a starting point.
