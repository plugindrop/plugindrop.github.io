This lineup brings together Waves plugins that cover vocal leveling, compression, EQ, reverb, and tape saturation, spanning everyday mixing tasks through to mastering. The tools address vocals, bass, guitar, and drums, with several modeled on classic studio hardware such as vintage tube and optical compressors, passive EQs, and tape machines, alongside reverb designs drawn from classic plate and algorithmic technology.

## Waves Vocal Rider
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves Vocal Rider -->

Vocal Rider suits vocalists, podcasters, and video narrators who want consistent levels without the tonal coloring that heavy compression can cause. Users set a target level in the plugin and it automatically rides the gain up or down in real time, writing an automation track they can fine-tune afterward in the studio or during live mixing.

## Waves Abbey Road Reverb Plates
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves Abbey Road Reverb Plates -->

Abbey Road Reverb Plates suits mixing and mastering engineers working on pop, film score, or classical projects who want a bright, diffused plate reverb for vocals and instruments, including high-diffusion smoothing on drums and percussion. Users can dial in everything from short, snappy tones to huge, lush decays using the variable decay times, pre-delay, EQ, crosstalk, and drive controls, choosing among all four modeled Abbey Road Studios plates to match the source material.

## Waves API 2500
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves API 2500 -->

Producers working on hip hop, pop/R&B, and rock who want a single compressor that covers drum bus glue through full mix bus duty will find the API 2500's VCA-style flexibility a good fit. They can switch between feedback and feedforward topologies, dial in Thrust for bass shaping, and use the variable L/R linking and knee options to tailor punch and tone to the track.

## Waves Kramer Master Tape
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves Kramer Master Tape -->

Kramer Master Tape suits rock-oriented engineers chasing the push-into-distortion compression and tube warmth of early analog tape machines, especially on individual tracks or full masters. They can dial in that character using the speed, bias, and flux controls, add wow, flutter, and hiss for extra authenticity, and apply the tube saturation to warm up harsh-sounding instruments, with a built-in tape delay (up to 500ms) offering time, feedback, and filter controls for further shaping.

## Waves PuigTec EQs
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves PuigTec EQs -->

This plugin suits mixing and mastering engineers working on vocals, bass, guitar, and other sources who want a gentle, transparent, passive-style tone-shaping tool rather than an aggressive modern EQ. They can use the EQP-1A to boost and cut the same low or high frequencies simultaneously for punchy, tight bass and drums, and turn to the MEQ-5's three midrange bands to sculpt vocals, guitars, and synths during mixing or mastering.

## Waves Renaissance Reverb
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves Renaissance Reverb -->

Renaissance Reverb suits producers and mix engineers who want a flexible, classic-style algorithmic reverb with a wide range of spaces rather than a single emulated room. They can pick from the plugin's 12 reverb types (halls, chambers, plates, and more), shape the tail with the dual-band EQ and damping controls, and start from any of the 265 presets before fine-tuning with the standard reverb parameters.

## Waves Renaissance Vox
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves Renaissance Vox -->

Ideal for singers, voiceover artists, and podcast creators who want a fast, reliable vocal compressor without digging through complex menus. Simply adjust the Gate, Compression, and Output Gain controls while watching the smart metering to dial in a polished, present vocal sound, and draw on the included presets as starting points.

## Waves CLA-2A
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves CLA-2A -->

Waves CLA-2A suits engineers mixing vocals, bass, or guitar who want the character of a classic electro-optical tube compressor without hunting down vintage hardware. Users dial in the attack/release-free interface with Trim, Mix, and an optional Limiter mode, or start from the included Chris Lord-Alge presets for vocals, guitar, bass, and drums, then blend in parallel compression or sidechain filtering as needed.

## Waves CLA-76
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves CLA-76 -->

This plugin suits mix engineers and producers who want a fast, aggressive FET-style compressor for tracking drum dynamics and leveling vocals, rather than the smoother sound of optical compressors. Users can apply its "Bluey" or "Blacky" tonal models with the super-fast attack and ALL ratio mode for punchy drum and vocal control, use the Mix knob for parallel compression, or load Chris Lord-Alge's presets as a starting point, all with zero added latency.

## Waves H-Reverb
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Waves H-Reverb -->

Waves H-Reverb suits producers and mix engineers who want to move beyond a single reverb character and shape everything from natural rooms and halls to unconventional, non-linear spaces. They can use its blend of algorithmic and convolution technology alongside built-in EQ, compression, modulation, and pre/post delay to sculpt reverb tails precisely, or start from one of its 240-plus presets and adjust from there.
