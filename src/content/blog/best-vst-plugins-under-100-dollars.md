## Reverb

### Valhalla VintageVerb

- **Developer:** Valhalla DSP
- **Price:** $50 USD (vendor page, 2026-10-01)

Valhalla's product page says VintageVerb "features 22 classic digital reverbs and 3 color modes inspired by the most beloved reverb hardware from the 1970s and 1980s." The 1970s, 1980s and Now color settings are listed on the same page.

[Valhalla VintageVerb on valhalladsp.com](https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-vintage-verb/)

## Delay

### Native Instruments Replika XT

- **Developer:** Native Instruments
- **Price:** $69.00 (vendor page, 2026-10-01)
- **Formats:** Windows: VST, VST3, AAX; macOS: VST, VST3, AU, AAX

Native Instruments describes Replika XT as "a creative multi-mode delay" with five delay modes. The page names Modern ("clean, precise, controllable repeats") and Vintage among them, and says the plugin includes seven time-based and pitch effects that can be added to the repeats.

[Replika XT on native-instruments.com](https://www.native-instruments.com/en/products/komplete/effects/replika-xt/)

## Modular Effects

### Kilohearts Snap Heap

- **Developer:** Kilohearts
- **Price:** $29 (vendor page, 2026-10-01); the page also offers a 10-day trial

Kilohearts describes Snap Heap as "a modular Snapin Host which allows you to build up to seven serial or parallel effects chains and modulate every parameter" with its modular modulation system.

[Snap Heap on kilohearts.com](https://kilohearts.com/products/snap_heap)

## Audio Repair

### iZotope RX 12 Elements

- **Developer:** iZotope
- **Price:** $99.00 (vendor page, 2026-09-28)

RX 12 Elements is the entry version of iZotope's RX audio repair software. iZotope's product page lists Windows 10 (22H2) and Windows 11 (24H2) as supported Windows versions.
