## Short Answer

Waves runs **flash sales nearly every week of the year**. The typical discount is **90–96% off**, which means the "$999" Waves Platinum Bundle almost never actually costs $999 — it typically sells for **$69–$99**, with historical lows around **$73**. If you're staring at a Waves plugin at full listed price right now, the practical advice is simple: **don't buy it yet.** Wait a few days. A sale is coming.

This isn't a once-a-year Black Friday thing either — Waves has run some version of a sitewide sale continuously for over a decade. The "regular price" on their site functions more like a reference point than an actual price anyone pays.

## When to Buy Waves Plugins: Season-by-Season Guide

Because Waves discounts are near-continuous, the "when to buy" question is less about calendar timing and more about **which sale gets you the lowest number**:

- **Black Friday / Cyber Monday (late November)** — historically the deepest cuts (93%+), and the best shot at the $73 floor price on bundles.
- **NAMM season (January)** — Waves typically runs a "New Year" promo alongside new product launches, often 90% off across the catalog.
- **Spring and Summer flash sales (roughly monthly)** — smaller in marketing push but usually land in the same 90% off range as Black Friday, just without the hype.
- **Random Tuesday** — genuinely, Waves runs unadvertised flash sales throughout the year that show up in your inbox if you're on their mailing list. These are frequently just as good as the "big" sales.

## Should You Buy the Bundle or Individual Plugins?

This is the actual decision most people are wrestling with, so it's worth being direct about it:

- **Buy the Platinum Bundle** if you're a working mixer/mastering engineer who wants broad coverage (EQ, compression, reverb, restoration) and doesn't want to shop plugin-by-plugin. At $79–$99, it's cheap enough that "will I use all of it" barely matters.
- **Buy individual plugins** (like CLA Vocals or the SSL Collection) if you already own a strong core toolkit and just want one or two specific classic emulations. At $19–$49 per plugin during sales, there's little reason to overpay for a bundle you won't fully use.
- **Skip Waves entirely** if you need modern, CPU-efficient, actively-updated tools with cleaner UIs — Waves' codebase and interface design lag behind newer competitors, even if the DSP quality holds up.

## When to Buy

**Buy now if:**
- Waves is already showing 90%+ off (check the live price before assuming — this is true most of the year, but not literally every day)
- You're buying during Black Friday/Cyber Monday or the January NAMM window, when the deepest discounts (93%+) tend to land
- The bundle or plugin you want is already under $30–$99 depending on tier — that's within the normal sale range and unlikely to drop dramatically further

**Wait if:**
- You're looking at anything close to full list price ($999, $299, etc.) — this almost never holds for more than a few days
- Black Friday is within 4–6 weeks — historically the single best shot at the absolute floor price (~$73 on Platinum)
- You're not in a rush and can tolerate checking back weekly — given the frequency of flash sales, patience rarely costs you more than a week or two

The bottom line: with Waves, the question isn't really "when do they go on sale" — it's "how low will this specific sale go." Track the price for a week before buying, and you'll almost never pay more than $99 for a bundle that lists at $999.
