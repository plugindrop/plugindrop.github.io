## What you actually get

SSL lists this as a fully parametric 24-band mastering-grade EQ, which gives you far more bands than a typical channel strip EQ for detailed tonal shaping. According to the product listing, it ships with 17 different EQ types and filter shapes, so you can switch between surgical notches and musical shelving curves without leaving the plugin.

The product page also notes anti-cramping algorithms designed to preserve high-frequency detail that can otherwise get lost in a digital signal path. SSL lists mid-side and independent left/right processing, which matters for stereo-width decisions late in a mix or at mastering.

A few more specifics worth knowing:

- Band soloing lets you isolate and pinpoint problem frequencies before you touch the gain.
- Real-time visual frequency analysis shows what's happening across the spectrum as you work.
- SSL lists DSP optimization aimed at light, efficient CPU use.

## Where it fits in a session

This is an EQ built for precision work, not creative sound design. It fits naturally on the master bus during mastering, where the 24-band count and anti-cramping behavior help with fine tonal correction.

It also works on busy mix buses or stem groups, where mid-side control lets you narrow or widen specific frequency ranges independently. Band soloing makes it useful earlier too, for hunting resonances on individual tracks before a mix gets printed.
