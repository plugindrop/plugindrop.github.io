**Short answer:** Valhalla DSP **never goes on sale.** The price has always been **$50** and there are no plans to change that.

---

## Why Valhalla DSP Has No Sales

Sean Costello (founder of Valhalla DSP) has made a public commitment to permanent $50 pricing for all Valhalla plugins. This is a philosophical choice: he believes professional-quality tools should be accessible without artificial sale cycles. The $50 price IS the deal — these plugins compete with reverbs costing 5-10x more.

Valhalla Supermassive is completely free and an excellent starting point. If you love it, the $50 for VintageVerb or Room is easy to justify without waiting for a sale.

## Should You Just Buy It at Full Price?

At **$50**, here's how to think about it:

- **Buy now** if you actively need it for your current work
- **Try the free alternatives first** if you're still exploring
- **Accept the price** — waiting will not get you a better deal


**Buy direct from [Valhalla DSP](https://www.google.com/search?q=Valhalla+DSP)** — $50 is the only available price.


## Free Alternatives Worth Considering

Since Valhalla DSP never goes on sale:

- **[Valhalla Supermassive (FREE)](https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-supermassive/)** — Valhalla's own free plugin — excellent for ambient and large-space reverbs
- **[TAL-Reverb-4](https://tal-software.com/products/tal-reverb-4)** — Free plate-style reverb with a warm, vintage character
- **[Dragonfly Reverb](https://michaelwillis.github.io/dragonfly-reverb/)** — Free open-source reverb with hall, room, and plate modes
