## What Is Scaler 3?

Scaler 3 is a music theory plugin developed by Plugin Boutique and Scaler Music, designed to help producers find chords, scales, and progressions that work together musically. It functions as both an educational tool and a creative compositional aid, sitting inside your DAW as a VST/AU/AAX instrument or MIDI effect. Whether you're stuck on a chord progression or trying to understand why a melody isn't clicking, Scaler 3 aims to bridge the gap between music theory and practical production.

---

## Key Features

- **Chord Detection** — Scaler 3 listens to audio or MIDI input and identifies the chords and scales being played in real time.
- **Scale & Mode Library** — A large built-in library of scales, modes, and tonalities to explore, from common major/minor to more exotic options.
- **Chord Progressions & Suggestions** — Generates contextually relevant chord progressions based on your chosen key and scale, with one-click variations.
- **Expressions & Performances** — Pre-built rhythmic and melodic patterns that can be applied to any chord set, adding movement and groove automatically.
- **MIDI Drag & Drop** — Export chord patterns and progressions directly into your DAW timeline via simple drag and drop.
- **Artist & Genre Presets** — Curated sets of progressions and sounds inspired by specific styles and genres, useful for quickly setting a mood or direction.
