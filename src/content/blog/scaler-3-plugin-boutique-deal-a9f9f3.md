## What Is Scaler 3?

Scaler 3 is a music theory plugin by Plugin Boutique and Scaler Music, designed to help producers find chords, scales, and progressions that work together musically. It sits inside your DAW as a VST/AU/AAX instrument or MIDI effect, bridging the gap between music theory and practical production.

---

## Key Features

- **Chord Detection** — Listens to audio or MIDI and identifies chords and scales in real time.
- **Scale & Mode Library** — Large built-in library covering common to exotic scales and modes.
- **Chord Progressions & Suggestions** — Contextually relevant progressions with one-click variations.
- **Expressions & Performances** — Pre-built rhythmic and melodic patterns applied to any chord set.
- **MIDI Drag & Drop** — Export chord patterns directly into your DAW timeline.
- **Artist & Genre Presets** — Curated progressions inspired by specific styles and genres.
