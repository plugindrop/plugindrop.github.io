## What's Actually In the Bundle

The SSL 4000 Bundle is Waves' emulation of the Solid State Logic 4000 series console — the desk behind a huge share of classic rock, pop, and hip-hop mixes. The bundle typically includes:

- **SSL E-Channel** — the classic 4000 E-series channel strip (EQ + dynamics)
- **SSL G-Master Buss Compressor** — the "glue" compressor found on countless mix busses
- **SSL G-Channel** — the later G-series channel strip variant
- **SSL 4000 Collection** extras (routing/summing tools, depending on bundle version)

For mixers who want that console-glue sound without buying outboard SSL hardware, this is one of the most-referenced software approximations in the mixing community — not because of marketing, but because the underlying circuits (E and G series channel strips, G-Bus compressor) are genuinely industry-standard reference points.

## How the SSL 4000 Bundle Compares

If you're weighing this against the free-standing alternative, here's the practical tradeoff:
