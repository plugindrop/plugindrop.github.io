## FabFilter Pro-Q 4

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Pro-Q 4 -->

FabFilter Pro-Q 4 is an equalizer plugin that offers up to 24 bands of processing. What sets it apart is its Spectral dynamics feature, which allows for more precise handling of problem frequencies in a mix.

The plugin is built for mixing and mastering engineers, and it supports stereo, surround, and Dolby Atmos sessions. For mastering work, the 3 dB and 6 dB display ranges give a finer view of subtle adjustments, while the 12 dB and 30 dB ranges are better suited to the larger moves typical of mixing.

On the sound side, Spectral dynamics targets problem frequencies more precisely and efficiently than older approaches. The Dynamic EQ section includes Attack and Release controls along with an optional free side-chain filter, giving more control over how the dynamic processing reacts. The dynamic EQ processing itself has also been improved, resulting in less distortion and a cleaner overall sound across its zero latency, Natural Phase, and linear phase modes.

Pro-Q 4 sits alongside other EQ plugins such as BALANCE, Eventide SplitEQ, and SSL Native X-EQ 2. Price history shows a single tracked sale from a third-party retailer around Black Friday 2025, which does not indicate a regular or typical discount pattern from the plugin maker's own store.

## Native Instruments Kontakt 8

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Native Instruments Kontakt 8 -->

Native Instruments Kontakt 8 brings a full update to the long-running sampling platform, built around three new creative tools alongside the familiar sample engine. Conflux is the standout addition, blending organic recorded samples with synth-style oscillators—including wavetable, FM, and ring modulation—so you can reshape sounds in real time rather than just trigger fixed samples. Leap works as a loop and sample player with built-in manipulation controls, and it comes paired with twelve genre-specific expansion packs covering styles such as Afrobeats, Latin Trap, Platinum Pop, Progressive Trance, and Soul Gold, making it useful for sound design work as well as material aimed squarely at trap and pop production. Rounding out the toolset, the Chords and Phrases tools generate MIDI melodies and sequences with built-in randomization, giving you a quick way to sketch out musical ideas before committing to a full arrangement.

Underpinning all of this is the Kontakt 8 Factory Library 2, which now ships with more than 900 instruments, and the package continues to serve as a platform for the vast ecosystem of third-party sample-based instruments built on Kontakt. Hardware integration remains a core feature, with seamless support for Kontrol keyboards and Maschine controllers, and the instrument-building tools themselves have been improved for anyone creating their own custom instruments within the platform.

On the system side, Mac users need macOS 12, 13, or 14 with the latest update, running on an Intel Core i5 or equivalent, or an Apple Silicon Mac via Rosetta 2 or natively where supported; 4 GB of RAM is the minimum with 6 GB recommended. Windows users need Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with the latest service pack, and the same 4 GB minimum / 6 GB recommended RAM applies.
