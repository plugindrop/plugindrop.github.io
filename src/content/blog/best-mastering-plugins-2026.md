## FabFilter Saturn 2

FabFilter Saturn 2 is a multiband distortion and saturation plug-in covering a wide range of styles, from subtle, clean, warm tube or tape saturation through to wild multiband guitar amp effects and bit crushing. Multiband processing handles up to six bands, and each band has its own distortion style along with independent drive, mix, feedback, dynamics, tone, and level controls.

A modulation section adds envelope followers and other modulation sources, with a live visualization showing both the modulation targets and the sources driving them. This moves the plug-in beyond a straightforward mixing tool and into something that can also work as a creative sound-design effect.

Crossover slopes between bands can be adjusted, with 6, 12, 24, and 48 dB/octave options available. An optional linear phase mode applies to the crossover filtering, making the multiband processing better suited to mastering work, alongside newer, more subtle saturation styles aimed at the same kind of use. Two optional high-quality processing modes are offered: a "Good" mode that uses 8x oversampling, and a "Superb" mode that uses 32x oversampling to keep aliasing to an absolute minimum.

Newer Amplifier distortion styles are modeled after well-known American and British amps, while newer Transformer distortion styles range from subtle to warm. The interface features an interactive multiband display, and the number of XY controllers available was increased to six. Envelope followers also gained a transient-detection mode, turning them into transient detectors.

The plug-in comes in VST, VST3, CLAP, AU, AAX Native, and AudioSuite formats. On Mac, it runs on macOS 10.12 or higher, with support for Intel or Apple Silicon (M1) processors, 64-bit only. On Windows, it runs on Windows 11, 10, 8, 7, or Vista, in either 32-bit or 64-bit versions.

Band count tops out at six, and the crossover slope options remain fixed at four settings: 6, 12, 24, and 48 dB/octave.

## Sonnox Oxford EQ

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Sonnox Oxford EQ -->

Oxford EQ is a 5-band parametric EQ with variable-slope high-pass and low-pass filters, built around four completely different sounding EQ types. One of those types is a clinical EQ similar to the SSL4000 E series, with a tighter cut suited to removing problem frequencies. A second type is a classic EQ with curves similar to Neve and SSL G series consoles. A third type offers wide, gentle curves well suited to mastering work.

Because the high-pass and low-pass filters use a variable slope rather than a single fixed setting, the cut can be made as gentle or as steep as the material requires, rather than being limited to one fixed curve shape.

This range of EQ types makes the plugin useful both for corrective work, such as mix engineers cutting out problem frequencies, and for mastering passes that call for wide, gentle shaping across a whole mix.

On Mac, the plugin needs macOS 10.12 or later, works on both Intel and Apple Silicon (M1) machines, and is 64-bit only. It is available in AAX Native, AU, and VST3 formats, running in Pro Tools 11 or later or in any desktop application that supports Audio Unit or VST3 plugins.

On Windows, it needs Windows 10 or later and comes in AAX Native and VST3 formats, for use in Pro Tools 11 or later or any host supporting VST3 plugins. Licensing on Windows goes through iLok Cloud, which needs an active internet connection to work.

Comparable options in this space include Softube Trident A-Range, FabFilter Pro-Q 4, and BALANCE.

## FabFilter Pro-L 2

FabFilter Pro-L 2 is a true peak limiter built for mastering and mixing, combining several limiting styles in one plugin and aiming for transparent sound together with maximum loudness. It suits any type of music or audio, including mono, stereo, or surround material, and it is treated as an essential tool for engineers working on both mastering and mixing. External side-chain triggering is also included, which supports stem mastering workflows.

The plugin offers eight different limiting algorithms, each with its own character and purpose, so the overall sound can range from clean and transparent to punchier and more aggressive depending on the material being processed. Adjustable look-ahead, attack, and release settings shape how the limiting behaves, with look-ahead able to range from no delay up to about twenty milliseconds so the limiter can anticipate transients before they hit. True peak limiting keeps the final output from exceeding the chosen true peak ceiling, including inter-sample peaks, and oversampling runs as high as thirty-two times using a linear-phase process to keep that peak control accurate.

Metering is detailed and accurate, covering both output level and gain reduction, including true peak readings alongside momentary, short-term, and integrated loudness measurements built to standard broadcast and streaming loudness guidelines. An Audition Limiting mode lets a listener isolate just the gain change being applied, rather than hearing the full processed signal, which makes it easier to judge how hard the limiter is working. A real-time display tracks gain reduction over time so it's clear where and when limiting is happening. Surround and immersive formats are supported up to large multichannel Dolby Atmos layouts, with linking across surround channels, and an optional DC offset filter is also built in.

The plugin is available in common plugin formats for both Mac and PC hosts. On Mac, it runs on macOS 10.12 or higher, covering both Intel and Apple Silicon (M1) processors, 64-bit only. On Windows, it supports Windows 11, 10, 8, 7, or Vista, in either 32-bit or 64-bit versions.

## How to choose

TDR Nova is a parallel dynamic equalizer with four dynamic EQ bands plus high-pass and low-pass filter sections. Each band can work as a static filter or as a frequency-dependent compressor or expander. Equal loudness functions make it easier to judge a setting without being thrown off by loudness differences. Another mode is built for mastering, intended to blend and balance entire tracks. The high and low filters have an adjustable slope ranging from 6 to 36 dB per octave.

FabFilter Pro-L 2 is a true peak limiter. It offers eight limiting algorithms and up to 32x oversampling. Loudness metering follows the ITU-R BS.1770-4 and EBU R128 standards. Surround and immersive support reaches 9.1.6 Dolby Atmos.
