## What Is the SSL 4000 E Channel Strip?

The SSL 4000 E Channel Strip is a plugin modeled on the channel strip circuitry of Solid State Logic's 4000 E series analog console. It combines EQ, compression, and gate/expander sections in a single processor built for tracking, mixing, and bus processing. Solid State Logic has built its reputation on the 4000 series consoles, which were installed in commercial studios worldwide starting in the 1980s. The plugin brings that console's channel path into a software format for use inside a standard DAW session.

## Key Features

- **Console-style EQ** — a parametric EQ section based on the 4000 E channel EQ, used for shaping tonal balance on individual tracks or groups.
- **Compressor** — a channel compressor modeled on the console's dynamics section, suited to controlling peaks on vocals, drums, and bus channels.
- **Gate/Expander** — a noise gate and expander stage for cleaning up bleed or tightening drum and vocal tracks.
- **High-pass and low-pass filters** — dedicated filters for trimming low-end rumble or taming harsh top end before the EQ stage.
- **Console-modeled signal path** — the overall channel strip follows the routing order of the original hardware, so the EQ, dynamics, and filters interact the way they do on the console.
