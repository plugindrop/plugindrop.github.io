## What Is JUPITER-8?

JUPITER-8 is a software synthesizer built around the sound and architecture of the original Roland Jupiter-8, the eight-voice analog polysynth from 1981 that shaped the sound of 1980s pop, film scores, and synth-pop. It's licensed by Roland and modeled on the hardware unit's dual-oscillator voice architecture, cross-modulation options, and analog-style filter. The plugin is aimed at producers who want that specific vintage character in a modern DAW workflow rather than sourcing and maintaining the original hardware.
