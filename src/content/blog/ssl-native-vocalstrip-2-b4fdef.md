## What Is SSL Native Vocalstrip 2?

SSL Native Vocalstrip 2 is a dedicated vocal processing plugin from Solid State Logic — the British console manufacturer behind some of the most iconic mixing desks in recording history. It packages a full channel-strip signal chain specifically tuned for the human voice, drawing on SSL's hardware processing heritage. Rather than a generic channel strip, every module in Vocalstrip 2 is designed around the particular dynamics, frequency range, and transient character of vocal recordings.

---

## Key Features

- **De-Esser** — A frequency-conscious sibilance control module designed to tame harsh high-frequency artifacts without dulling the vocal presence.
- **Gate / Expander** — Cleans up noise and bleed between phrases, with expander mode for more transparent attenuation.
- **Compressor** — SSL-flavored dynamic control with the characteristic density and glue the brand is known for, calibrated for vocal material.
- **Equalizer** — A purpose-built EQ section with bands shaped for vocal enhancement — cut muddiness, add air, and dial in presence.
- **Transient Shaper** — Adds control over the attack and sustain of vocal consonants and phrases for extra definition.
- **Modular Signal Chain** — Modules can be reordered within the strip, letting producers customize processing order to fit their workflow.
