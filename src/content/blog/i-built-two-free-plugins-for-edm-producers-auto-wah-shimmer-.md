## What Are Satin Funk and Shimmer?

Satin Funk is an envelope filter built around two filter stages working together. A Moog-style ladder filter handles the low end for warmth and body, while a state-variable vowel-peak filter sweeps across the formant range. Together they cover a range from a smooth, buttery auto-wah to a sharper, Mu-Tron-style quack.
