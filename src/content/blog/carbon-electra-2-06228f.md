## What you actually get

Carbon Electra 2 is a synthesizer plugin built around scale-aware sound design. According to the product listing, several core systems lock to musical scale logic rather than raw pitch or time values:

- **Scale Lock oscillators** — oscillators can lock to scale degrees instead of fixed pitch values, so patches stay in key as you play.
- **Scale-locked sequencer** — the built-in sequencer can lock to diatonic notes and chords within the selected scale, useful for generating melodic ideas that don't need manual quantizing.
- **Musical filter lock** — the filter can track scale notes, incoming chord tones, or interval relationships, tying timbral movement to harmony rather than a fixed cutoff curve.
- **Wave Terrain oscillator mode** — a new topographic, terrain-style oscillator mode that the listing describes as an alternative to wavetable synthesis.
- **Custom terrain images** — producers can upload their own .png images to shape oscillator behavior, with control over edge interaction in 3D and terrain size and movement.
- The plugin also retains classic analogue waveforms and wavetable oscillator modes alongside the new terrain engine.

## Where it fits in a session

The scale-lock features are most useful at the sketching stage, when you want melodic or chordal ideas that stay harmonically coherent without stopping to quantize. The sequencer's chord-and-scale awareness suits writing hooks or basslines that need to lock to a progression already running in the session. Wave Terrain oscillators are better suited to sound design passes — building evolving pads, textures, or risers where terrain movement, not a static waveform, drives the tone over time.
