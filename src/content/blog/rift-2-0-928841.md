## What Is Rift 2.0?

Rift 2.0 is a multi-engine distortion plugin developed by **Minimal Audio**. It's built for producers and sound designers who want precise, creative control over distortion — from subtle harmonic saturation through to full-on signal destruction. Version 2.0 expanded on the original with additional engines, modulation capabilities, and deeper processing options, positioning it as a versatile tool for both mixing and sound design contexts.

---

## Key Features

- **Multiple Distortion Engines** — Rift 2.0 offers a range of distortion types, giving users access to different sonic characters from a single plugin.
- **Built-in Modulation** — Integrated modulation routing lets you animate the distortion parameters directly, without needing external LFO plugins.
- **Pre and Post Filtering** — Filters on both sides of the distortion stage give you tonal control over what frequencies enter and exit the processing chain.
- **Mid/Side Processing** — Allows independent distortion of the mid and side signals, useful for stereo shaping on buses and mix elements.
- **Visual Feedback** — A real-time display helps you monitor how the signal is being shaped, which is valuable when dialing in subtle settings.
- **Modular Signal Flow** — The signal routing is designed to be flexible, letting you adapt the plugin to different processing scenarios.

## Who Is It For?

- **Electronic music producers** — Rift 2.0's modulation features suit synth-heavy genres like techno, bass music, and experimental electronic where animated distortion textures are common.

- **Mix engineers** — Pre/post filtering and M/S processing give mixing engineers the surgical control needed when applying distortion to individual tracks or stems.
