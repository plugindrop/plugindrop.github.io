**TL;DR:** Track temporary price drops for 20 plugins and see [our evergreen comparisons](/best/synth/) for longer-term research.

This season's drum machines and synths lean heavily into vintage revival, with virtual recreations of classic hardware from Roland, Korg, Ensoniq, ARP, and Crumar sitting alongside purpose-built bass and sub-heavy synths. From faithful circuit modeling to expanded polyphony and modern effects chains, each one updates a distinct piece of synthesizer history for today's producers.

## SubLab XL
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:SubLab XL -->

SubLab XL suits producers working on macOS (10.12+, Intel or Apple Silicon) or Windows 8 and up who want a bass synth that runs natively in their DAW. It's available as a VST2, VST3, AU, or AAX plugin, so users can load it directly into any host supporting those formats on their platform.

## Drumazon 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Drumazon 2 -->

Drumazon 2 suits producers who want an accurately modeled 909 drum machine with the flexibility to dial in a modern, punchy edge, since it recreates each original instrument individually and layers on per-instrument EQ and compression. Users can build a kit quickly from the included factory presets, shape the sound further with the bitcrusher, distortion, reverb, delay, chorus, and filter options, program rhythms with the velocity, articulation, tap, and randomizer features of its sequencer, and finish the mix using the master section's 3-band compressor and limiter with ready-to-use master presets.

## Cherry Audio Atomika
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Atomika -->

Cherry Audio Atomika suits producers and synth enthusiasts drawn to rare, gritty Soviet-era analog character who still want modern studio flexibility, since it emulates the Polivoks' distinctive programmable op-amp filter while adding 16-voice polyphony, unison, and expanded modulation. They can play it as a flexible virtual analog synth across AU, VST, VST3, or AAX hosts, shaping sounds with the Starve, Filter Drive, and Amp Drive controls, switching between lowpass, highpass, notch, bandpass, and peak filter modes, and building on over 350 presets using the arpeggiator and onboard Phasor, Flange/Chorus, Echo, and Reverb effects.

## Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio KR 55C Drum Machine -->

Cherry Audio's KR-55C suits producers and sound designers drawn to post-punk and early-80s drum machine character who want a wider sonic range than the original hardware offered, since it combines all 240 factory patterns from the KR-55A and KR-55B with 200 additional patterns and kits spanning other genres. Users can step-edit or program patterns in real time via the X0X-style 16-button layout, chain up to 99 patterns in song mode, shape each instrument's tone with the EQ and tuning controls, and process sounds individually through the mixer and effects section, which includes overdrive, flanger/chorus, delay, reverb, and a master bus compressor and limiter.

## Cherry Audio Yellowjacket
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Yellowjacket -->

Yellowjacket suits producers chasing the buzzy, quirky tone of the 1978 EDP Wasp but who want modern flexibility, since Cherry Audio modeled the original's oscillators, filter, and touch-capacitive keyboard while adding unison, 16-voice polyphony, and an advanced arpeggiator with probability and humanise controls. They can build lush, evolving textures or chained-synth effects using the Multi voice modes, which add adjustable "mini synths" for per-note variation, single-fingered chords, arpeggiated patterns, and stereo width.

## Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit -->

Crumar Spirit suits musicians and sound designers who want an analog monosynth with modern flexibility, offering multi-voice modes, extensive modulation, and built-in effects for everything from classic monophonic leads to richer layered textures. Users can play it monophonically or switch into unison, polyphonic (4, 8, or 16 voice), or cycle modes, then shape sounds further with the dual-filter section, Mod X/Shaper Y modulation, the Matrix Z mod matrix, the arpeggiator, and the two independent effects chains, drawing on over 430 included presets as a starting point.

## Cherry Audio DS 2 Synthesizer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio DS 2 Synthesizer -->

Cherry Audio DS-2 suits producers chasing the gritty, harmonically edgy character of vintage hybrid synths rather than smooth, polished digital tones or warm analog warmth, since it recreates the original 1978 Crumar DS-2's distinctive sound. They can layer or split its monophonic Synth section (with unison, multi-voice, and up to 16-voice polyphony) against the now fully polyphonic 32-note Poly section, shaping timbres further with the expanded LFOs, arpeggiator, and the three independent effects chains built from its 20 included effects.

<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments FM8 -->

<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments Massive -->
