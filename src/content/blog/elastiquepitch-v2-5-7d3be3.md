## What Is ElastiquePitch V2.5?

ElastiquePitch V2.5 is a real-time pitch shifting and correction plugin from zplane.development, the company behind the élastique time-stretching and pitch-shifting engine. That same élastique technology is licensed by major DAW makers, including Ableton and Steinberg, for their own built-in time-stretch and warp tools. ElastiquePitch packages that engine into a standalone effect for pitch correction, vocal tuning, and creative pitch manipulation inside your DAW of choice.

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Pitch Shifting** — Built on zplane's élastique engine, letting you shift pitch on vocals or instruments during playback without offline rendering.
- **Formant Control** — Designed to keep vocal character natural when pitch is moved up or down, rather than producing the "chipmunk" or "helium" effect of naive pitch shifting.
- **Plugin Format Support** — Runs as a standard effect plugin inside compatible DAWs; check the product page for the exact list of supported formats (VST/AU/AAX) and host compatibility.
- **Low-Latency Processing** — Built for real-time use during tracking, mixing, or live performance rather than only offline processing.
- **zplane Pedigree** — Comes from the developer whose élastique algorithm underpins pitch and time tools in several major commercial DAWs, which is a useful reference point for the underlying technology.
