## What Is SynthMaster One?

SynthMaster One is a wavetable and VA (virtual analog) synthesizer developed by KV331 Audio. It sits in the SynthMaster family as a more streamlined instrument compared to its sibling, SynthMaster 2 — designed to give producers a powerful sound engine without the steeper learning curve of a fully modular workflow. KV331 Audio is a Turkish indie developer with a strong track record in the plugin space, and SynthMaster One has been a consistent catalog offering across Windows and Mac platforms.

## Key Features

- **Wavetable + VA Oscillators** — Dual oscillators support both wavetable scanning and classic virtual analog waveforms, covering a wide tonal range from modern leads to vintage basses.
- **Semi-Modular Routing** — A flexible signal path lets you route oscillators, filters, and effects in multiple configurations without building a full modular patch from scratch.
- **Extensive Modulation Matrix** — Multiple LFOs, envelopes, and modulation sources can be assigned to nearly any parameter for evolving, expressive sound design.
- **Built-In Effects Chain** — Onboard effects including chorus, delay, reverb, and distortion reduce the need for external processing on synth patches.
- **Large Factory Preset Library** — Ships with a substantial collection of presets covering a broad range of genres and use cases; check the product page for the current preset count.
- **VST / VST3 / AU / AAX Support** — Compatible with all major DAWs on both Windows and Mac.
