## What Is the Yamaha PSR-SX720+?

The Yamaha PSR-SX720+ is an arranger workstation from Yamaha's PSR-SX series — a line aimed squarely at performers, hobbyists, and producers who want a self-contained instrument with rich onboard accompaniment. The "+" designation signals a meaningful hardware refresh: Bluetooth Audio connectivity is now built in, letting you stream audio wirelessly to and from compatible devices without dongle setups or extra cables. Yamaha is one of the longest-standing names in arranger keyboards, and the SX series sits in the mid-range sweet spot of their lineup — above entry-level PSR models, below the flagship Genos tier.

## Key Features

- **Bluetooth Audio:** The standout new addition — wirelessly stream audio from your smartphone or tablet directly through the keyboard's speaker system.
- **Style-based Accompaniment Engine:** Access a library of auto-accompaniment styles spanning genres from jazz and pop to world music, each with multi-part band arrangements.
- **Onboard Voices:** Yamaha's SX series is built on their Super Articulation and CFX/Bösendorfer-sampled piano voices — check the official product page for the exact voice count.
- **USB Audio/MIDI:** Connect directly to a DAW or computer for recording and playback without additional audio interfaces.
- **Built-in Speakers:** The SX720+ ships with integrated stereo speakers, making it stage and living-room ready out of the box.
- **Registration Memory:** Save and recall panel settings for quick live performance switching — useful for gigging performers managing multiple sounds per set.
