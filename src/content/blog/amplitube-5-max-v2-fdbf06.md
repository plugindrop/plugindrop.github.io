## What Is AmpliTube 5 MAX v2?

AmpliTube 5 MAX v2 is the top-tier edition of IK Multimedia's AmpliTube 5 guitar and bass amp simulation platform. It bundles the full collection of amps, cabinets, effects, and studio gear available in the AmpliTube 5 ecosystem into a single package. Designed for guitarists, producers, and audio engineers, it runs both as a standalone application and as a plugin (VST, AU, AAX) inside any major DAW.

## Key Features

- **Extensive Amp & Cabinet Library:** The MAX tier includes a large collection of amp heads and cabinet models spanning classic and modern tones — check the product page for the current full count.
- **Cab Room with Advanced Speaker Simulation:** A dedicated virtual cabinet room lets you position microphones and blend multiple mics for realistic cabinet response.
- **Multi-Chain Signal Routing:** Build complex pedalboard and rack signal chains with flexible parallel and series routing options.
- **Integrated Effects Suite:** Covers the full signal chain — wah, compressor, overdrive, delay, reverb, and more — without leaving the plugin.
- **Custom Shop Expansion:** Access IK Multimedia's Custom Shop directly within the plugin to expand your library with additional individual gear models.
- **Standalone + Plugin Modes:** Works independently from a DAW for live practice or direct recording, as well as inside your session as a standard plugin format.
