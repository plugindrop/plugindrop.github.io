## What you actually get

Uhbik 2 bundles five processors built for mixing and sound design work. According to the product listing, the Delay module runs five virtual tape heads with per-tap feedback and tone-shaping filters, which makes it useful for building rhythmic, evolving echo patterns rather than a single fixed repeat.

- u-he lists a Flanger that simulates dual tape machines or BBD circuits per channel, covering both classic flange sweeps and lush chorus tones.
- The Phaser uses up to 42 allpass filters, giving control over comb frequency, feedback, and modulation depth for dense phasing.
- A Shifter (frequency shifter) is listed for pushing sounds from subtle chorus-like movement into more disorienting, pitched textures.
- The Tremolo goes beyond basic volume wobble, with modulation of volume, tone, and stereo position for rhythmic gating effects.

Each module is built as a standalone processor, so producers can load only the one they need instead of opening a full channel strip.

## Where it fits in a session

The Delay and Flanger modules suit mix-bus or send-channel duty, where tape-style modulation adds movement to vocals, guitars, or synth pads without extra plugins. The Phaser and Shifter fit sound-design passes on synths or risers, where allpass stacking or frequency shifting can create transitions that a stock phaser or pitch shifter can't match. The Tremolo is positioned for rhythmic work on groove elements, since stereo and tone modulation go further than a simple amplitude LFO.
