## What Is Mercuriall Audio Spark?

Spark is a guitar amplifier simulation plugin from Mercuriall Audio Software, built on their Ampbox engine. It models the legendary Marshall JCM800 2204 — a 50-watt master volume amp that defined rock tone for decades. The plugin delivers everything from edge-of-breakup bite to full-on snarling crunch, complete with that unmistakable upper-mid growl Marshall amps are known for. It runs as a VST, AU, and AAX plugin.

## Key Features

- **JCM800 2204 Amplifier Model** — Faithful emulation of the classic 50W Marshall, covering clean sparkle through high-gain crunch territory.
- **Tube Push-Pull Power Amp with Reactive Load** — Models the interaction between power amp tubes and guitar cabinet for realistic power amp saturation and Presence knob behavior.
- **#34 Mod Option** — A refined hot-rodded voicing inspired by Slash's modded JCM800 era for tighter, more focused gain.
- **#36 Mod Option** — Thicker, more driven gain structure with enhanced saturation and sustain for heavier tones.
- **Preamp Tube Selection** — Choose between 12AX7JJ or 12AX7WC preamp tubes to shape your tone character.
- **Switchable Power Amp Feedback Circuit** — Increased bass resonance depth and smoothed high frequencies for tonal flexibility.
