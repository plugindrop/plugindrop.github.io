## What Is Unfiltered Audio Battalion?

Unfiltered Audio Battalion is a drum machine synthesizer plugin developed by Unfiltered Audio — a company known in the production community for building tools with unusually deep modulation architectures. Rather than functioning as a straightforward sample player, Battalion is built around synthesis-first drum sound generation paired with an integrated sequencer. Unfiltered Audio's broader catalog (including BYOME and Indent 2) has established the brand as one that pushes past conventional plugin design, and Battalion fits squarely within that philosophy.

## Key Features

- **Synthesis-based drum voice generation** — Drum sounds built through synthesis engines, not just sample playback, enabling original textures that deviate from stock library sounds.
- **Integrated step sequencer** — Pattern creation happens inside the plugin itself, streamlining the workflow from sound design to beat construction.
- **Per-voice modulation routing** — Modulation can be applied at the individual drum voice level, producing evolving, expressive rhythms rather than static hits.
- **Per-channel processing** — Each drum voice includes its own processing controls for shaping tone directly within the instrument.
- **Sound design depth** — Parameter density goes beyond typical drum machine convention, consistent with Unfiltered Audio's broader design approach.
