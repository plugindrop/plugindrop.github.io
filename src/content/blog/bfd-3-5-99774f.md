## What Is BFD 3.5?

BFD 3.5 is a professional acoustic drum simulation platform developed by BFD Drums. It's built around deep-sampled drum kits recorded with multiple microphone positions and velocity layers, giving producers and engineers a high level of realism and control. The 3.5 update brought workflow and compatibility improvements to the already mature BFD 3 engine. It operates as a standalone application and as a plugin within your DAW.

---

## Key Features

- **Multi-mic sampling engine** — Kits are recorded across multiple mic positions (kick in/out, overheads, room), letting you blend the mix inside the plugin itself.
- **Groove editor** — An onboard pattern and groove editor lets you build drum performances without leaving the plugin environment.
- **Extensive kit customization** — Swap individual drum pieces, tune each component, and adjust bleed and ambience independently.
- **Deep mixer section** — BFD's internal mixer supports per-channel EQ, compression, and routing — useful for producers who want to print stems or do in-plugin mixing.
- **Large factory library** — The plugin ships with a substantial built-in sample library, with additional BFD expansion packs available separately.
- **DAW and standalone support** — Works in both plugin mode (VST/AU/AAX, check the product page for exact formats) and as a standalone instrument.
