## Key Features

- **Multi-mic drum sampling** — kits are captured with multiple microphone positions (close, overhead, room) so you can rebalance the mix after the fact.
- **Built-in mixer** — a dedicated console with EQ, compression, and effects routing designed around drum tracking workflows, not a generic channel strip.
- **Groove library** — a large collection of MIDI grooves and fills for programming realistic patterns without manual note entry.
- **Kit and articulation editing** — control over individual drum pieces, articulations, and velocity layers for detailed sound design.
- **DAW integration** — works as a plugin inside major hosts or on its own as a standalone app.
