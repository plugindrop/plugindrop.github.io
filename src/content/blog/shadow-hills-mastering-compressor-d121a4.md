## What Is the Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor?

The Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor is a plugin emulation of the Shadow Hills Industries hardware unit — a two-stage mastering compressor that has appeared on countless professional records. The hardware sells for several thousand dollars, making plugin versions the practical path for most producers. Plugin Alliance developed this emulation to capture both stages of the original's signal chain: an optical compressor followed by a discrete VCA, each with its own character and control set.

## Key Features

- **Dual-stage compression** — Optical and discrete VCA stages can be used independently or in series, giving you layered control over dynamics
- **Three transformer options** — Nickel, Iron, and Steel transformer modes each impart a different tonal saturation character, from subtle to more colored
- **Optical stage** — Program-dependent, smooth compression ideal for transparent glue on the mix bus
- **Discrete VCA stage** — Faster, punchier response suitable for more assertive dynamic control
- **Mastering-grade metering** — Gain reduction and output metering designed for precision at the final stage of a signal chain
