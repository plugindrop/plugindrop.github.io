## What you actually get

The product listing describes a reverb effect built around several processing layers rather than a single algorithm. Here's what's included:

- **Ducking control** — the vendor lists a ducking function that pulls the reverb signal down under dry-signal peaks, which keeps tails from masking transients in busy mixes.
- **Freeze function** — according to the product listing, this holds a reverb tail indefinitely, useful for building pad textures or sustaining a transition without re-triggering the source.
- **Multimode filter** — Plugin Boutique's listing includes a multimode filter for shaping the reverb's frequency content, so you can tame harsh highs or thin out low-end buildup before it hits the mix bus.
- **Finishers** — the vendor lists four Finisher categories: basic gaters, delay and modulation effects, and cinematic and sequenced soundscapes. That range pushes the plugin past plain reverb into rhythmic and ambient sound design.

## Where it fits in a session

A reverb with this feature set tends to work on more than one bus. Drop it on a vocal or drum bus where the ducking control can manage tail buildup during dense passages.

The multimode filter gives you a way to carve reverb tone before it reaches a mastering chain, so it can sit earlier in the signal path than a typical "set and forget" send effect.
