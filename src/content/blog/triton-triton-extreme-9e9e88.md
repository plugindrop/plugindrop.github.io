## What Is TRITON Extreme?

TRITON Extreme is a software instrument plugin based on KORG's iconic TRITON Extreme hardware workstation — a synth that defined the sound of countless pop, R&B, and hip-hop records in the early 2000s. KORG brought this workstation to the plugin world so producers can access its signature tones directly inside their DAW. The original hardware was renowned for its expansive PCM sound library, versatile synthesis engine, and the addition of MOSS (Modelling Object-based Sound Synthesis) physical modeling technology built into the Extreme edition. The plugin aims to preserve that legacy in a modern, accessible format.

---

## Key Features

- **HI (Hyper Integrated) Synthesis Engine** — The core architecture mirrors the original TRITON hardware, combining PCM sample playback with synthesis processing for rich, layered sounds.
- **MOSS Physical Modeling** — The Extreme version of the TRITON hardware was specifically notable for its MOSS board, which added physical modeling synthesis to the PCM engine; check the product page to confirm exactly which MOSS voices are included in the plugin.
- **Extensive Onboard Sound Library** — Ships with the PCM patches and programs from the original TRITON Extreme, covering keys, pads, basses, brass, and more.
- **Built-in Effects Processing** — Integrated effects chain consistent with the original hardware's signal path.
- **Arpeggiator** — Pattern-based arpeggiator inherited from the workstation's feature set.
- **DAW Integration** — Runs as a standard plugin inside your host without needing the original hardware.
