## Kick Ninja and Sub Ninja: What Are These Plugins?

The Him DSP is a developer focused on precision drum synthesis tools. Kick Ninja is a dedicated kick drum synthesizer designed to give producers direct control over the transient, body, and tail of a kick — without relying on samples. Sub Ninja is its companion plugin, targeting the sub-bass layer that underpins a kick's low-end punch. Together they form a synthesis-based toolkit built specifically around kick drum construction.

These are not general-purpose synths or samplers. They are purpose-built instruments for producers who want to sculpt kick sounds from scratch.

---

## Key Features

- **Dedicated kick synthesis engine** — Kick Ninja generates kicks entirely via synthesis, covering everything from tight tech house transients to deep techno thuds.
- **Sub-bass companion design** — Sub Ninja is purpose-built to complement kick synthesis, giving producers a matched low-end layer tuned to the kick's pitch.
- **Transient shaping controls** — Direct control over the click and attack phase allows precise tuning of the initial punch.
- **Pitch envelope control** — Adjustable pitch sweep is central to the kick design workflow, dialing in the characteristic "drop" of electronic kicks.
- **Streamlined, focused interface** — Both plugins are designed around a single task, keeping the workflow fast for producers who don't want to dig through menus.
