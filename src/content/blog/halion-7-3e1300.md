## What Is HALion 7?

HALion 7 is Steinberg's sample-based synthesis and sound design workstation, built for producers who want deep control over how sounds are triggered, layered, and shaped. It combines multiple synthesis methods in one engine rather than locking you into a single approach. Steinberg also sells a lighter "player" edition, HALion Sonic, so HALion 7 is positioned as the full editing and sound-design toolset.

## Key Features

- **Multi-engine synthesis** — sample playback sits alongside granular, wavetable, FM, and physical modeling tools within the same instrument, so you can blend synthesis types in a single patch.
- **Advanced sample editing** — built-in tools for slicing, mapping, and manipulating audio material directly inside the engine.
- **MegaTrig sample triggering** — Steinberg's system for advanced articulation and round-robin style triggering, aimed at more expressive sampled instruments.
- **Modular signal flow** — flexible routing between synthesis zones, filters, and effects for building custom instruments from scratch.
- **Factory content library** — ships with a bundled sound library; check the product page for the exact size and instrument list.
- **DAW integration** — works as a VST3/AU/AAX plugin and standalone, with tight integration inside Cubase and Nuendo.
