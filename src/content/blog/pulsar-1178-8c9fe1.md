## What Is Pulsar 1178?

Pulsar 1178 is a FET compressor plugin by Pulsar Audio, modeled after the classic hardware 1178 — the stereo counterpart to the legendary 1176. Pulsar Audio is a French developer with a strong reputation for meticulous analog circuit emulation, and this plugin carries that pedigree. The 1178 hardware was prized for its low noise floor and punchy FET character, and this plugin aims to bring that behavior into the DAW with modern precision.

---

## Key Features

- **FET Compression Circuit Modeling** — Captures the fast transient response and harmonic character of the original 1178 hardware topology.
- **All-Buttons Mode** — Replicates the classic "nuke" mode where all ratio buttons are engaged simultaneously, a staple technique for aggressive parallel compression.
- **Stereo & Mid/Side Processing** — Supports both standard stereo operation and M/S mode for advanced mixing applications.
- **Multiple Ratio Settings** — Includes the standard 1176/1178-style ratio options for dialing in subtle leveling through heavy limiting.
- **Oversampling** — High-quality oversampling options help maintain audio fidelity at the extremes of the compression range.
- **Analog-Style VU Metering** — Classic gain reduction and output metering consistent with the original hardware aesthetic.
