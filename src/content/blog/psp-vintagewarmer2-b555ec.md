## What Is PSP VintageWarmer2?

PSP VintageWarmer2 is a vintage-style saturation and soft-clipping plugin developed by PSP Audioware. It models the warmth and harmonic character associated with analog hardware — compressors, tape machines, and console summing — without requiring hardware. The plugin operates as both a single-band processor and a multi-band unit, making it flexible enough for individual tracks or full mixes. PSP Audioware has been building professional audio tools since the late 1990s and VintageWarmer has been part of their catalog for a long time.

---

## Key Features

- **Single-band and multi-band modes** — Process the full signal or split into up to three bands for more surgical warmth control.
- **Soft-clipping saturation algorithm** — Designed to emulate the gentle, musical clipping behavior of vintage analog circuitry.
- **Brick-wall limiting stage** — Keeps output levels in check after saturation is applied, useful for mastering and bus processing.
- **Oversampling** — Reduces aliasing artifacts when pushing the saturation hard.
- **VU-style metering** — Visual feedback consistent with classic hardware aesthetics and workflow.
- **Dual-stage drive and output controls** — Separate input drive and output level give fine control over the saturation-to-level relationship.
