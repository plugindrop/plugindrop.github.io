## What Is the Desolate & Velvet Guitars Bundle?

The Desolate & Velvet Guitars Bundle combines two cinematic guitar libraries from e-instruments, a developer known for deeply sampled virtual instruments. Both libraries run on Native Instruments' free Kontakt Player, making them accessible without a full Kontakt license. Together, they cover a wide tonal range — from twangy, atmospheric tension (Desolate) to mellow warmth and emotive depth (Velvet).

## Key Features

- **Nine distinct guitars across two libraries** — Desolate includes four guitars (solid body, '60s chime, baritone, semi-hollow), while Velvet adds five more (semi-hollow, mellow baritone, shimmer guitar, non-traditional jazz semi-hollow, and amped acoustic).
- **Real amps and real spring reverb** — Every sample was recorded through two iconic vintage amps (American clean and British chime) with blendable dry/wet spring reverb.
- **Deep articulation system** — Plucks, chords, sustains, mutes, swells, feedback, harmonics, vibrato bar, and textured loops with major/minor variations, all switchable via modwheel.
- **Extensive FX and presets** — Velvet Guitars alone ships with 25 main presets and 20 FX chain presets covering compression, drive, delay, modulation, and reverb.
- **24-bit / 48 kHz sample quality** — Approximately 68+ GB combined content across both libraries, with around 62,000 individual samples in Velvet Guitars alone.
- **NKS 2 compatible** — Full integration with NI hardware controllers for hands-on sound design.
