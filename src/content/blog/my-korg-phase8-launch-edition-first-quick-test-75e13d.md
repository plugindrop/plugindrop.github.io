## What Is the Korg Phase8?

The Korg Phase8 is an 8-voice acoustic synthesizer developed by Korg Berlin. It's not analog. It's not digital. It uses electromagnets to excite chromatically tuned steel resonators — real, physical tines — and feeds their vibrations into a synthesis engine with envelope shaping, wavefolding, and modulation. Think of a Fender Rhodes turned inside-out, crossed with a kalimba and strapped to a sequencer. There's genuinely nothing else quite like it on the market right now.

## Key Features

- **8 Independent Electromechanical Voices** — Each voice uses a steel resonator and electromagnet for truly acoustic sound generation.
- **13 Chromatically Tuned Resonators** — The instrument ships with 13 resonators; 8 can be installed at any time, letting you customize your tuning setup.
- **Analogue Wavefolding & Modulation** — Three amplitude modulation types including tremolo and two pitch-dependent modulation modes, one with optional harmonic quantization.
- **Built-in Sequencer** — Supports step programming and unquantized live recording, with per-voice step skip for polymetric patterns. Save and recall across 8 memory slots.
- **Hands-On Physical Interaction** — Touch, pluck, strum, or tap the resonators directly. Add found objects for completely new textures.
- **MIDI/USB-MIDI & CV Connectivity** — Full external control over parameters, tempo sync, and note triggering from external gear.
