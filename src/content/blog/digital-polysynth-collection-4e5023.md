## What Is Digital Polysynth Collection?

Digital Polysynth Collection is a Roland software-instrument bundle containing recreations of four digital polyphonic synthesizers: D-50, JD-800, SYSTEM-1, and SYSTEM-8. It is aimed at producers who want these specific Roland synth architectures available inside a DAW.

The bundle uses Roland’s Digital Circuit Behavior (DCB) technology. Roland describes this as combining original sample ROM data with behavior modeling of the hardware’s digital components and circuitry.

It is particularly relevant for projects that call for digital-era Roland sounds, including layered patches, arpeggiated parts, pads, and synth leads. The collection is a targeted four-instrument bundle, not a general-purpose library of dozens of unrelated synths.

## Key Features

- **Four Roland software synths:** The bundle includes D-50, JD-800, SYSTEM-1, and SYSTEM-8 recreations.

- **Digital Circuit Behavior technology:** Roland states that DCB models the behavior of the original instruments’ digital components and circuitry.

- **Extended software controls:** The listed feature set includes extended parameters, an advanced arpeggiator, and onboard effects.

- **Plugin formats:** The collection supports VST3, AU, and AAX plugin formats.
