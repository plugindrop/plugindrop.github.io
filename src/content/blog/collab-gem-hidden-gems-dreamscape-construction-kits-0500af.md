Construction kits fit at the writing and arrangement stages of a production. They can be used when setting the tonal direction of a new session, sketching an intro, or developing a section before replacing or layering parts with original recordings and programmed elements.

A practical use case is starting with a kit as a reference point for rhythm, harmony, and atmosphere. Producers can then build a project around that direction with their own drums, synths, vocals, transitions, and mix decisions.
