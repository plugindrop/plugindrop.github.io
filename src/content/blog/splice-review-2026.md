**TL;DR:** Splice sells a sample subscription (Splice Sounds) and a pay-as-you-go plugin program (Rent-to-Own). On its pricing page Splice lists a Creator plan (about 200 downloads a month; $4.99 for the first month, then $19.99/month) and a Sounds plan (about 100 downloads a month; $12.99/month billed yearly). Unused credits roll over, and downloads stay yours if you cancel. This page sticks to what Splice publishes; we have not tested the service hands-on.

## Splice Sounds: The Sample Subscription

<div class="video-embed"><iframe loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/x4I_vyN_tjI" title="Splice Sounds — sound demo" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

- **Developer:** Splice
- **Plans listed:** Creator (about 200 monthly downloads) and Sounds (about 100 monthly downloads)
- **Catalog:** Full access to 3M+ sounds on both plans
- **Works with:** Any DAW; Splice apps for desktop and mobile

Splice describes its plans in monthly downloads, backed by credits. Per Splice's FAQ, every sample costs one credit, while MIDI patterns and presets use up to three credits each. Splice states that unused credits automatically roll over to the next month, and that you keep everything you download, even if you cancel.

Both plans include the desktop and mobile apps, DAW plugins, presets for Serum and Vital, new packs every week and curated collections. The Creator plan additionally lists 1,000+ presets and "Rare Finds". Splice's FAQ says INSTRUMENT, its sample-based virtual instrument, is included on the Creator and Creator+ plans.

### If you cancel

Per Splice's FAQ, a cancelled plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. Remaining credits expire 28 days after the final billing period ends, and you can put a plan on hold for up to two months instead. After cancelling you can still browse your library and re-download samples that are already in it.

### Plugins and DAW support

Splice says Sounds works with any DAW. Its own plugins, including Bridge, Astra and Beatmaker, are in VST3 or AU format. Bridge lets you audition sounds in the context of your project without using credits. Splice also lists in-DAW integrations for Pro Tools 2025.6+, Studio One 7+ and Ableton Live 12.3 (described in the FAQ as currently in beta).

## Splice INSTRUMENT

Splice INSTRUMENT is a sample-based virtual instrument, available as a plugin (VST, AU), with browsable presets played on a chromatic keyboard. Splice lists a dedicated INSTRUMENT plan at $12.99/month for unlimited instrument access, says two premium packs drop every month, and offers a free download of the instrument with new free drops each month.

Each plugin has its own monthly price on Splice's marketplace, which also lists some plugins for outright sale and some as free. The number of months varies by plugin (Serum 2's page lists 25), so check the plugin's page on splice.com before starting. If you would rather compare one-time prices, retailers such as [Plugin Boutique](https://www.pluginboutique.com/?a_aid=69cb95abe1763&chan=art&data1=splice-review-2026) sell perpetual licenses for many of the same products; check both prices before deciding.

## How to Choose

- **Want a steady flow of samples and can use about 100 to 200 downloads a month:** compare the Sounds and Creator plans on splice.com/plans. Creator also lists 1,000+ presets and Rare Finds.
- **Mostly want presets for a virtual instrument:** the INSTRUMENT plan is listed separately at $12.99/month, and Creator includes it.
- **Need a specific plugin now and prefer to spread the cost:** Rent-to-Own is Splice's pay-as-you-go option. Look at the plugin's monthly price and compare it with the one-time price elsewhere.
- **Already own a large sample library:** check whether the monthly download allowance matches what you would use before subscribing.
