**TL;DR:** Splice offers two main ways to get music-production tools: an ongoing subscription for sample packs, called Splice Sounds, and a separate pay-as-you-go option for software instruments and effects, called Rent-to-Own. The subscription side comes in a couple of different plans, each allowing a different number of downloads each month — one plan allows for a larger number of monthly downloads, while another allows for a somewhat smaller number but is billed annually rather than monthly.

In both cases, any download credits that go unused in a given period carry over rather than expiring, and anything already downloaded remains the subscriber's to keep even if the subscription is later cancelled. This overview reflects only what is published on the company's own plans page, rather than any independent, hands-on evaluation of how the service performs in practice.

## Introduction

<!-- role:intro -->

Splice sells two kinds of plans: a monthly sample subscription called Splice Sounds, and a pay-as-you-go plugin program called Rent-to-Own. The sample subscription is offered in two tiers, each with a different monthly download allowance — a larger-allowance plan with roughly 200 downloads a month, and a smaller-allowance plan with roughly 100 downloads a month billed on an annual basis. Any download credits left unused in a given month carry over rather than expiring, and sounds already downloaded remain usable even if the subscription is later canceled. The following overview reflects only what is publicly listed for these plans.

## Splice Sounds: The Sample Subscription

- **Developer:** Splice
- **Plans listed:** Creator (about 200 monthly downloads) and Sounds (about 100 monthly downloads)
- **Catalog:** Full access to 3M+ sounds on both plans
- **Works with:** Any DAW; Splice apps for desktop and mobile

Splice describes its plans in monthly downloads, backed by credits. Per Splice's FAQ, every sample costs one credit, while MIDI patterns and presets use up to three credits each. Splice states that unused credits automatically roll over to the next month, and that you keep everything you download, even if you cancel. Both plans include the desktop and mobile apps, DAW plugins, presets for Serum and Vital, new packs every week and curated collections. The Creator plan additionally lists 1,000+ presets and "Rare Finds". Splice's FAQ says INSTRUMENT, its sample-based virtual instrument, is included on the Creator and Creator+ plans. ### If you cancel

Per Splice's FAQ, a cancelled plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. Remaining credits expire 28 days after the final billing period ends, and you can put a plan on hold for up to two months instead. After cancelling you can still browse your library and re-download samples that are already in it. ### Plugins and DAW support

Splice says Sounds works with any DAW. Its own plugins, including Bridge, Astra and Beatmaker, are in VST3 or AU format. Bridge lets you audition sounds in the context of your project without using credits. Splice also lists in-DAW integrations for Pro Tools 2025.6+, Studio One 7+ and Ableton Live 12.3 (described in the FAQ as currently in beta). ---

## Splice INSTRUMENT

Splice INSTRUMENT is a sample-based virtual instrument, available as a plugin (VST, AU), with browsable presets played on a chromatic keyboard. ---

## Splice Rent-to-Own: Plugins

- **Developer:** Splice, with plugin vendors
- **How it works (per Splice):** Try any software free, then pay as you go until you own it. No interest. The number of months varies by plugin (Serum 2's page lists 25), so check the plugin's page on splice.com before starting. If you would rather compare one-time prices, retailers such as [Plugin Boutique](https://www.pluginboutique.com/?a_aid=69cb95abe1763&chan=art&data1=splice-review-2026) sell perpetual licenses for many of the same products; check both prices before deciding. ---

Q: How do Splice Sounds credits work?
A: Every sample uses one credit, and MIDI patterns and presets use up to three credits each. Any credits left unused at the end of a billing period roll over to the following month.

Q: Do downloads stop working after a subscription is cancelled?
A: No. Anything already downloaded remains available even after cancelling. Any credits still unused at that point expire 28 days after the final billing period ends.

Q: Is it possible to pause a subscription instead of cancelling it?
A: Yes, a plan can be placed on hold for up to two months, which prevents accrued credits from being lost during the pause.

Q: Are plugins available on a pay-as-you-go basis?
A: Yes. Full licenses for more than 130 software tools can be paid off gradually, with no added interest, until the plugin is fully owned rather than paid for all at once.
