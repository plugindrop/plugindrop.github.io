## What Is the Mercury-6 by Cherry Audio?

The Mercury-6 is Cherry Audio's software emulation of the Roland Jupiter-6, a classic analog polysynth from the early 1980s. Cherry Audio is known for producing detailed, affordable software recreations of vintage hardware — their catalog includes emulations of everything from Oberheim to Moog-era designs. The Mercury-6 targets producers and sound designers who want that warm, characterful Jupiter-6 sound without hunting down aging hardware.

---

## Key Features

- **Dual DCOs per voice** — faithfully models the Jupiter-6's oscillator architecture, delivering that signature analog-style thickness
- **Cross-modulation (Cross-Mod)** — a distinctive Jupiter-6 feature that enables FM-style timbres and metallic, evolving sounds
- **Arpeggiator and chord memory** — both classic features from the original hardware, preserved in the plugin
- **Unison mode** — stacks voices for massive, detuned pads and leads
- **Extensive modulation routing** — goes beyond the original hardware with expanded mod matrix options typical of Cherry Audio's design philosophy
