## What you actually get

IK Multimedia lists 47 stomp boxes, 34 amps, 27 cabinets, 14 speakers, and 11 microphones, plus 36 rack effects and 8 rooms, which gives a large pool of gear combinations without leaving the plugin window.

- The product listing states you can run up to 57 effects with 3 cabinets and 3 amps at once, which is enough headroom to stack dirty and clean paths or split a signal for parallel processing.
- According to the product listing, VIR (Volumetric Impulse Response) cabinet emulation supports up to 600 impulse responses per speaker, and you can load your own IRs too — useful if you already have cabinet captures from other rigs.
- IK Multimedia lists an amp section with impedance matching and a 3D amp cabinet room with selectable room simulation and flexible dual miking, so mic position can be adjusted after the take instead of re-tracking.
- The listing notes a mixer for microphone, room, and DI signals alongside master level, with rack and stomp effects that are swappable and routed flexibly, so a chain can be rebuilt without starting the signal path over.

## Where it fits in a session

AmpliTube 5 sits at the front of a guitar or bass chain when tracking direct-in, standing in for a physical amp and mic setup. It also works for re-amping dry DI tracks later, once a mix direction is set and tone decisions can be made without re-recording.

During mixing, the cabinet, mic, and room controls let you adjust tone on an already-recorded part instead of bouncing back to a live room. IK Multimedia lists it for both studio applications and live performances, so the same rig can carry over to a stage setup.
