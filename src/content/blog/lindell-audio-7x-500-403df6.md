## What Is Lindell Audio 7X-500?

Lindell Audio is a Swedish company with deep roots in both hardware manufacturing and software emulation. The 7X-500 is a plugin compressor modeled after the character and behavior of a 500-series format hardware unit — the kind of gear that lives in professional racks and adds punch, glue, and warmth to mixes. It targets the gap between surgical digital compression and the tactile feel of analog hardware, all within your DAW.

---

## Key Features

- **Hardware-modeled circuit emulation** — Captures the non-linear behavior and harmonic character of a physical 500-series compressor rather than a "clean" digital response.
- **Bus compression focus** — Designed with mix bus and subgroup applications in mind, making it well-suited for gluing groups of tracks.
- **Vintage-style gain reduction** — Delivers the kind of pump and bloom that classic hardware compressors are valued for.
- **Attack and release controls** — Standard compressor parameters modeled to respond the way hardware does, not just numerically.
- **Gain makeup control** — Allows you to restore level after compression without leaving the plugin.
