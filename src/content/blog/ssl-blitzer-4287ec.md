## Ten Compression Curves Put Blitzer’s Controls in Plain View

<!-- role:product -->

Blitzer provides 10 unique compression curves across 10 ratios, giving each ratio its own shape and tone rather than treating ratio choice as a single-number setting. In plain terms, it is a compressor for reducing or controlling level changes in a track while offering tools to reshape how the signal responds.

The main controls cover ATTACK and RELEASE for tailoring compressor response, while the TRANSIENTS attack modification control can reintroduce some of the original signal’s dynamics. MIX handles parallel compression, so the compressed signal can be blended with the unaffected signal for punch. AUTO GAIN maintains levels based on threshold and ratio, helping level changes remain managed as settings change.

Blitzer also includes a detection sidechain with an HPF and a bell filter. The HPF removes unwanted low-end frequencies from triggering compression, a specific option for drum busses. The bell filter is intended for taming unruly vocals and harshness. For faster setup work, the plug-in includes cross-platform preset management, A/B preset switching, undo/redo, and contextual help accessed through the “?” control.

Its supplied tutorial is titled *SSL Blitzer Plug-in Tutorial: Overheads, drum bus, lead vocals and full mix processing.*, pointing to overheads, a drum bus, lead vocals, and full-mix processing as the demonstrated contexts.

## FAQ

<!-- role:faq -->

Q: What does BLITZ! mode do?

A: BLITZ! is Blitzer’s “all buttons in” compressor mode. It can slam room mics or energize an entire mix.

Q: Can Blitzer add colour without compressing?

A: Yes. The 1:1 ratio can warm tracks with soft saturation and gentle harmonics without compression, while DRIVE can add gentle warmth or decimate the signal.

Q: How does Blitzer help with drum-bus compression?

A: Its detection sidechain HPF can remove unwanted low-end frequencies from triggering the compressor, and MIX can be used for parallel compression and punch.

Q: What helps when compressing vocals?

A: The detection-sidechain bell filter is provided to tame unruly vocals and harshness, while ATTACK, RELEASE, and TRANSIENTS adjust compressor response.
