## How to read these numbers

A single price screenshot tells you almost nothing — it could be a rare drop, a typo, or a regional pricing quirk. What matters is the pattern across many checks over time:

- **If a price sits near $189 for extended stretches**, that's the realistic floor for a routine sale — expect to see it again during major promotional calendar events (Black Friday, New Year, summer sales).
- **If a price briefly touches $99**, that's an outlier worth acting on immediately if you see it, but not something to wait around for indefinitely.
- **If a price never drops below $249**, that's a signal the product either isn't currently in a discount cycle or that stock/licensing terms have changed — worth rechecking before assuming the sale pattern still holds.

This is the core reason to track price history instead of relying on a single deal page: it separates a genuine floor from a marketing headline.
