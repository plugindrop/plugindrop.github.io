## What Is Arithmophone Pentatone?

Pentatone is a free iOS synthesizer app from Arithmophone that takes a different approach to mobile music-making. Instead of handing you a standard piano keyboard or a chromatic grid, it presents a pentatonic layout — the five-note scale used across folk, blues, pop, and countless other genres. The idea is simple: if every note on the keyboard sounds good together, you can focus on playing rather than avoiding wrong notes. It runs on iPhones and iPads with iOS 15 or above.
