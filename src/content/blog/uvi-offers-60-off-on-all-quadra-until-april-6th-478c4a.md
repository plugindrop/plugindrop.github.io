## What Is UVI Quadra?

Quadra is a series of four multi-instrument and sequence designer plugins from UVI, the Paris-based developer known for deep-sampled virtual instruments. Each Quadra title layers multiple sound sources into a single playable instrument with built-in sequencing, letting producers generate everything from standard melodic parts to complex evolving progressions and harmonizations. The series runs in the free UVI Workstation player or in UVI Falcon.

The four titles in the Quadra lineup are:

- **Quadra: Modular** — 30 modular systems turned into playable, sequenceable instruments.
- **Quadra: Traveler** — World instruments and textures from across the globe.
- **Quadra: Muted and Harmonics** — Focused on muted articulations and harmonic timbres.
- **Quadra: Metal and Wood** — Resonant metallic and wooden sound sources.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Instrument Layering** — Each Quadra combines multiple sound sources into a single patch, creating rich, stacked textures without complex routing.
- **Built-In Sequence Designer** — An integrated step sequencer lets you build rhythmic patterns and evolving phrases directly inside the instrument.
- **Wide Timbral Range** — From conventional melodic parts to experimental, evolving soundscapes — each title covers a broad sonic territory.
- **Runs in Free UVI Workstation** — No need to own Falcon; the free UVI Workstation plugin hosts all Quadra instruments.
- **Adaptable to Multiple Genres** — The four titles collectively span electronic, cinematic, ambient, world, and experimental production styles.
