## A Single-DCO Engine With AKAI MPC/FORCE Touch Control
<!-- role:product -->

A Japanese hardware synth from 1982 is the starting point for Jura, built by AIR Music as what the brief calls a recreation of that original circuit rather than a new design. The engine centers on a single DCO, a dedicated sub oscillator, and a VCF filter stage, with a Classic Juno Chorus section carried over from the source hardware. AIR Music adds a second envelope not found on the original unit, plus audio extras under the hood: the VCF gains saturation and drive, and the DCO can switch between modern and vintage noise types. Jura ships in two workflow layouts, the original front panel design and an extended modern UI with more tweaking surface, and in two formats: an AU/VST/AAX plugin and an AKAI MPC/FORCE Standalone version. On qualified MPC or FORCE hardware, every parameter gets complete touch control, turning the plugin into a hands-on instrument rather than a mouse-driven one. A companion video, "Introducing AIR: Jura The Synth Of 82," walks through this same single-DCO, Juno Chorus layout.

## The 1982 Circuit Lineage Behind Its Chorus
<!-- role:sound -->

Hits by The Eurythmics, Cyndi Lauper, and Aha in the 1980s trace back to the original 1982 hardware that Jura recreates, and the brief credits more recent use of that same synth to Charlie Puth, Tame Impala, and the Weeknd. AIR Music says it built Jura to carry that circuit forward while fitting modern production workflows, pairing the single-DCO and sub-oscillator path with the Classic Juno Chorus section, a delay stage that runs up to 2 seconds and syncs to BPM, a reverb section, and a 3-band EQ. The second envelope AIR Music added beyond the original hardware extends how far that signal path can be shaped before it reaches those effects.

## Who Reaches for Jura's Touch Control and Arpeggiator
<!-- role:audience -->

- **Touch-controlled hardware workflow** — On a qualified AKAI MPC or FORCE standalone unit, producers get complete touch control of every parameter, shaping the synth by hand instead of through a mouse.
- **Classic PWM sound design** — The oscillator section builds pulse-width-modulation sounds using manual control, an LFO, or either of two envelope modulation sources.
- **Arpeggiated pattern work** — The built-in arpeggiator lets producers sequence patterns directly inside the plugin or the standalone instrument.

## How Jura's DCO and Chorus Compare With Other Plugin Boutique Synths
<!-- role:peers -->

Cherry Audio's GX-80 points at a different hardware lineage, offering "precision recreation of both the legendary CS-80 and unmatched GX-1 filters," while Jura's filter and oscillator section stays built around its own single-DCO, sub-oscillator, and Juno Chorus design tied to the 1982 source unit.
