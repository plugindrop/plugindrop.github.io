## What you actually get

Jura is a software synth built around analog-style subtractive synthesis. According to the product listing, it ships with a switchable interface that toggles between classic and expanded control layouts, so quick live tweaks and deeper sound-design sessions can use different levels of detail without separate patches.

- AIR Music Technology lists an oscillator section with pulse, saw, sub, and noise waveforms, which covers everything from thin leads to fuller bass layers from one sound source.
- The product listing describes pulse width modulation driven manually, by LFO, or by either of two envelope sources, useful for the slowly shifting pads this synth style is known for.
- A VCF filter section with cutoff, resonance, saturation, and drive is listed, plus modulation via an invertible envelope, LFO, keyboard tracking, and velocity, giving the filter real dynamic range across a keyboard.
- The listing also includes a dedicated Juno Chorus section and a BPM-syncable delay of up to two seconds, both useful for widening a mono patch or adding tempo-locked movement.

## Where it fits in a session

The filter and PWM controls work well at the sound-design stage, before a patch is committed to a track. The Juno Chorus section is suited to a mix pass where a flat pad or lead needs stereo width without extra plugins. The BPM-synced delay fits late in a signal chain, on a send, for rhythmic echoes that stay locked to the song's tempo.
