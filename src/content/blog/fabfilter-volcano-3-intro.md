## Volcano 3: What It Does and How You Shape It
<!-- role:intro -->
<!-- pick:FabFilter Volcano 3 -->

FabFilter Volcano 3 is a creative filter plug-in built around classic EQ-type filter shapes. Its core strength is ultra-flexible filter routing, including per-channel and mid/side modes, so you can treat the left and right channels, or the stereo width, as separate filtering paths. The plug-in also offers a 6 dB/oct filter slope option alongside its other slope choices, giving you another way to shape how steeply a filter cuts. For tracking what is happening as you work, Volcano 3 includes live modulation visualization for targets and sources, letting you watch how each modulation source is affecting a parameter in real time. If you want extra headroom for quality, the High Quality option enables maximum internal oversampling for the best results. It comes as stereo and mono plug-ins, and its interface can be resized, with various interface sizes and full screen mode available for different studio setups. This suits work that calls for flexible, per-channel or mid/side filtering with modulation from several source types.

## Working With Volcano 3's Filter Features
<!-- role:character -->

Volcano 3 offers ultra-flexible filter routing, including per-channel and mid/side modes, so a track's left and right or mid and side content can be filtered on separate terms. Alongside its other slope settings, the plug-in adds a 6 dB/oct filter slope option, giving you a gentler cut when a steeper one is more than a part needs. On screen, live modulation visualization for targets and sources shows what each modulation source is doing, so adjustments are easier to follow as you make them. There is also a High Quality option, enabling maximum internal oversampling for the best results, and you can switch it on whenever you want that extra accuracy. Taken together, these controls let you choose how filtering is applied and see the changes as you make them.
