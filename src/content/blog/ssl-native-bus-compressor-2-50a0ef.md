## G-Series bus compression with dry/wet control
<!-- role:product -->

SSL Native Bus Compressor 2 brings the SSL G-Series center-section bus compressor approach into a native plug-in. In plain terms, it is designed to process a full mix or other combined signal rather than only one isolated source. The aim is to make the parts of that signal work together as one.

The plug-in provides attack, release, and compression-ratio controls, giving producers specific controls over how compression responds. It also adds features not found on the original hardware: dry/wet signal blending for parallel processing and a sidechain highpass filter.

Dry/wet blending keeps an adjustable amount of the unprocessed signal alongside the compressed signal. That makes parallel processing available from within the plug-in. The sidechain highpass filter changes the control signal used by the compressor, with the stated purpose of reducing low-end pumping.

SSL Native Bus Compressor 2 also integrates with SSL 360 and can be controlled from SSL 360-enabled hardware surfaces including UC1 and UF8. The available video material is not supplied here, so this deal focuses on the documented controls and intended mix-bus role.

## Glue, punch, and retained dynamic character
<!-- role:sound -->

The documented sound direction is glue and punch for a mix. SSL positions the Bus Compressor as a way to glue a mix together, while retaining its dynamic character even at higher compression ratios.

That matters when compression is being used across a combined signal: the objective is not simply to reduce level, but to shape how the mix responds as a whole. The SSL Bus Compressor is also associated with making a mix “sound like a record,” a phrase used in the supplied description.

The new attack, release, and compression-ratio options provide the relevant controls for setting that response. The sidechain highpass filter adds another route to manage low-end pumping, while dry/wet control allows the compressed and uncompressed signals to be blended.

## Mix-bus processing and parallel compression in one plug-in
<!-- role:audience -->

A producer working on a full mix can use SSL Native Bus Compressor 2 for stereo bus compression and for dry/wet parallel processing. These are two distinct supported applications: processing a mix as a combined signal, and blending compressed signal with dry signal.

The sidechain highpass filter is relevant when low-end pumping needs to be reduced. Rather than requiring a separate parallel-processing setup, the dry/wet control is part of the plug-in’s listed feature set.

SSL 360 users can also place the plug-in in a workflow involving SSL 360-enabled control surfaces. UC1 and UF8 are the specifically named compatible hardware surfaces in the supplied product information.
