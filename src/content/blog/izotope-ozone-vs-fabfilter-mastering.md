## Introduction

<!-- role:intro -->

FabFilter Pro-L 2 and iZotope Ozone 12 often get compared, but they aren't really solving the same problem. Ozone is a full mastering environment that bundles EQ, multiband dynamics, stereo imaging, low-end control, and a limiter into one plugin. Pro-L 2, on the other hand, does one job: limiting. It is built specifically as a true peak limiter, intended for use by mastering and mixing engineers working on mono, stereo, or surround material, and it also supports external side-chain triggering for stem mastering work.

What sets Pro-L 2 apart within that narrow focus is depth. It offers eight separate limiting algorithms, each with its own character and intended purpose, so an engineer can choose a style of limiting rather than being locked into one approach. It supports up to 32x linear-phase oversampling and includes genuine true peak limiting, which helps prevent peaks from exceeding target levels after conversion to other formats. Metering is built to match that precision, with highly accurate output and gain reduction metering, including true peak metering, plus an optional DC offset filter for cleaning up low-frequency DC bias before the signal is limited.

Put simply, the aim behind Pro-L 2 is transparent sound paired with maximum loudness, giving engineers a way to push levels without the limiting process becoming obvious or damaging to the material. That makes it less a competitor to an all-in-one suite like Ozone and more a tool that often sits inside or alongside one, handling the final loudness stage with a level of control and sonic transparency that a general-purpose limiter module may not match.

## FabFilter Pro-L 2

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:FabFilter Pro-L 2 -->

FabFilter Pro-L 2 is a true peak limiter built for mastering and mixing engineers who want transparent sound combined with maximum loudness. It offers eight distinct limiting algorithms, each with its own character and purpose, ranging from completely transparent, linear-phase processing to more colored, musical limiting styles. The plugin handles true peak limiting with up to 32x linear-phase oversampling, which helps catch inter-sample peaks that might otherwise cause distortion during playback or streaming.

Metering is a major strength of the plugin. Output and gain reduction are shown with high accuracy, including dedicated true peak metering, so engineers can keep a close eye on loudness and headroom while they work. An optional DC offset filter is also built in, letting users remove unwanted low-frequency offset from a signal before it passes through the limiter.

The interface is interactive and visually clear, putting full control over every parameter in the engineer's hands rather than automating the process. It is meant to sit at the end of a signal chain rather than replace earlier mixing and mastering steps such as equalization, compression, or imaging, which are expected to already be done by the time a track reaches this limiter.

It suits engineers working on mono, stereo, or surround material, and its external side-chain triggering also makes it useful for stem mastering. Taken together, it is best suited to professional mastering engineers, mixing engineers adding a final polishing stage, and anyone who needs accurate, reliable loudness metering alongside high-quality limiting.

## Limiting Quality & Transparency

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Limiting Quality & Transparency -->

Limiting Quality & Transparency — A genuine split

Ozone 12's IRC LL algorithm is excellent. For the vast majority of music production use cases — streaming releases, EP masters, album finales — it sounds clean, handles transients well, and is easy to dial in. Most producers won't hear a difference between it and Pro-L 2 at moderate limiting depths.

Push harder — deep limiting for club records, high-loudness commercial masters, or anything where the limiter is genuinely working hard — and the gap widens. Pro-L 2's Transparent and Modern algorithms retain more inter-sample peak accuracy, deliver finer control over attack and release shaping, and exhibit less pumping or harshness at equivalent gain reduction. Experienced mastering engineers consistently reach for Pro-L 2 at the ceiling stage precisely because of this precision.

**Winner: FabFilter Pro-L 2** — for high-demand limiting where transparency and control are non-negotiable.

Metering & Streaming Compliance — Pro-L 2 wins clearly

**Winner: FabFilter Pro-L 2** — essential for streaming-compliant mastering workflows.

## FAQ

<!-- role:faq -->

Q: Can I use FabFilter Pro-L 2 inside iZotope Ozone?
A: Yes, and this combination is common. Ozone's built-in maximizer can be bypassed so its output feeds into Pro-L 2 as the final limiting stage, combining Ozone's equalization, imaging, and dynamics processing with Pro-L 2's limiting algorithms.

Q: Is iZotope Ozone 12 good enough for professional mastering releases?
A: Yes. Ozone 12 Advanced is used regularly on commercially released music. Its IRC LL limiting algorithm is highly transparent, and its AI-assisted workflow has matured considerably, so the results it can produce are genuinely professional in quality.

Q: Does FabFilter Pro-L 2 include any EQ or compression?
A: No. Pro-L 2 is a limiter and loudness meter only. It does not include equalization, compression, multiband processing, or stereo imaging, so separate tools, or a full mastering suite, are needed to cover those stages.

Q: Which plugin is better for electronic music and club mastering?
A: For high-loudness electronic music that pushes the limiter hard, Pro-L 2's Modern and Aggressive algorithms handle extreme gain reduction more smoothly than many alternatives. For competitive streaming loudness in genres such as EDM, techno, or hip-hop, having Pro-L 2 available specifically for that final stage is worthwhile.

Q: Does iZotope offer a subscription or trial?
A: iZotope makes a free trial of Ozone available. FabFilter offers a fully functional trial version that runs for thirty days and requires no registration.
