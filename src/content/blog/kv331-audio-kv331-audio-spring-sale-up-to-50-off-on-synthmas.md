## What Is Synthmaster?

Synthmaster is a semi-modular software synthesizer developed by KV331 Audio. It supports multiple synthesis engines — including wavetable, virtual analog, additive, and spectral morphing — making it a versatile tool for a wide range of sound design applications. The lineup includes Synthmaster 2 (the flagship), Synthmaster One (a streamlined, single-oscillator design), and Synthmaster Player (a free preset player). KV331 also sells a large catalog of expansion packs covering genres from ambient to cinematic to EDM.

## Key Features

- **Multiple Synthesis Engines:** Synthmaster 2 combines wavetable, VA, additive, and phase modulation synthesis within a single instrument, covering broad sonic territory.

- **Built-In Arpeggiator and Step Sequencer:** Both tools are included natively, with per-step parameter control for rhythmic sound design.
