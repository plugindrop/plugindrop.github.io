## Native Instruments Komplete 26: What's in the Bundle?

Komplete 26 is Native Instruments' flagship music production suite, designed to cover the full production workflow from initial composition through to final mix and master. It draws from three major portfolios: Native Instruments' own instruments and samplers, iZotope's industry-standard mixing and mastering processors, and Brainworx's professional mixing tools. For producers who want a single purchase to handle a wide range of creative and technical tasks, this bundle is built around that goal.

---

## Key Features

- **190+ Instruments & Effects** — A cross-genre toolkit covering synthesis, sampling, drums, orchestral, and sound design.
- **180,000+ Sounds** — Presets and samples spanning genres from cinematic to electronic to urban production.
- **iZotope Tools Included** — Mixing and mastering processors from iZotope (such as Ozone and Neutron series) are part of the package through NI's portfolio integration.
- **Brainworx Mixing Plugins** — Professional-grade studio processors from the Brainworx catalog are included, expanding the mixing toolkit.
- **Full Production Coverage** — Tools span composition, sound design, mixing, and mastering — designed to reduce the need for separate purchases across the workflow.
- **Multiple Tiers Available** — Komplete 26 is offered in different editions (Standard, Ultimate, Collector's Edition), with upgrade pricing available for existing users.
