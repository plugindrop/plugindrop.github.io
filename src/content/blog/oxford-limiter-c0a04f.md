## What Is the Oxford Limiter?

The Oxford Limiter is a professional mastering limiter developed by Sonnox (formerly Sony Oxford). Originally designed for high-end studio environments, it's built around transparent limiting with inter-sample peak detection — a feature that matters increasingly as music gets streamed and decoded on consumer devices. Sonnox has a long reputation in broadcast and mastering circles, and the Oxford Limiter is one of their flagship dynamics tools.

## Key Features

- **Enhance Function** — Increases perceived loudness without simply squashing transients, giving masters more punch without obvious limiting artifacts
- **Inter-Sample Peak (ISP) Detection** — Catches peaks that exceed 0 dBFS after digital-to-analog conversion, helping prevent distortion on streaming platforms and playback devices
- **Soft Clip** — Provides a gentler ceiling option for material that needs subtle saturation-style rounding rather than hard limiting
- **Dithering** — Built-in dithering options for final output, removing the need for a separate dither plugin at the end of your mastering chain
- **Dual Metering** — Displays both true peak and perceived loudness, giving you the visual feedback needed for mastering to streaming standards

## Who Is It For?

- **Mastering engineers** — The ISP detection and transparent limiting character are designed around the demands of final-stage mastering, not just mix-bus limiting
- **Bedroom producers finishing their own tracks** — At this price, it brings professional-grade loudness tools within reach for self-releasing artists who want competitive masters
- **Mix engineers handling final delivery** — Useful for projects where a separate mastering stage isn't in the workflow and final limiting happens inside the mix session
- **Producers targeting streaming platforms** — The true peak and ISP features align directly with Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube's loudness normalization requirements
