## What Is Ozone 12 Advanced?

Ozone 12 Advanced is iZotope's flagship mastering suite — a full-stack plugin and standalone application designed to take mixes from rough to release-ready. It combines AI-assisted analysis with a deep library of mastering processors, covering everything from dynamic EQ and limiting to stereo imaging and stem-level rebalancing. As the "Advanced" tier, it includes the full set of modules and processing options that the Standard version excludes.

---

## Key Features

- **AI Mastering Assistant** — Analyzes your track and suggests a starting signal chain, giving you a calibrated starting point rather than a blank slate.
- **Master Rebalance** — Adjust the level of individual elements (vocals, bass, drums) in a fully mixed and bounced file using source-separation technology.
- **Low End Focus** — Targets mud and clash in the low frequencies, allowing punch and clarity control without heavy-handed EQ moves.
- **Dynamic EQ** — Combines the precision of an EQ with the response of a compressor, reacting only when frequencies exceed a set threshold.
- **Vintage Modules** — Includes Vintage Tape, Vintage EQ, Vintage Compressor, and Vintage Limiter for analog-modeled color and saturation.
- **Tonal Balance Control** — Compares your master's frequency distribution against a target reference range in real time.
