## What Is Martinic?

Martinic is a developer specializing in high-fidelity emulations of vintage electronic instruments and effects. Their catalog focuses on meticulously modeled recreations of classic hardware — from 80s analog synths to Italian tape delays and 60s combo organs. Their flagship product, the AX73, is a detailed analog modeling recreation of the original Akai AX73 synthesizer, updated with modern features. All Martinic plugins are available in VST, VST3, Audio Units, AAX, and CLAP formats.

## Key Features of the Martinic Lineup

- **ACE Modeling Technology** — Martinic uses their proprietary ACE (Analog Circuit Emulation) approach to recreate hardware behavior at the component level.
- **AX73 Synthesizer** — A faithful analog model of the Akai AX73 with expanded polyphony and modern workflow enhancements. Regularly priced at $149.
- **AX Chorus** — A standalone recreation of the chorus effect found on both the AX73 and AX60 synthesizers.
- **AXFX Effect Rack** — An 8-unit multi-effect rack descended from the AX73's built-in external effects section.
- **Retro Pack** — A bundle including Elka Panther (60s Italian combo organ), Kee Bass (Rheem Kee Bass emulation), Lem Echo Music (vintage Italian tape delay), Scanner Vibrato, Pianet T, and Colorsound Tremolo.
- **Preset Collections** — Multiple AX73 sound collections covering genres from epic trance to ambient textures.
