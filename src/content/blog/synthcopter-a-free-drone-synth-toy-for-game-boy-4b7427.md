## What Is Synthcopter?

Synthcopter is a homebrew Game Boy ROM that turns the classic handheld into a drone synthesizer toy. Developed by Ben Vandermeer, it takes inspiration from the retro arcade game Stunt Copter (originally by Duane Blehm) and reimagines the concept with a sound-generating twist. The ROM file runs on original Game Boy hardware as well as software emulators, making it accessible to anyone with a flash cart or a free emulator on their computer. It's released under a **Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0** license, meaning it's free for non-commercial use with attribution.
