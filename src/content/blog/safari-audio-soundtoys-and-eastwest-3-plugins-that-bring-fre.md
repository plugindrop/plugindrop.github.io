## What Are Safari Audio, Soundtoys, and EastWest?

Three very different developers. One common thread: each has built a reputation for tools producers actually use on real sessions.

**Safari Audio** is a boutique plugin developer focused on bringing tactile, character-driven processing to the DAW. Their tools are designed for producers who want something beyond the generic stock plugin.

**Soundtoys** is one of the most recognized names in creative effects. Based in Vermont, they've been building analog-modeled processors for decades — EchoBoy, Decapitator, Little AlterBoy, and the wider Soundtoys 5 suite are fixtures in countless professional mix chains.

**EastWest** is a leading developer of virtual instruments and sample libraries. Their Hollywood series and Composer Cloud subscription are go-to resources for composers and producers working with orchestral, cinematic, and genre-specific sounds.

- **Composer Cloud integration** — many titles available via subscription alongside perpetual licenses
- **Wide genre coverage** — orchestral, cinematic, pop, and world instruments in the catalog
