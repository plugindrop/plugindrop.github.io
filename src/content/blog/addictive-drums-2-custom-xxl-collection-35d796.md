## Ten ADpaks, Ten MIDIpaks, and the Custom XXL Pak Structure
<!-- role:product -->

Ten ADpaks, ten MIDIpaks, and ten Kitpiece Paks sit at the center of the Custom XXL Collection, covering kits, beats, and single drums respectively, all built to run inside the full Addictive Drums 2 Engine. Rather than locking buyers into one fixed set of sounds, the collection lets producers pick their own choice of sounds and rhythms, and those pak selections can be made all at once or spread out and redeemed over time. Layered on top of the ten-pak structure, the bundle also includes a MIDI Library of 5000 beats, speciality "Trig" Sounds, and 130 production-ready presets.

Within the same product line, the Addictive Drums 2 Custom XL Collection also runs on the full Addictive Drums 2 Engine and offers the same choice of sounds and rhythms, but its listed feature set does not carry the ten-ADpak, ten-MIDIpak, ten-Kitpiece-Pak structure, the 5000-beat MIDI library, or the 130 presets that define the XXL tier. A separate Drum Machine Collection bundle takes a different approach altogether, packaging five Roland drum-machine recreations — the TR-808, TR-606, TR-909, TR-707, and TR-727 — rather than kit, beat, and single-drum paks for the Addictive Drums 2 engine.
