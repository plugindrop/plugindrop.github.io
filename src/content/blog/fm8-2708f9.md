## What Is FM8?

FM8 is a software FM synthesizer developed by Native Instruments. It implements frequency modulation synthesis — the same technology that powered classic hardware synths like the Yamaha DX7 — in a modern, feature-rich plugin format. Designed for producers who want deep, complex tonal control, FM8 covers everything from glassy electric pianos and punchy basses to aggressive leads and evolving pads. It runs as a VST/AU/AAX plugin within any compatible DAW.

---

## Key Features

- **8-Operator FM Engine** — A full 8-operator architecture gives you significantly more routing flexibility than classic 4-operator hardware synths, enabling complex, layered FM textures.
- **Morph Section** — Allows real-time blending between preset sounds, useful for live performance or sound design experimentation.
- **Integrated Effects** — FM8 includes a built-in effects chain (reverb, delay, chorus, EQ, and more), reducing the need for external processing during sound design.
- **Expert Mode** — A detailed operator matrix gives advanced users full control over operator routing, feedback, and modulation depth.
- **Arpeggiator** — A built-in arpeggiator with adjustable patterns adds rhythmic and melodic variation without additional plugins.
- **Large Preset Library** — Ships with a substantial factory preset library covering classic FM tones, modern synth sounds, and sound-designed patches.
