## What Is SSL X Limit?

X Limit is a limiter plugin from Solid State Logic, the UK console manufacturer behind decades of analog mixing consoles used in commercial studios. It's part of SSL's X-Series line of native plugins, which are built around simple, fast-to-use interfaces rather than deep parameter menus. The plugin is designed for peak limiting duties on mix buses and masters, giving producers a way to control loudness and transient peaks without opening a full mastering suite.
