## What Is the SSL 4K B Compressor Plugin?

The SSL 4K B is an official compressor plugin from Solid State Logic — the British console manufacturer whose hardware has been a fixture in professional studios for decades. This plugin is a software emulation of the compressor section found in the legendary SSL 4000 series console channel strip. It's designed to bring the character and control of that hardware into a DAW environment, targeting the mixing and tracking workflows where the original hardware made its name.

## Key Features

- **Classic SSL 4000 Series Character** — Modeled after the compressor section of the iconic SSL 4000 B channel strip, delivering that recognizable British console tone.
- **VCA-Style Compression** — Fast, musical VCA circuitry behavior that responds well to both transient shaping and sustained signals.
- **Attack & Release Controls** — Adjustable timing parameters let you dial in compression response from punchy to smooth.
- **Ratio Selection** — Multiple ratio options to handle everything from gentle dynamic control to heavier processing.
- **Make-Up Gain** — Integrated output gain to compensate for gain reduction and keep levels consistent.
- **Sidechain High-Pass Filter** — Helps prevent low-frequency content from over-triggering the compressor, a critical tool on bass-heavy material.
