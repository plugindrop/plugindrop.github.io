## SL 4000 B Circuit Modelling in a DAW

<!-- role:product -->

Released in 1976, the SL 4000 B console is the source design for SSL 4K B. This channel strip plug-in brings that console’s approach into a DAW, combining an EQ and compressor in one processor rather than requiring separate plug-ins for those functions. It is based on the rare SL 4000 B, a console installed in Studio 2, known as the Stone Room, at London’s Townhouse Studios.

The plug-in’s detailed circuit modelling covers the Jensen JE-115K-E Transformer Mic Pre, dbx “black can” 202 VCA fader, 4000 B EQ, and 4000 B Compressor. The EQ precedes the Brown Knob EQ found on the 4000 E. The compressor has a design based on the Bus Compressor, using peak detection and a Feedback VCA topology in the sidechain; it also includes a De-Esser mode.

SSL 4K B integrates with the SSL 360° Plug-in Mixer ecosystem and is controllable from UC1 and UF8 hardware. The supplied video, *SSL 4K B Channel Strip plug-in and history of the SL 4000 B console with Chris Jenkins*, focuses on the channel strip and the history of the SL 4000 B console.

## Peak Detection and the B-Series Sound Direction

<!-- role:sound -->

Peak detection and a Feedback VCA topology in the sidechain distinguish the 4000 B Compressor design. That makes sidechain work a specifically named use for SSL 4K B, while the built-in De-Esser mode extends the compressor section beyond straightforward compression duties.

Its sound character is full of tone, punch, and rich non-linear analogue character. The 4000 B EQ is described as musical, and the plug-in is positioned as a companion to the cleaner 9K-inspired Channel Strip 2. In practical terms, that establishes a clear choice within SSL’s own channel-strip direction: 4K B centers the SL 4000 B lineage and its non-linear analogue character, while Channel Strip 2 is the cleaner 9K-inspired companion.

## Two Listed Compressor Peers, Two Different Briefs

<!-- role:peers -->

Waves CLA-76 emphasizes attack and release times that track drum dynamics, plus an exclusive ALL ratio mode for explosive compression sound. SSL 4K B instead combines its B-series EQ and compressor inside a channel strip, with a Bus Compressor-based design, peak detection, Feedback VCA sidechain topology, and a De-Esser mode.

Waves API 2500 is modelled in close collaboration with API and is an official emulation of the famed API bus compressor. SSL 4K B takes a broader channel-strip route: it models the SL 4000 B console design and includes the 4000 B EQ alongside its compressor section. For producers comparing the listed options, the API 2500’s supplied description centers on a bus compressor, while SSL 4K B’s supplied description centers on the SL 4000 B channel-strip architecture.
