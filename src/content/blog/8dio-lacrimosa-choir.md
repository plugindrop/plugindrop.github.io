## What Lacrimosa Offers

- **200-singer ensemble:** basses, tenors, altos, and sopranos are all covered.
- **True Layered Legato:** velocity-based transitions across dynamic layers.
- **Multi-vowel phrases:** 40+ expressions with phoneme control.
- **Advanced articulations:** staccato, sustain, marcato, and more.
- **Multiple mic positions:** blend close, room, and hall signals.
- **Kontakt 6+ required:** full version only, not the free Player.

### How the specs translate to scoring

- **Section balance:** with 70 basses and 60 tenors against 30 sopranos, the low end carries the weight. That suits power chords and slow, heavy swells. Delicate high-register lines will sit differently than in a soprano-led library.
- **Layered legato:** velocity-driven dynamic layers let a phrase move from soft to loud without an obvious switch. This matters most for long sustained lines under strings and brass.
- **Phoneme control:** vowel and syllable choices shape how the choir blends with an orchestral bed. Open vowels cut through a dense mix, while closed ones sit further back.
- **Mic blending:** close, room, and hall signals let you push the choir forward or back in the space without reaching for extra reverb.

## Best Fit and Poor Fit

**Best fit:**
- Trailer-style cues that need sheer mass and density
- Orchestral underscore where the choir is a main voice, not a pad
- Dramatic game cutscenes and large-scale themes
- Composers who already own a smaller choir and want a big one to complement it

**Poor fit:**
- Solo or small-group vocal textures (Aetheria is the smaller option in this lineup)
- A choir that sits underneath a mix rather than dominating it
- A first choir library on a tight budget with no plan to buy two more titles

### Example scenarios

- **Trailer builder:** a rising 8-bar cue that opens on low sustained basses and tenors, then adds altos and sopranos at the peak. Lacrimosa's section weighting fits this directly.
- **Game composer with mixed needs:** Lacrimosa for boss and battle cues, plus a mid-size or small choir for exploration and quiet scenes. The bundle can cover both in one purchase.
- **Hybrid producer on a budget:** if the main need is soft, lyrical lines, a smaller library is a better match than paying for 200 voices you will rarely use at full scale.
