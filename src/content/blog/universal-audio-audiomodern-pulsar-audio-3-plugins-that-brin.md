## What Is UAD Sound City Studios?

Sound City Studios in Van Nuys, California, is one of the most storied recording facilities in rock history. Universal Audio's plugin version recreates the studio's signature room acoustics, console character, and tape behavior inside your DAW.

UA developed this in collaboration with the studio itself. It's part of their UAD ecosystem, meaning it runs on UAD hardware or within their LUNA recording system. For producers chasing that large-format analog studio feel without leaving the box, this is a direct path there.

## Key Features

- **Neve 8078 Console Emulation** — models the actual console used in the original Sound City tracking room
- **Room Acoustic Capture** — studio-accurate reverb from the live room, not a generic algorithmic approximation
- **Tape Machine Modeling** — adds the warmth and saturation characteristic of analog tape recordings
- **Mic Preamp Character** — emulates the preamp coloration from the studio's original signal chain
- **DAW Integration** — works within UA's plugin ecosystem across supported platforms
