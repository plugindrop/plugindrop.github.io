## Common mistakes when buying synths on sale

- **Treating every sale as urgent.** Recurring sale tiers mean most "last chance" framing is marketing. Check against the recorded history first.
- **Chasing the lowest-ever price.** A rare low is, by definition, unlikely to appear on your schedule.
- **Ignoring the cost of waiting.** If you would use the synth now, the time value of having it can outweigh a modest saving.
- **Buying from memory.** Prices change, so a figure remembered from months ago may no longer apply.
- **Skipping the comparison.** A synth you won't open often is worth less than one that fits your workflow, whatever its discount.
