## What Is Auto-Tune?

Auto-Tune is the industry-defining pitch correction plugin developed by Antares Audio Technologies. First introduced in 1997, it pioneered real-time pitch correction and has since become a fixture in professional studios, home setups, and live rigs worldwide. The Antares catalog spans from entry-level correction tools to full vocal production suites with harmonizing, doubling, and formant shifting capabilities.

---

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Pitch Correction (Auto Mode):** Corrects pitch instantly with adjustable Retune Speed — from transparent natural correction to the hard-tuned aesthetic common in hip-hop and R&B.
- **Graph Mode:** Enables manual, note-by-note pitch editing with surgical precision for demanding vocal sessions.
- **Auto-Key Integration:** Automatically detects the key and scale of your track so correction is applied musically without manual setup.
- **Flex-Tune Technology:** Selectively corrects out-of-tune notes while preserving natural pitch expression in well-performed passages.
- **Formant Correction:** Maintains the natural character of the voice while shifting pitch, reducing unnatural artifacts.
- **Subscription & Perpetual Licensing:** Available as a one-time purchase or monthly/annual subscription depending on product tier.
