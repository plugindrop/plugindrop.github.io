## The Plugin Sale Calendar

Most music producers think of Black Friday as one day. For plugins, it's a six-week event.

| Period | What happens |
|---|---|
| Late October | Teaser "early access" deals from some brands |
| Nov 1–14 | First major sales. Arturia and NI often start here |
| Nov 15–21 | Heaviest wave. Most brands are live |
| Nov 22–29 (BF week) | Peak. FabFilter, Spitfire, u-he go live this week |
| Nov 30 (Cyber Monday) | Some deals expire; others extend |
| December | Most sales continue 1–2 more weeks. NI runs until mid-January |

The practical takeaway: if you see a deal on November 5th, don't buy — almost every brand will match or beat it by November 20th. If something drops on Black Friday itself, wait for Cyber Monday to make sure the price doesn't fall further.

### Native Instruments / Komplete — "Cyber Season" Nov 1 – Mid-January

Native Instruments doesn't use the term Black Friday. They call their annual sale **Cyber Season**, and it runs longer than any other brand in this guide.

**Cyber Season 2023: Nov 1, 2023 – Jan 15, 2024**
**Cyber Season 2024: Nov 28 – Dec 15, 2024**

**Key details:**
- 50% off **full versions** of Komplete bundles
- Upgrades and updates are typically **not** included in the discount
- Individual plugins: 40–50% off depending on year
- Free plugins given away during the campaign

**2024 pricing (50% off full versions):**
- Komplete 15 Select: $99 (reg. $199)
- Komplete 15 Standard: ~$200 (reg. $399)
- Komplete 15 Ultimate: ~$300+ (reg. $599+)

**Strategy:** If you already own a previous version of Komplete, check whether upgrade pricing is included — in 2024 it wasn't, only fresh purchases. The cheapest route to Komplete is often buying a hardware bundle that includes Select, then upgrading.

**Verdict: 4/5 for full version buyers.** The Cyber Season window is huge, so there's no rush. But the upgrade exclusion is a meaningful limitation.

### Spitfire Audio — 50% Off + 20% Extra for Email Subscribers

Spitfire runs a clean, consistent Black Friday sale with one differentiator: email subscribers get an extra 20% off code, making the effective discount **up to 70% off** for some products.

**BF 2023: Nov 23 – Dec 1 (including BBCSO)**
**BF 2024: similar window, up to 50% off sitewide**

**Key products included:** BBC Symphony Orchestra, Albion series, Abbey Road products, LABS bundle content.

**Extra 20% math:**
- BBCSO Core normally $349 → 50% off = $175 → additional 20% email code = **$140 effective price**
- The non-subscriber price is good; the subscriber price is exceptional

**Strategy:** Subscribe to Spitfire's email list at least 2 weeks before November. The extra 20% code comes via email and is typically time-limited to the first few days of the sale.

**Verdict: 5/5 if you're on the email list. 3/5 if you're not.** The spread between subscriber and non-subscriber prices is the largest of any brand on this list.

### Waves — 78–92% Off, But Their Pricing Is Unusual

Waves has a complex pricing model. Most individual plugins list at $149–$299 but sell for $20–$45 year-round in flash sales. Black Friday is when they claim "lowest ever prices" and plugins hit $5.99–$29.99.

**BF 2024: "up to 92% off bundles, up to 90% off plugins" (Nov 18–24 plus free plugin drop on BF day)**

Key caveats with Waves:
- The "regular" price on their site is not the price anyone actually pays
- Identical discounts appear 3–4 times a year (BF, summer sale, end of year)
- Bundle pricing is the real story — Platinum bundle hit $99–$199 in aggressive promos

**Verdict: 3/5 as a BF-specific deal, because these prices appear regularly.** If you want Waves plugins, just wait for any sale, not specifically Black Friday.

## Brands That Never Go On Sale

Some plugins have never discounted — not at Black Friday, not at any other time. This isn't a rumor; it's a documented business decision from the developers.

### Valhalla DSP
ValhallaRoom ($50), VintageVerb ($50), Delay ($50) — unchanged since launch. The developer has stated publicly that the $50 price point is the sale price. There is no Black Friday discount, no bundle deal, no coupon code. If you want Valhalla plugins, buy them when you need them.

### Xfer Serum
Serum sells for $189 and has never been discounted. Serum 2 (the update) continues this policy. No Black Friday, no summer sale, no bundle. The only way to get Serum cheaper than $189 is through a hardware bundle that includes it.

### iZotope (most products)
iZotope does run sales, but they're unpredictable in timing and depth. Their Elements tiers (Ozone Elements, etc.) occasionally hit $29 at Black Friday. The full suite products (Ozone 11 Advanced, RX 11 Advanced) discount during specific campaigns but not always at BF.

## FAQ

**Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?**
If it's October or early November and you don't need it immediately, wait. Most deals start by November 15. For FabFilter specifically, buying at any other time of year means paying full price when 25% off is coming in a few weeks.

**Do these deals apply to Plugin Boutique?**
Yes. Most plugin brand BF sales are reflected on Plugin Boutique at the same price. The advantage of buying through PB is earning loyalty points on each purchase, which compounds if you buy several things during the sale window.

**What if I miss the sale?**
FabFilter typically has a second sale window around the winter holidays. Arturia and NI also run January/February sales. The u-he 50% window is the hardest to catch — if you miss it, you might wait until next November.

**Is there anything that goes deeper than 50% off?**
Waves routinely hits 80–90% during BF (though they also discount this heavily the rest of the year). Spitfire with the subscriber code gets into the 60–70% range. Beyond that, look for third-party retailers like Audiodeluxe, which occasionally offer site-specific deals that beat the official brand price.
