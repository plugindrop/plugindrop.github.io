## What Is XLN Audio RC-20 Retro Color?

RC-20 Retro Color is a vintage effects plugin by XLN Audio, the studio behind Addictive Drums and Addictive Keys. It's built to add analog warmth, lo-fi texture, and retro degradation to any signal — synths, vocals, drums, or full mixes. Instead of a single effect, it combines multiple signal-coloring modules into one interface, letting producers go from subtle vinyl warmth to heavy tape destruction without chaining separate plugins.

## Key Features

- **Noise Module** — Introduces vinyl crackle, tape hiss, and ambient room textures for authentic analog atmosphere.
- **Wobble Module** — Simulates pitch instability and flutter, replicating the mechanical imperfections of cassette decks and reel-to-reel machines.
- **Distortion Module** — Harmonic saturation ranging from subtle tube warmth to full lo-fi crunch.
- **Space Module** — Built-in reverb for adding vintage room depth and spatial character.
- **Flux Module** — LFO-driven modulation and randomization that adds organic unpredictability to the signal.
- **Compress/Limit Section** — Onboard dynamics control to glue degraded signals and manage levels.
