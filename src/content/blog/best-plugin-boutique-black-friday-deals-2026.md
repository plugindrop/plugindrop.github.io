## UAD Ampex ATR 102 Mastering Tape Recorder
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Ampex ATR 102 Mastering Tape Recorder -->

The Ampex ATR-102 plug-in suits producers and mixing/mastering engineers who want to add analog tape character to a mix or master, available for both Apollo Realtime/UAD-2 hardware and as a UAD Native plug-in across Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, and Studio One. Users can select between tape types and color settings to shape saturation and harmonics, route the full ATR-102 circuit including its transformers and amplifiers, and use Auto-Gain to keep Repro and Record levels properly staged while adjusting the sound.

Soundtoys Decapitator suits mixers who want to add analog-style saturation and character to vocals, drums, or synths without routing complex sub-mixes. They can dial in one of five saturation models, shape the result with the built-in tone control, and use the mix knob to blend in dry signal for parallel processing, reaching for the Punish button when they want the effect pushed to an extreme.

## Virtual Guitarist Bundle
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Virtual Guitarist Bundle -->

The Virtual Guitarist Bundle suits guitarists and producers who want ready-made, realistic rhythm guitar accompaniment without hiring a session player, especially for quickly adding guitar parts in various styles to a track. Users can load one of the five instruments (SPARKLE 2, IRON 2, CARBON, AMBER 2, or SILK 2) as a VST2 or AU2 plugin and pick from more than 30 styles and 350 phrases, using Instrument mode, picking and strumming combinations, the Open Chords feature, and the stomp box and Finisher effects to shape organic, distorted, or processed guitar sounds.

## Soundtoys PanMan
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Soundtoys PanMan -->

PanMan suits producers and mix engineers who want panning to feel like part of the performance rather than a static placement choice, such as making a sound sweep whenever the snare hits or tying pan movement to a vocal's loudness. They can use its Step or Shape rhythm modes to build tempo-locked patterns, set a trigger threshold so the track's own dynamics drive width, speed, and offset, and dial in one of the seven analog modes for added saturation when pushed harder.

## SubLab XL
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:SubLab XL -->

SubLab XL suits producers working on macOS (10.12+, Intel or Apple Silicon) or Windows (8 and higher) who want a sub-bass synth with expanded sound-shaping tools like added FX, modulation, and macros. They can use it as a VST2, VST3, AU, or AAX plugin within their existing DAW setup on either platform.

## DeNoizzer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:DeNoizzer -->

DeNoizzer suits producers and engineers cleaning up imperfect recordings, such as removing electronic hum, traffic rumble, or room ambience, without dulling the rest of the mix. They can use the Learn button on an isolated section of the noise for a few seconds to capture its profile, then shape the result with Threshold, Speed, and the High and Low Pass Filters, checking progress on the live spectrum display or by toggling AB comparison.
