## Xpand!2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Xpand!2 -->

This plugin suits producers and composers who need a quick multitimbral workstation for bass, pad, or sound-design parts without assembling multiple separate instruments. Each patch lets them build up to four parts with independent MIDI channels, note ranges, and polyphonic (up to 64 voices) or monophonic voice modes, then sculpt the results with six context-sensitive smart knobs, tempo-syncable arpeggiation, and the built-in effects processors.

## SubLab XL
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:SubLab XL -->

SubLab XL suits producers working on macOS (10.12+, Intel or Apple Silicon) or Windows (8 and higher) who want a sub-bass synth with expanded sound-shaping tools like added FX, modulation, and macros. They can use it as a VST2, VST3, AU, or AAX plugin within their existing DAW setup on either platform.

## Bloom KSHMR
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Bloom KSHMR -->

Bloom KSHMR suits producers working in Indian, South Asian, or Bollywood-inspired styles who want authentic percussion, strings, and vocal textures without sourcing samples themselves. They can dial in a vibe fast by browsing the 250 KSHMR-designed presets, then shape the result with the Lo-Fi, Tube, Width, and Space macros or the rearrangeable Noise, Reverb, and Dynamics effects, importing their own samples if they want a fully personalized kit.

## DeNoizzer
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:DeNoizzer -->

DeNoizzer suits producers and engineers cleaning up imperfect recordings, such as removing electronic hum, traffic rumble, or room ambience, without dulling the rest of the mix. They can use the Learn button on an isolated section of the noise for a few seconds to capture its profile, then shape the result with Threshold, Speed, and the High and Low Pass Filters, checking progress on the live spectrum display or by toggling AB comparison.

## Motion: Fractal
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Motion: Fractal -->

Motion: Fractal suits sound designers who want to turn static sounds into evolving granular textures, pulsating rhythms, or glitched-out sequences. They can shape results with the built-in trance gate and stutter repeats, control parameters directly with the cursor, and route effects flexibly while watching the modulation respond in the immersive 3D visualisation.

## Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ -->

Cherry Audio's Blue3 suits organists and keyboardists working across blues, gospel, rock, and jazz who want authentic tonewheel organ tones with rotary speaker and vibrato character rather than samples. They can shape their sound using the Main, Edit, Cabinet, and Perform pages—adjusting drawbars, percussion, key click, and overdrive, then finishing with the built-in tape echo, reverb, and graphic EQ for stage or studio use.

## Motion Bundle Upgrade from any Motion product
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Motion Bundle Upgrade from any Motion product -->

This plugin suits producers who want to go beyond basic effects and shape sound with distortion, bitcrushing, delays, reverbs, and granular textures across a combined toolset. They can use it by routing audio through its FX sections, mapping macro sliders to parameters, and drawing on its preset library to quickly build layered, evolving sound design.

## Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B -->

Wurlybird 140B suits players and producers chasing the classic Wurlitzer electric piano sound for rock and pop tracks, since it's modeled on the sought-after 140B and carries the same rich, distinctive tone heard on hits by artists from Ray Charles to Billie Eilish. They can shape that sound further with the built-in Vibrato/Tremolo, Tune, Hum, and Velocity Curve controls, the stompbox-style effects chain (including Overdrive, Chorus, Delay, and Reverb), and the 50 included presets covering classic to modern tones.

## Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Crumar Spirit -->

This plugin suits synthesizer enthusiasts and sound designers who want classic analog monosynth tones with modern flexibility, from vintage-style leads and basses to experimental drones and textures. Users can switch between monophonic, unison, polyphonic (4, 8, or 16 voice), and cycle voice modes, shape sound with the dual oscillators, Upper and Lower filters, and twin ADSR envelopes, add movement through the Mod X and Shaper Y sections or the four-slot Matrix Z mod matrix, and finish with the built-in distortion, flanger/chorus, echo, and reverb effects, all while working from over 430 presets or driving the arpeggiator's Ripple, Arp, and Leap modes for rhythmic variation.

## Lifeline Bundle
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Lifeline Bundle -->

This plugin suits producers who want a modular, drag-and-drop effects chain covering re-amping, saturation, bit reduction, stereo width, and reverb within one resizable, zero-latency interface. They can route audio through its five effects modules individually or in combination (up to 1024 configurations), using the included presets or building custom multiband chains with the gain, tone, and dry/wet controls on each module.

## Arturia Analog Lab Pro
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Arturia Analog Lab Pro -->

Analog Lab Pro suits producers, performers, and sound designers who want fast access to vintage and modern synth and keyboard tones without hunting through individual instruments. Users browse the over 2000 curated V Collection and Pigments presets by type, style, or genre, then shape sounds instantly with the four macro controls, or combine two presets as layered or split Multis in the Studio view.

## Kilohearts Phase Plant
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Kilohearts Phase Plant -->

Phase Plant suits sound designers and music producers who want to build original patches from scratch rather than relying on presets, since its open modular architecture lets them freely combine generators, modulators, and effects. Users work within this semi-modular environment to design anything from compact synths to complex evolving patches, refining their own unique sound with precision in a workflow built to be intuitive.

Phase Plant suits sound designers and music producers who want to build original synth sounds from scratch rather than tweak presets. With its open modular architecture, they can combine any number of generators, modulators, and effects to design patches ranging from simple synths to complex evolving sounds.

## UAD Moog Minimoog
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Moog Minimoog -->

This plugin suits performing synth players and producers chasing the classic Minimoog Model D sound who want near-zero-latency, exacting emulation of its oscillators and ladder filter for real-time playing and tracking. They can use it directly inside a supported DAW (Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Studio One, Cubase, Pro Tools, or LUNA) via Audio Units, VST 3, or AAX, exploring its self-oscillation, extreme resonance, and custom mods such as switchable note priority, legato, and the extra LFO to expand on the original hardware's sonic palette.

## UAD Opal Morphing Synth
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Opal Morphing Synth -->

Opal suits producers and sound designers who want big, record-ready analog-meets-wavetable sounds without getting lost in deep synthesis, thanks to morphing filters, oscillators, LFOs, noise, and curated presets for fast genre-spanning use. They can shape and finish sounds further using the onboard UA effects, including vintage spring reverb, tape delay, modulation, and 1176-style compression.

## UAD Ampex ATR 102 Mastering Tape Recorder
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Ampex ATR 102 Mastering Tape Recorder -->

The Ampex ATR-102 plug-in suits producers and mixing/mastering engineers who want to add analog tape character to a mix or master, available for both Apollo Realtime/UAD-2 hardware and as a UAD Native plug-in across Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, and Studio One. Users can select between tape types and color settings to shape saturation and harmonics, route the full ATR-102 circuit including its transformers and amplifiers, and use Auto-Gain to keep Repro and Record levels properly staged while adjusting the sound.

## Softube Tape
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Softube Tape -->

Softube Tape suits computer-based mix engineers and producers who want their digital mixes to sound more cohesive and "glued together" rather than like a collection of separate tracks. They can place a single instance on the master bus to emulate mixing down to a stereo tape machine, or apply it more heavily across individual tracks for a vintage-style sound, choosing from three tape machine types and adjusting the Amount knob to taste.

## UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator -->

The UAD EMT 140 Classic Plate Reverberator suits mixers and producers who want the warm, authentic plate reverb sound heard on iconic recordings, particularly for making lead vocal tracks stand out in a dense mix. They can shape that tone using the plug-in's damping and filter controls, along with its Balance, Width, and Modulation settings.

## UAD Manley Vari Mu
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Manley Vari Mu -->

The UAD Manley Vari Mu suits mixing and mastering engineers who need glue and tonal cohesion across individual tracks, stereo buses, or a full mix. They can use it by linking channels for a matched stereo image, choosing Left/Right or Mid/Side operation to shape stereo imaging, and dialing in the plug-in's Dry/Wet Mix control for parallel compression.

## UNMIX::DRUMS
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UNMIX::DRUMS -->

UNMIX::DRUMS suits producers, mixers, and remixers who need to shape drum presence within an already-mixed track or drum bus rather than working from individual stems. They can boost drums by up to +18dB or pull them down toward near-complete removal using frequency-dependent controls, the sub-bass resynthesizer, and SMOOTH or PUNCHY boost modes to rebalance a full mix for mastering, remixing, or sample-based production.

## BALANCE
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:BALANCE -->

BALANCE suits mix engineers, sound designers, composers, and producers who want a fast, low-overhead way to adjust overall tonal balance across a session. Users adjust the target curve with the SLOPE, WEIGHTING, BASS, and AIR controls, set the overall EQ amount and wet gain, and apply 12dB/Octave low-cut and high-cut filters, optionally switching to the compact EASY view when they don't need the Metal/Direct 3D accelerated analyzer on screen.

## INTENSITY 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:INTENSITY 2 -->

INTENSITY 2 suits mixing and mastering engineers as well as sound designers who want to add detail, clarity, and perceived loudness without juggling a complex interface. They can use its single main control alongside mid/side processing and an optional soft-knee saturating limiter across up to 16 channels, running as a 64-bit AAX Native, AU, or VST 3 plugin.

## ORANGE VOCODER IV
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:ORANGE VOCODER IV -->

Orange Vocoder IV suits producers and sound designers who want vocal effects, robotic or creature voices, and mutated grooves, thanks to its 24 vocoding algorithms plus formant shifting, expansion, and stereo operation. They can shape sound further with the rewritten synthesizer section (Unison, Mono Legato, thru-zero linear FM), the new MIDI-controlled pitch quantizer with grain morphing, and the streamlined multi-view UI with module sub-presets and smart randomization, running as AU, AAX Native, VST 2.4, or VST 3 on macOS or Windows.
