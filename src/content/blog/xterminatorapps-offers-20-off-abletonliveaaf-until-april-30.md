## What Is Abletonlive.aaf?

Abletonlive.aaf is a standalone desktop utility by XterminatorApps that converts AAF (Advanced Authoring Format) files directly into Ableton Live project files (.als). Ableton Live has never natively supported AAF import — a long-standing pain point for anyone moving sessions between Pro Tools, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Media Composer and Ableton. This tool bridges that gap, turning what used to be hours of manual track rebuilding into a near-instant conversion.

## Key Features

- **Frame-accurate audio clip placement** — Transfers clip positions from your AAF timeline directly into the Ableton session with precise timing preserved.
- **Volume automation mapping** — Automation data gets translated to Ableton mixer tracks, so your level rides carry over.
- **Clip gain preservation** — Individual clip gain values are maintained on each audio clip during conversion.
- **Fade and crossfade handling** — Fades and crossfades from your original session are reconstructed in the Ableton project.
- **Marker import** — Session markers transfer over, keeping your timeline navigation intact.
- **Track naming and structure** — Track names and layout from the source AAF are preserved, so you're not staring at a wall of unnamed tracks.
