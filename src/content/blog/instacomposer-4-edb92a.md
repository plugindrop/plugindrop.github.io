## MIDI Engine 2.0 Drives InstaComposer 4's Generation Modes
<!-- role:product -->

InstaComposer 4 centers on a newly reworked generation engine, MIDI Engine 2.0, which handles rhythm, timing, phrasing, pitch selection and harmonic cohesion across every mode in the plugin. Melody, Ostinato, Chord, Rhythm and Drums modes have each been refined under this engine, and two further modes, Arpeggiator and Rhythm V2, extend the melodic and rhythmic material it can produce. Musical ideas come from configurable rules, algorithms and parameters rather than an automated process, and generated ideas can be reworked again with a single button press. Full-song generation assembles intros, verses, choruses, bridges and outros in one step, drawing on up to six independent tracks and up to eight scenes per song section; the maker describes the resulting arrangements as more varied and cohesive than before. Foliage adds complementary notes around a main melody, Syncopation, Gallop, Density, Burst and Congestion controls shape the groove of generated patterns, and Humanize adds variation to timing and velocity. Timeline Playback lets a producer jump straight to any point in a composition for faster auditioning, and a built-in chord and scale analysis tool identifies chords, progressions and tonal centres from existing MIDI, suggesting alternative harmonic candidates to explore. Generated or imported MIDI can be edited directly inside the plugin, with range controls to keep notes within a chosen register, and SoundFont support lets a producer load their own SF2 instruments alongside the built-in acoustic piano and General MIDI-style collection. Where Effectrix 2 works as a 32-step effect sequencer processing incoming audio through 14 effects, and Minimal Audio Lucid rebuilds incoming sound grain by grain and retunes it to a chosen key and scale, InstaComposer 4 does not touch an audio signal at all: its output is MIDI note data for melodies, chords, rhythms and full songs, generated from configurable rules rather than derived from existing audio.
