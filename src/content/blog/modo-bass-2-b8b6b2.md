## What Is MODO BASS 2?

MODO BASS 2 is a virtual bass instrument made by IK Multimedia. Instead of playing back recorded samples, it uses physical modeling technology to generate the sound of strings, body, and pickups in real time. That approach lets producers change instrument, pickup, and playing technique after a part is already written, without re-recording or re-sampling anything.

## Key Features

- **Physical modeling engine** — Sound is generated algorithmically rather than triggered from samples, which keeps the plugin's install footprint small compared to multi-gigabyte sample-based bass libraries.
- **Multiple bass models and body types** — The instrument includes a selection of modeled basses covering different pickup configurations and tonal characters; check the product page for the exact model list.
- **Adjustable playing technique** — Fingerstyle, pick, slap, and muting-style articulations can be dialed in and edited independently of the notes already played.
- **Built-in amp and cabinet simulation** — Signal routing includes amp and cab modeling so the instrument can go from clean DI tone to amped-up bass without a separate rig plugin.
- **Mixer and effects section** — An onboard mixer lets you shape tone and blend mic/DI signals without leaving the plugin.
- **Standalone and plugin formats** — Runs as a standalone app and as a plugin; confirm supported formats (VST/AU/AAX) on the product page for your DAW.
