## What Is the UAD Enigmatic '82 Overdrive Special Amp?

The UAD Enigmatic '82 Overdrive Special Amp is a brand-new native guitar amp simulation plug-in from Universal Audio, added to the company's UAD Native lineup. It's built around the circuit design of a classic D-style amplifier — a reference to one of the most sought-after and expensive hand-wired amp lineages in guitar history, known for touch-sensitive, harmonically rich breakup tones. The plug-in runs natively on macOS and Windows, with no UAD Apollo interface or DSP hardware required to use it.

Universal Audio has built its reputation on detailed circuit-level modeling, and this release continues that approach for the native (non-DSP) format.
