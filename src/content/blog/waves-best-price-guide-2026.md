## FAQ

**How often do Waves plugins go on sale?**
Waves runs major sitewide sales 8–12 times per year. The most reliable windows are Black Friday (November), the Summer Sale (June–July), and a Spring promotion (March–April). Flash deals on individual plugins appear outside these windows as well — sometimes weekly. Email lists from both Waves and Plugin Boutique are the most reliable notification channels.

**Is buying through Plugin Boutique safer than buying directly from Waves?**
Yes — Plugin Boutique is an authorized Waves reseller with strong consumer protections, and their Virtual Cash system adds value that you don't get buying direct. Your license is still registered through Waves Central regardless of where you purchase.

**Should I use Waves Creative Access or buy perpetual licenses?**
Creative Access makes the most sense as a temporary evaluation tool. Use it for 3–6 months to identify which plugins you consistently reach for, then buy those perpetually on sale. Long-term, perpetual ownership is cheaper than indefinite subscription payments.

**What's the actual difference between Waves Gold and Platinum bundles?**
Gold covers the core mixing toolkit: Renaissance series, SSL G-Master, C4 Multiband, and 40+ processors. Platinum adds mastering and advanced production tools: L3 Multimaximizer, IR-L Convolution Reverb, InPhase, and several additional dynamics processors. If you master your own work, Platinum is worth the price gap.

**Do Waves plugins require an internet connection to run?**
No — Waves plugins use offline licensing via the Waves Central app. You authorize your machine during installation, and the plugins run fully offline afterward. Waves Central also manages updates, installs, and license transfers.
