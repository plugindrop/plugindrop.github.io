## What FabFilter Pro-Q 4 actually does

Understanding what you're paying for helps you decide whether a deal is worth waiting for. Pro-Q 4 is FabFilter's flagship equalizer, and it is generally used for:

- **Surgical corrective EQ:** cutting resonances, harsh frequencies, and low-end mud with many bands and precise control.
- **Dynamic EQ:** letting a band react to the signal, so a cut or boost only applies when a frequency gets too loud.
- **Mid/side and stereo-placement processing:** shaping the center and the sides of a mix separately.
- **Linear-phase processing:** useful on mastering-style tasks where phase shifts between bands are a concern.
- **Visual analysis:** a large, interactive spectrum display that makes it easier to see what you're correcting.

If your workflow leans on any of those, the plugin tends to earn its cost quickly. If you only need basic low-cut and tone-shaping moves, a stock DAW equalizer may be enough, and paying any price for Pro-Q 4 is less urgent.
