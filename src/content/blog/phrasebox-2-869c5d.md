## What Is Phrasebox 2?

Phrasebox 2 is a MIDI plugin designed to generate melodic phrases, arpeggios, and patterned sequences inside your DAW. It operates as a MIDI effect — meaning it feeds structured melodic output into whatever synth or instrument plugin you already own. The tool sits in the Music Theory Tools category, built for producers who want to create moving, harmonically grounded lines without manually plotting every note in the piano roll. Its focus is phrase generation driven by a central arpeggiator engine, with modulation tools layered on top for added movement.
