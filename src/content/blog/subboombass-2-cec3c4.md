## Karplus-Strong String Synthesis Expands Rob Papen's Bass Engine
<!-- role:product -->

Rob Papen's SubBoomBass 2 pairs analogue modelled waveforms with new spectrum waveforms, high-quality samples, and Karplus-Strong string synthesis, combining the original SubBoomBass's tone-shaping tools with engines absent from earlier versions. The interface was redesigned for clearer control over its parameters, and a resizable GUI supports 100%, 125%, 150%, and 200% scaling for different screen sizes.

A new control menu in the upper-right corner handles global settings and update checks, while the oscillator, filter, and sequencer sections of the top panel were reworked. An X/Y screen gives performers a two-dimensional control surface, and a pattern mode runs four sequences at once, extending step-sequencing beyond a single pattern. The sequencer also adds a ratcheting feature for subdividing individual steps, and a grain sample mode applies granular resynthesis to the instrument's sample library.

New zero-latency filter types and ring modulation sit alongside a post-filter distortion mode, plus clipper, tremolo, and vibrato effects. Monophonic play modes favoring the low or high note, MIDI MPE compatibility, and additional modulation sources and destinations round out the sound-shaping options. The Bank Manager carries over from the original SubBoomBass with a star feature for marking favorite presets, and every original SubBoomBass preset ships alongside a large set of new ones.

NKS compatibility lets the instrument map directly to NKS-enabled hardware controllers. SubBoomBass 2 installs as VST, AU, and AAX on Mac and supports Windows 7, 8, and 10 in 32- and 64-bit versions, with registration handled through a Rob Papen account using a 32-digit serial number tied to the user's Plugin Boutique and Rob Papen accounts.
