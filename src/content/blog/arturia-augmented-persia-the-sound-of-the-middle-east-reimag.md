## What Is Arturia Augmented PERSIA?

Arturia Augmented PERSIA is a hybrid software instrument built on the same architecture as Arturia's acclaimed Augmented series — the line that blends deeply sampled acoustic instruments with a proper synthesis engine underneath. This entry focuses specifically on the tonal world of Persian and broader Middle Eastern music: instruments like oud, saz, and kamancheh recorded and layered with modern sound-design tools. It sits alongside Augmented STRINGS, Augmented BRASS, and Augmented VOICES in Arturia's software catalog.
