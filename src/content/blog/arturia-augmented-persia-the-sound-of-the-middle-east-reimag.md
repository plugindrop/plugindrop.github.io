## What Is Arturia Augmented PERSIA?

Arturia Augmented PERSIA uses the same architecture as the Augmented series, which pairs deeply sampled acoustic instruments with a synthesis engine. This entry focuses on the tonal world of Persian and broader Middle Eastern music: oud, saz and kamancheh-style strings, recorded and layered with modern sound-design tools. It sits next to Augmented STRINGS, Augmented BRASS and Augmented VOICES in Arturia's software catalog.

Middle Eastern hybrid instruments are a thin category with almost no direct competition. If you need this palette, the real decision is when to pay, not whether something better is coming. The [When to Buy](#when-to-buy) section covers that.

## What's Inside the Instrument

Based on Arturia's published description of the Augmented engine, these are the main building blocks:

- **Hybrid dual-layer engine.** Real acoustic samples sit alongside synthesis layers. You can blend organic and electronic textures in one patch instead of stacking separate plugins.
- **Middle Eastern instrument library.** It is built around recorded Persian and regional instruments (oud, saz, kamancheh-family strings). That regional focus is what separates it from generic "ethnic" sample packs.
- **Modulation system.** Deep routing supports evolving, expressive textures rather than static loops.
- **Integrated effects.** Built-in reverb, delay and modulation let you shape the sound inside the plugin.
- **Macro controls.** Quick-access knobs cover otherwise complex parameter sets. They suit live tweaking and DAW automation.
- **Arturia Software Center (ASC).** Licensing and activation run through ASC, so there is no iLok dongle to manage.

### How the hybrid design helps in practice

- **Scoring.** Keep the acoustic layer prominent for melodic lines, then bring in the synth layer for a pad or drone under an orchestral bed.
- **Fusion and global bass.** Blend the plucked or bowed sample layer with a synthesized sub or texture so one patch carries both the regional character and the low end.
- **Ambient work.** Use the modulation and macros to turn a short phrase into a slowly evolving texture.
