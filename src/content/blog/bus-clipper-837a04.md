## What Is Bus Clipper?

Bus Clipper is a dynamics processor plug-in built specifically for hard clipping applications on mix buses, stem buses, and master chains. Rather than using traditional limiting algorithms, it applies clip-based peak reduction — a technique that shaves transient peaks with minimal impact on the perceived loudness character of a mix. It sits in the "transparent aggression" camp of dynamics tools: push levels harder without the pumping or squash that can come from standard limiters.

---

## Key Features

- **Hard Clip Engine** — Applies a clean clip ceiling to audio peaks, designed for bus-level use where transparency matters.
- **Oversampling** — Higher internal processing resolution to minimize aliasing artifacts that can color the signal at high gain reduction settings.
- **Soft Knee Option** — Allows a gradual transition into the clip ceiling for a smoother, less surgical response when needed.
- **Gain Staging Controls** — Input and output gain on the same panel, letting you drive into the clipper and trim back for gain-matched comparisons.
- **Clip Metering** — Visual feedback showing exactly when and how hard the ceiling is engaged, useful for dialing in subtle vs. aggressive settings.
- **Streamlined UI** — Single-purpose workflow with a focused interface — not a Swiss Army knife, just a well-designed clipper.

---

## Who Is It For?

- **Mix engineers working on dense, loud productions** — Clipping is a widely-used technique in modern pop, EDM, and hip-hop to add perceived loudness without sacrificing punch.
- **Mastering engineers** — A clipper before or after a limiter is a common mastering chain configuration; Bus Clipper targets this precise use case.
- **Producers managing stem buses** — Parallel processing on drums, bass, or vocals benefits from this kind of transparent peak control.
- **Anyone exploring alternatives to limiters** — Clipping can yield a different dynamic feel compared to lookahead limiting; this tool is built to let you explore that difference intentionally.
