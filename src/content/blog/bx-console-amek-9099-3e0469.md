## What Is the bx console AMEK 9099?

The bx console AMEK 9099 is a channel strip plugin by Brainworx (Plugin Alliance), built as a precise emulation of the AMEK 9099 analog console — a desk designed by Rupert Neve. It packages EQ, dynamics, and analog saturation into a single channel strip, modeled from the actual hardware. Brainworx is widely known for their console emulation line, and the AMEK 9099 sits alongside their SSL, Neve, and API channel strip recreations.

---

## Key Features

- **TMT (Tolerance Modeling Technology):** Brainworx's proprietary system models the small component variations between individual hardware channels, so each instance in your session sounds slightly different — exactly like tracking through a real console.
- **Full Channel Strip:** Combines EQ, compressor, gate/expander, and analog saturation in one plugin, covering the complete signal path of the original desk.
- **Rupert Neve Console Heritage:** The AMEK 9099 hardware was designed by Neve himself, and this emulation targets the characteristic warmth and punch associated with that lineage.
- **Mono and Stereo Operation:** Supports both mono and stereo instances to match real-world routing flexibility.
- **Multiple Plugin Formats:** Available in VST2, VST3, AU, and AAX — covering all major DAWs on both Windows and macOS.

## Who Is It For?

- **Mixing engineers seeking analog console character:** Designed to impart the tonal qualities of a Rupert Neve-engineered desk without the hardware cost.
- **Producers building dense arrangements:** Channel strip plugins with TMT help differentiate multiple similar sources (drums, layered synths) in a mix.
- **Home studio owners who track in-the-box:** The saturation and dynamics section add the kind of subtle glue that purely digital signal chains often lack.
- **Console emulation collectors:** If you already use Brainworx SSL or Neve strips, the AMEK 9099 fills out a different tonal flavor in your toolkit.
