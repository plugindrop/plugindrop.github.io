## What Is Inner Pitch?

Inner Pitch is a pitch-shifting effect plugin developed by Auburn Sounds. Its core design philosophy centers on preserving the natural character of source material — pitch changes that sound musical rather than robotic. Destruction and experimental effects are available, but they're opt-in rather than the default. The plugin shipped its 2.0 update as a free upgrade, which improved pitch-shifting accuracy and added a hard-tune robot pitch correction mode for Full Edition users.

## Key Features

- **High-quality pitch shifting** — Designed to maintain the integrity of the original performance across a wide pitch range.
- **Formant shifting** — Adjust formants independently from pitch for more natural vocal transformations.
- **Internal delay unit** — Includes ping-pong mode, diffusion, and infinite feedback for creating endless ascending or descending pitch effects.
- **3 types of in-loop distortion** — Includes a unique ADPCM-like game codec distortion for lo-fi character.
- **3-band EQ in the feedback loop** — Shape the tone of the pitch-shifted signal directly within the processing chain.
- **Stereo widening** — Uses L/R pitch offset to create width without traditional stereo tricks.
