## What Is the NAP Works Devious Pocket?

The NAP Works Devious Pocket is a compact standalone device designed to run a Digital Vinyl System (DVS) setup without a laptop. DVS technology lets DJs control digital audio files using traditional vinyl records or timecode CDs — but until now, that workflow almost always demanded a laptop running software like Serato or Traktor. The Devious Pocket aims to change that by putting the processing power directly into a small, dedicated hardware unit.

NAP Works is positioning this as a genuine laptop replacement for vinyl-focused DJs, not just a companion gadget.

- **Standalone DVS Processing** — Handles timecode signal interpretation internally, eliminating the laptop from the signal chain entirely.
