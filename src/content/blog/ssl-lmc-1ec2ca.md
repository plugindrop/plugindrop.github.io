## What Is the SSL LMC?

The SSL LMC is a plugin emulation of the Listen Mic Compressor circuit built into Solid State Logic's legendary 4000-series console. Originally designed as a utility circuit for talkback and studio communication, producers discovered its extreme, fast-acting compression character made it unusually powerful on drums and parallel bus duties. SSL — the company behind the consoles that defined the sound of major-label records for decades — developed this plugin to bring that circuit to the box.
