## What Is Trip Hop EZkeys MIDI?

Trip Hop EZkeys MIDI is a genre-specific MIDI pattern pack from Toontrack, the Swedish developer behind the EZkeys piano and keyboard instrument. The pack is built to work natively inside EZkeys, delivering keyboard and piano phrases that capture the signature sound of trip hop: minor-key tension, spacious voicings, and slow melodic movement that sits naturally under atmospheric, beat-driven production.

Toontrack's EZkeys MIDI format goes beyond raw loops — patterns are organized by song structure so you can assemble a complete arrangement quickly.
