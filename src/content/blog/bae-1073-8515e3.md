## What Is BAE 1073?

BAE Audio (Brent Averill Enterprises) built its name manufacturing hardware reproductions of the original Neve 1073 console module — a circuit design that has shaped commercial recordings since the early 1970s. The BAE 1073 plugin is a software emulation of that hardware: it replicates the transformer-based coloration, stepped EQ curves, and signal character of BAE's own physical unit. It sits in the EQ category on Plugin Boutique, though the 1073 circuit functions as much as a tone-shaping and coloration tool as it does a corrective equalizer.

## Key Features

- **Three-band EQ modeling**: Captures the high-shelf, switchable mid-peak, and low-shelving structure of the original 1073 circuit, including its characteristic stepped frequency selections.
- **High-pass filter section**: A switchable HPF that mirrors the filtering behavior found on the hardware unit.
- **Transformer saturation modeling**: Replicates the harmonic coloration introduced by the transformer-coupled signal path — the defining element of 1073-style processing.
- **Hardware-accurate gain staging**: Reflects the gain structure of BAE's physical 1073, rather than a generic approximation of the topology.
- **Mono and stereo operation**: Applicable across individual tracks and bus processing workflows.
