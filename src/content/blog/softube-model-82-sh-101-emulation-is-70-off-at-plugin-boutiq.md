## What Is Softube Model 82?

Model 82 is Softube's software recreation of the Roland SH-101, the legendary monophonic synthesizer first released in 1982. The SH-101 became a cornerstone of early electronic music — its warm bass lines and punchy leads can be heard across decades of house, techno, acid, and synth-pop. Softube, a Swedish audio software company widely respected for its analog circuit modeling, built Model 82 to bring that classic hardware character into modern DAWs, including its built-in step sequencer.

---

## Key Features

- **Analog Circuit Modeling** — Softube builds its emulations from the ground up using circuit-level modeling, targeting the harmonic character and response of the original hardware.
- **Built-In Step Sequencer** — True to the original SH-101, Model 82 includes a step sequencer for programming basslines and melodic patterns directly in the plugin.
- **Monophonic Synthesis Engine** — Designed for the single-voice, lead/bass workflow the SH-101 was known for — not a polyphonic pad machine.
- **VCO, VCF, VCA Signal Path** — The classic subtractive synthesis chain that made the original hardware so immediate and musical to use.
- **DAW Integration** — Works as a VST/AU/AAX instrument, fitting into any modern production setup.
- **Softube Console 1 Compatible** — Can be controlled via Softube's hardware controller for hands-on playability (check the product page for compatibility details).
