## UAD Moog Minimoog
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Moog Minimoog -->

The UAD Moog Minimoog plugin suits keyboard players and producers who want the classic Minimoog Model D sound inside a DAW without hardware, since it reproduces the original's module interactions, ladder-filter growl, and pitch-tracking quirks with near-zero latency. They can load it as an instrument track in supported hosts such as Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, or Studio One, and use features like switchable note priority, legato, sample and hold, and the extra LFO to go beyond the original hardware's capabilities.

## Virtual Guitarist Bundle
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Virtual Guitarist Bundle -->

Virtual Guitarist Bundle suits guitarists and producers who want realistic, great-sounding guitar accompaniment without hiring a session player, offering organic, distorted, and processed rhythm guitar tones across more than 30 styles and 350 phrases. Users can play picking and strumming parts (including the new Instrument mode and Open Chords feature), layer up to four guitarists for a fuller sound, and shape the tone further with stomp box emulations and the Finisher multi-effect rack.

## Heavyocity Ascend
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Heavyocity Ascend -->

Heavyocity Ascend suits cinematic composers who want a deeply detailed, warm-sounding grand piano for scoring work, offering 110 snapshot presets across categories like Core Concert D, Ascended, Rhythmic, and Rhythmic Melodic to cover a range of moods. They can browse and layer these presets using the intuitive sample browser, build evolving rhythmic parts with the built-in ARP, and blend the instrument's 16 sound sources in real time with the three-point mixer, running the plugin as a standalone app or VST/AU/AAX instrument within Kontakt Player.

## Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ -->

Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ suits organists and keyboardists working across blues, gospel, rock, and jazz who want authentic tonewheel tones with full polyphony across upper, lower, and pedal manuals. Players can shape their sound using the drawbar utilities, scanner vibrato/chorus, adjustable rotary speaker and microphone settings, and the built-in tape echo, reverb, and seven-band EQ, or start from one of the over 200 included presets.

## Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B

They can shape the sound using the Vibrato, Tune, Hum, and Velocity Curve controls, run it through the built-in stompbox effects like overdrive, chorus, and delay, or start from one of the 50 included presets, all while working in AU, VST, VST3, AAX, or standalone format.

<!-- pick:Native Instruments Kontakt 8 -->

## Native Instruments Scene Saffron
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments Scene Saffron -->
