## UAD Moog Minimoog
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:UAD Moog Minimoog -->

The UAD Moog Minimoog plugin suits keyboard players and producers who want the classic Minimoog Model D sound inside a DAW without hardware, since it reproduces the original's module interactions, ladder-filter growl, and pitch-tracking quirks with near-zero latency. They can load it as an instrument track in supported hosts such as Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Ableton Live, Cubase, or Studio One, and use features like switchable note priority, legato, sample and hold, and the extra LFO to go beyond the original hardware's capabilities.

## Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ -->

Cherry Audio Blue3 Tonewheel Organ suits organists and keyboardists working across blues, gospel, rock, and jazz who want authentic tonewheel tones with full polyphony across upper, lower, and pedal manuals. Players can shape their sound using the drawbar utilities, scanner vibrato/chorus, adjustable rotary speaker and microphone settings, and the built-in tape echo, reverb, and seven-band EQ, or start from one of the over 200 included presets.

## Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Cherry Audio Wurlybird 140B -->

Wurlybird 140B suits pianists and producers chasing the classic 60s Wurlitzer 140B electric piano tone for rock and pop tracks, especially those who value authentic key and pedal response over a more durable, stage-ready alternative. They can shape the sound using the Vibrato, Tune, Hum, and Velocity Curve controls, run it through the built-in stompbox effects like overdrive, chorus, and delay, or start from one of the 50 included presets, all while working in AU, VST, VST3, AAX, or standalone format.

## Native Instruments Action Strings 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments Action Strings 2 -->

Native Instruments Action Strings 2 suits composers and producers working on cinematic soundtracks who need a full orchestral string ensemble sound with dramatic tension. They can compose string parts using a range of articulations captured from multiple mic positions, drawing on over 1,370 dynamic phrases that can be tweaked and blended in limitless combinations, via the free Kontakt Player (version 7.10 or higher).

## XLN Audio Addictive Keys
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:XLN Audio Addictive Keys -->

XLN Audio Addictive Keys (Studio Grand) suits pianists and producers who want the character of a classic Steinway Model D concert grand across styles ranging from open and natural to processed and strange. Users can select from six mic perspectives and dial in ExploreMaps presets to shape the tone for either studio production or stage performance.

## XLN Audio XO
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:XLN Audio XO -->

XLN Audio XO suits producers and beatmakers who want to quickly browse, sort, and dig through large or disorganized one-shot drum sample collections. They can load their own samples (including duplicates, vague filenames, or files scattered across folders and drives) into XO's similarity-based sorting space, then sweep through sounds, build beats with the built-in sequencer, shape groove using the Accentuator and Groove Templates, and export the results as audio or MIDI, or as WAV stems, directly into their DAW or OS.

## Native Instruments 24K Drums
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments 24K Drums -->

Native Instruments 24K Drums suits hip hop and trap producers who want golden-era 2000s drum sounds without programming a kit from scratch, since it delivers hard-hitting kicks, razor-sharp snares, and polished percussion alongside bold, rich melodics. Producers can load it in the free Kontakt Player or Kontakt 8.1+ and immediately customize tracks using the included kits and MIDI patterns, or tap the Play Series interface for real-time control over the raw-to-crisp tonal range.

## Native Instruments Glaze 2
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments Glaze 2 -->

Native Instruments Glaze 2 suits pop and hip hop producers and vocalists who want ready-made vocal textures without building chords from scratch, especially those already working inside Kontakt or the free Kontakt Player. They can load any of its 150 presets, including the special chord sets for stacked chords, pads, leads, and talkbox sounds, then use the over 30 custom chord sets and background vocals mapped across the keyboard to trigger complex harmonies instantly, shaping the sound further with the included Kontakt effects macros and the Play Series interface's real-time controls.

## Native Instruments Halo Vox
<!-- role:pick -->
<!-- pick:Native Instruments Halo Vox -->

Native Instruments Halo Vox is suited to producers and vocal-focused music makers who want ready-made choral and ethereal vocal textures without building vocal sounds from scratch. They can load it in Kontakt Player (or Kontakt 8.1+) and explore its 150 editable presets, shaping pure vowels, layered textures, and processed vocal tones directly through the Play Series' real-time controls.
