## Step 1: Use the free 30-day trial first

FabFilter offers a 30-day evaluation of its plug-ins. The trial is the cheapest way to avoid paying for the wrong compressor. Use it in a structured way:

- **Load it on real projects.** Put it on the vocal, drum bus, and mix bus of tracks you are actually finishing, not on test loops.
- **Match your current workflow.** If you already own a compressor you rely on, A/B the two at matched output levels so loudness doesn't bias the comparison.
- **Note the specific gap.** Write down what the plugin does that your current tools don't. If you can't name a gap by day 30, that is useful information.
- **Check DAW compatibility and CPU load** on your own machine, with your typical track count.

## Step 3: Decide whether you need it

### Reasons to buy now

- You have no dependable compressor, and your mixes would benefit from one that suits many source types.
- Your trial confirmed a concrete improvement in your workflow.
- You earn from your mixes, so the time saved outweighs the cost.

### Reasons to wait

- You are still learning compression basics. Stock DAW compressors cover the same fundamentals.
- Your trial surfaced no clear gap.
- Your budget is tight and other gear, such as monitoring or acoustic treatment, would improve your mixes more.

## Practical buying scenarios

**You own an older FabFilter compressor.** Log in first. If a discount appears, compare it against the cost of staying on your current version. If your current version already does the job, you may not need to upgrade.

**You mix for clients.** A tool you know well and trust can justify its price. The trial lets you test it on client-style material before committing.
