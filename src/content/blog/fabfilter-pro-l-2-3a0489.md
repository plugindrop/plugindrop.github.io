## What you actually get

FabFilter lists eight different limiting algorithms in Pro L 2, each with its own character and intended use case. That gives you room to pick a gentler algorithm for transparent mastering or a more aggressive one for loudness-focused work.

- True peak limiting, so inter-sample peaks get caught before export or streaming encode.
- Up to 32x linear-phase oversampling, according to the product listing, aimed at cleaner results at higher limiting ratios.
- Loudness metering covering the EBU R128, ITU-R BS.1770-4, and ATSC A/85 standards, which matters for broadcast or streaming loudness targets.
- Surround support including Dolby Atmos 7.0.2 and 7.1.2 formats with flexible channel linking, per the listing.
- External side chain triggering, listed as built for stem mastering workflows.

## Where it fits in a session

Pro L 2 sits at the end of a mastering chain, right before dither and export, where its true peak limiting and oversampling matter most. The surround and Dolby Atmos support points to final mix and mastering stages for immersive audio deliverables rather than tracking or mixing.

The external side chain option is relevant specifically for stem mastering, where separate stems need linked limiting decisions. The loudness metering suite is useful at the delivery stage, when you're checking a master against a streaming platform's target.
