## What Is the SSL 4000 G Bus Compressor?

The SSL 4000 G Bus Compressor is the official plugin recreation of the hardware bus compressor built into Solid State Logic's legendary 4000 G Series mixing console. This isn't a third-party emulation — it's built by SSL themselves. The original hardware unit has appeared on an enormous number of commercially released records, prized for its ability to "glue" a mix together and add cohesion across the stereo bus. The plugin aims to translate that character directly into a DAW environment.

---

## Key Features

- **VCA-style compression engine** — replicates the circuit behavior of the original hardware, including its characteristic soft-knee response.
- **Attack and Release controls** — multiple selectable values for both parameters, mirroring the discrete options available on the original hardware unit.
- **Fixed ratio options** — switchable ratio settings (2:1, 4:1, and 10:1) matching the original console's compressor functionality.
- **Threshold and Make-Up Gain** — standard controls for dialing in the compression depth and compensating for gain reduction.
- **Auto Fade** — a feature carried over from the hardware, allowing for automated fade-out behavior directly within the plugin.
- **Parallel/wet-dry mix** — check the product page for details on the exact wet/dry implementation in the current version.
