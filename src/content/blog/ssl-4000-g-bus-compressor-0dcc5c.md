## What you actually get

The SSL 4000 G Bus Compressor is a plugin recreation of the bus compressor section from the SSL 4000 G console, the circuit widely credited with the "glue" sound heard on countless mixed records. It applies gentle, program-dependent compression across a stereo bus rather than aggressive single-track squashing. Expect the standard SSL-style control set: threshold, ratio, attack, release, and makeup gain, along with the compressor's characteristic auto-fade release behavior.

Check the product page for exact specs on supported ratios, sidechain options, and plugin formats. License and activation details, including any iLok requirement, are also listed there.

## Where it fits in a session

This kind of compressor is built for bus-level duty, not individual track processing. Common placements include:

- The master bus, for overall cohesion before a limiter
- Drum bus, to tighten kick, snare, and overheads into one punchy element
- Submixed vocal stacks or group buses that need to sit together

It is less suited as a primary vocal or instrument compressor where you need heavy, targeted gain reduction on a single source.
