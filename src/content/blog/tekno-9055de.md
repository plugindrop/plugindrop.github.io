## What Is Tekno?

Tekno is a fully synthesized drum machine plugin by Baby Audio, co-developed with analog circuit modeling specialist Jatin Chowdhury. Every sound it produces is generated from scratch in real time — there is no pre-recorded audio of any kind. It packs 18 purpose-built drum engines covering kicks, snares, claps, hats, cymbals, and percussion. This makes it a complete drum production tool built entirely around synthesis rather than sample playback.

## Key Features

- **18 Custom Drum Engines:** Each voice is purpose-built for its specific drum type, giving dedicated synthesis behavior rather than a shared one-size-fits-all architecture.
- **Analog Circuit Modeling:** Co-developed with Jatin Chowdhury, a leading analog DSP engineer, delivering hardware-inspired punch and saturation character throughout.
- **Zero Pre-Recorded Audio:** Every hit is synthesized in real time — no samples, no recordings, no clearance concerns.
- **Per-Voice Effects Chain:** Tape saturation, exciter, ring modulation, reverb, and compressor are available independently on each drum voice.
- **Smart Randomization + Humanize:** Dice icons algorithmically generate entirely new drum sounds on demand; a Humanize function adds subtle timing and velocity variation for organic-sounding grooves.
- **1,314 Voice Presets + 73 Global Presets:** Curated by professionals including Mr. Bill, Virtual Riot, and Yoad Nevo across a wide range of electronic styles.
