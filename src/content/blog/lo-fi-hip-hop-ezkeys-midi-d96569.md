## What Is Lo-Fi Hip Hop EZkeys MIDI?

Lo-Fi Hip Hop EZkeys MIDI is a MIDI content pack from Toontrack, built for EZkeys 2, their piano and keyboard instrument platform. It is not a standalone plugin. It adds material to EZkeys 2: chord progressions, melodic phrases, and accompaniment patterns in the lo-fi hip hop style. That style typically means a relaxed, slightly behind-the-beat rhythmic feel, jazz-influenced voicings, and slow harmonic movement.

Toontrack is a Swedish music software developer, best known for EZdrummer, Superior Drummer, and the EZkeys line. Its MIDI packs are designed around EZkeys 2's chord-following engine and song-building workflow, so the content loads into the host's own browser and timeline.

### How MIDI packs work in EZkeys 2

A MIDI pack contains note data, not audio. The sound comes from whichever piano or keyboard instrument you load in EZkeys 2, and the same patterns can be dragged out to other instruments. This has practical consequences:

- **Sound is swappable.** The same progression can drive a felt piano, an electric piano, or a third-party sampler.
- **Harmony is editable.** You can change voicings, transpose, or simplify chords after you drop the pattern in.
- **Arrangement is structured.** Patterns are organised by song section, which makes it easier to lay out a full track.

## Practical Ways to Use It

**1. Start a beat from the chords.** Drop a progression onto the timeline, pick a drum loop at a lo-fi tempo, and build the bass line from the chord roots. Because the voicings are already written, the first sketch can be done quickly.

**2. Use it as a reference for jazz-influenced voicings.** Open a pattern in the piano roll and look at how the chords are spaced and where the extensions sit. Producers who are still learning extended harmony can study these voicings and reuse the shapes in their own progressions.

**3. Re-voice with other instruments.** Send the MIDI to a Rhodes-style electric piano, a vintage sampler, or a muted guitar patch. A different timbre changes the character without changing the harmony.

**4. Edit for originality.** Pre-written MIDI is used by many producers, so a few changes help the result stand out: drop or move notes, shift the timing, change the inversion, or combine the chords from one pattern with the melody from another.

**5. Build content-ready loops.** Lo-fi hip hop is a common mood category for YouTube, streaming playlists, and background music, so section-based patterns make it easier to produce loops of a consistent length.
