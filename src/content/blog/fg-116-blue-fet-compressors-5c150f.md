## What Is the FG-116 Blue Series FET Compressor?

The FG-116 Blue Series is Slate Digital's software emulation of the iconic FET-based hardware compressors — specifically targeting the "Blue Stripe" revision of the legendary 1176-style unit. It's built for the Virtual Mix Rack (VMR) ecosystem, Slate Digital's modular plugin platform. The "Blue" designation points to a specific hardware revision prized for its fast attack characteristics and aggressive transient control. This is a workhorse compressor modeled for producers and engineers who want that punchy, characterful FET sound in the box.

---

## Key Features

- **FET-Style Compression:** Delivers the fast attack and release behavior that FET compressors are known for — ideal for controlling peaks on dynamic sources.
- **Blue Stripe Emulation:** Modeled after the Blue Stripe hardware revision, capturing a specific tonal character distinct from other 1176 variants.
- **"All Buttons In" Mode:** Supports the classic "British Mode" setting — a technique borrowed directly from hardware use that produces a dense, saturated compression character.
- **VMR Integration:** Operates as a module within Slate Digital's Virtual Mix Rack, allowing it to be chained with other VMR modules in a single plugin instance.
- **Analog-Modeled Circuit:** Designed with Slate Digital's analog modeling methodology, targeting the non-linearities and harmonic texture of the hardware source.
