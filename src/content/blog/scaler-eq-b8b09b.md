## What Is Scaler EQ?

Scaler EQ is a harmonic equalizer developed by ScalerAudio — the same team behind the widely used Scaler 2 chord and harmony plugin. Rather than treating EQ as a purely technical frequency-carving tool, Scaler EQ maps the frequency spectrum to musical notes, allowing producers to make boosts and cuts that correspond to actual pitches in a chosen key or scale. It bridges the gap between technical mixing and musical intuition.

---

## Key Features

- **Scale-aware frequency display** — The EQ overlays note names across the spectrum, so you can see exactly which frequencies map to which pitches in your chosen key.
- **Key and scale selection** — Set a root note and scale type to guide your EQ decisions relative to the musical content of your track.
- **Harmonic series visualization** — Highlights the harmonic relationships between frequencies, making it easier to identify resonances and problem areas in context.
- **Multiple filter shapes** — Includes standard EQ band types (bell, shelf, high-pass, low-pass) integrated within the musical display.
- **Real-time spectrum analysis** — Live frequency analyzer runs alongside the EQ curve for monitoring your signal as it plays.
