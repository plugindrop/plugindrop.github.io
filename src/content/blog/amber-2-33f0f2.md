## What you actually get

AMBER 2 is part of UJAM's Virtual Guitarist series, a phrase- and chord-based guitar instrument rather than a note-by-note sampled guitar library. You play chords or trigger patterns on your MIDI keyboard, and the engine translates that into recorded guitar performances rather than static single-note samples.

That performance-based approach is what separates the Virtual Guitarist line from a standard rompler:

- Chord and pattern-driven playback instead of manual note programming
- Built for fast guitar-part sketching inside a DAW session
- Part of a broader UJAM catalog that includes sibling instruments like Silk 2, Sparkle 2, Iron 2, and Carbon

## Where it fits in a session

AMBER 2 is useful when you need a guitar part quickly but don't have a guitarist on hand or time to record one. Drop it into a session during the arrangement phase to sketch chord progressions before deciding whether to keep the part or replace it with a live take later.

It also works as a scratch-track tool during songwriting, letting you audition chord voicings and rhythmic feel without picking up an instrument.
