## What Is Melodyne 5 Essential?

Melodyne 5 Essential is the entry-level edition of Celemony's industry-standard pitch and time editing software. It analyzes monophonic audio — lead vocals, solo instruments, bass lines — and breaks it into individual editable notes, giving producers precise control over pitch, timing, and dynamics. Celemony's DNA (Direct Note Access) technology underpins the full Melodyne lineup; the Essential tier focuses on single-voice (monophonic) content. It runs as a VST, AU, or AAX plugin inside your DAW, and also operates as a standalone application.

---

## Key Features

- **Note-based pitch editing** — Audio is represented as individual "blobs" that can be moved, tuned, and reshaped with note-level precision.
- **Timing and tempo correction** — Reposition and stretch notes without destructive processing.
- **Pitch modulation and vibrato control** — Shape pitch curves within individual notes to tighten or add expressiveness to a performance.
- **Formant correction** — Shift formants independently of pitch to preserve a natural timbre when transposing vocals.
- **Standalone + plugin operation** — Works as VST3, AU, and AAX, plus standalone for dedicated session editing.
- **Melodyne 5 engine** — Includes Celemony's updated note detection and editing improvements from the version 5 release.
