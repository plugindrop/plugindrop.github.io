## The Recycle Loop Built Into Effect Rack's 14-Plugin Chain
<!-- role:product -->

Soundtoys packages 14 of its own effects—among them EchoBoy, PrimalTap, Decapitator, Radiator, Tremolator, and FilterFreak—into Effect Rack, a single plug-in that runs as one self-contained chain rather than 14 separate inserts. Producers can arrange the included effects in any order inside the Rack and store the resulting chain as a preset, so a layered delay-into-saturation-into-filter combination loads back as one unit on a new track.

A global Mix knob blends the processed chain with the dry signal for parallel processing, and a global Recycle control routes the chain's output back into its input, which the brief describes as a way to construct modulated reverbs and extended delay tails without patching several plug-ins together. Compared with Virtual Guitarist Bundle, which centers on an instrument mode and a guitar model built around picking and strumming phrases, or Excite Audio Bundle, which draws on four sound sources run through sample and synth oscillator engines, Effect Rack is built around processing an existing signal through a configurable chain of 14 effects rather than generating new instrument material.

<!-- role:sound -->
