- **Phrase-based performance engine**: MIDI keys trigger pre-recorded bassist performances across multiple styles and groove variations, not just isolated samples.

- **Adjustable complexity and feel**: Within each plugin, producers can dial in timing tightness, playing complexity, and articulation intensity to match the track.
- **VST / AU / AAX support**: Full compatibility across major DAWs on both Mac and Windows.
- **Standalone mode**: Each instrument runs outside a DAW, making it practical for quick sketching without opening a full project.
