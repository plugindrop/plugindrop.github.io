## What you actually get

This is a software recreation of the Sequential Prophet-5 synthesizer, built with input from Sequential itself. The product listing notes it was officially developed in partnership with Sequential, which is the basis for its authenticity claims.

- The listing covers Rev1 & Rev2 SSM and Rev3 Curtis filter and envelope characteristics, giving you three distinct circuit behaviors from the original hardware's production history.
- Dual Layer architecture with Layer, Split, and Alternate modes supports up to 10 voices per layer, useful for stacking two patches or splitting the keyboard across layers.
- X-Modifiers let you assign dedicated envelopes and LFOs to almost every parameter, which opens up modulation routings beyond what the original hardware allowed.
- Two selectable FX slots plus dedicated Delay, Reverb, and Pan Spread effects mean you can shape the sound without reaching for a separate effects chain.
- The product listing counts over 460 presets, including the original 38 factory patches from 1978, so the historical sound set ships alongside newer programming.

## Where it fits in a session

The dual-layer and split modes make this a candidate for building stacked pad or lead sounds early in a session, before you commit to a final arrangement. The independent arpeggiator and chord modes per layer are suited to sequenced parts in electronic or synth-pop productions. Because it includes built-in Delay, Reverb, and Pan Spread, it can sit further along the chain without an external send setup, and MPE compatibility makes it a fit for expressive performance input from MPE controllers.
