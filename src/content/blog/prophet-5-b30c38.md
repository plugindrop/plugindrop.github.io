## Three Character Models Anchor the Prophet~5 Plugin
<!-- role:product -->

Sequential Circuits released the original Prophet~5 in 1978 as Dave Smith's five-voice analogue synthesizer, the instrument credited with pairing polyphony and patch memory with direct, knob-based control. For its first authorized software version, Sequential partnered with GForce Software to rebuild that circuitry rather than approximate it; GForce frames the result as the closest software has come to replicating the original experience, a claim tied to how precisely the plugin tracks the hardware's selectable circuit paths.

The plugin organizes those paths into three character models: Rev1 and Rev2 run through SSM-style circuitry, while Rev3 switches to the Curtis filter and envelope design. Every original control carries over, and GForce built a dedicated X-Modifiers section that gives nearly every parameter its own envelope and LFO, so patches drawn from the vintage circuitry can still move and evolve. Two independent layers run in Layer, Split or Alternate modes with up to 10 voices each, and a patch browser with tagging and search sits alongside more than 460 presets, including 38 factory patches written for the 1978 original.

## The Tone Built From SSM Warmth and Curtis Punch
<!-- role:sound -->

Rev1 and Rev2 draw their character from SSM circuit designs, which the brief ties to warmth, while Rev3's Curtis filter and envelope stage is described as adding punch and presence. Sequential's own account of the hardware's reach — cited from Genesis to Radiohead, Madonna to Aphex Twin — frames the Prophet~5 sound as versatile and powerful enough to have carried across genres and decades, a lineage the plugin's three character models are built to preserve rather than replace.

## Who Gets the Most From the Dual-Layer Workflow
<!-- role:audience -->

- **Sound designers** — The X-Modifiers section's per-parameter envelopes and LFOs give evolving, modulated patches room to move beyond static vintage tones.
- **Live performers** — Full MPE compatibility and comprehensive MIDI CC mapping support expressive, controller-driven playing across both layers.
- **Layer-based producers** — Layer, Split and Alternate modes with independent arpeggiators and chord modes per layer make stacked textures and keyboard splits straightforward to build.
