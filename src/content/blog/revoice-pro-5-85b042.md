## What Is Revoice Pro 5?

Revoice Pro 5 is a professional vocal processing plugin developed by Synchro Arts. Its core function is automatic audio alignment — syncing doubles, harmonies, and backing vocals to a lead track — alongside its flagship APT (Audio Performance Transfer) technology, which copies the timing, pitch, and level contour of one vocal performance onto another. It runs as both a DAW plugin and a standalone application, targeting music production and post-production workflows alike.

---

## Key Features

- **APT (Audio Performance Transfer):** Transfers timing, pitch, and level nuances from a guide track to a dub track automatically — no manual comping required.
- **Automatic Vocal Alignment:** Syncs doubles and backing vocals to a lead in seconds, preserving the natural feel of each performance.
- **Warp Audio:** Time-stretching and pitch-shifting with artifact control for transparent results.
- **Pitch and Timing Editing:** Manual editing tools for fine-tuning beyond what the automatic engine handles.
- **DAW Integration via Link Plugin:** Connects Revoice Pro to major DAWs without bouncing files out of the session.
- **Standalone Operation:** Runs independently for batch processing or offline workflows.
