## What you actually get

ValhallaSupermassive is a KVR Readers' Choice Hall of Fame free effect, and version 6.0 adds Helios, a mode Valhalla DSP describes as the most reverb-y of the plugin's modes. Helios aims for a warm, open sound with realistic damping of the tail, a high initial echo density, and a natural exponential decay. That puts it closer to a traditional reverb character than the delay-and-modulation textures the plugin is best known for.

## Where it fits in a session

Helios mode is aimed at spots where you want a believable room or hall tail instead of an obviously synthetic wash. That makes it a candidate for vocal sends, acoustic instruments, and mix bus glue where a natural decay matters more than texture. The plugin's other modes remain available for pads, ambient beds, and sound design work where a more processed, shimmering tail is the goal.

## Skip it if

- You already run an earlier ValhallaSupermassive version and only need the existing modes; the update is about adding Helios, not replacing core functionality.
- You need a format not listed among VST, AU, and AAX for your host.
