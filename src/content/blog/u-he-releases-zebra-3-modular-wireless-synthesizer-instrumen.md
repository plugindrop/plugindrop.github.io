## What Is u-he Zebra 3?

Zebra 3 is the next-generation wireless modular synthesizer from German plugin developer u-he. The "wireless" concept means deep patchability without literal patch cables — modules connect through a drag-and-drop grid interface that keeps the workflow visual and fast. Zebra 2 became legendary in film scoring and electronic music production circles, famously used in blockbuster soundtracks and revered on synthesis forums worldwide. Zebra 3 is a full successor built on that legacy with a modernized engine.

## Key Features

- **Wireless Modular Architecture** — Drag-and-drop signal routing replaces cables, keeping complex patches readable and editable at a glance.
- **Expanded Oscillator Options** — Multiple oscillator types (including spectral and wavetable modules) widen the sonic palette beyond what Zebra 2 offered.
- **Redesigned Modulation System** — A more flexible modulation matrix allows deeper, more expressive connections between modules.
- **Updated Filter Modules** — New and revised filter designs cover everything from clean surgical cuts to characterful analog-flavored saturation.
- **Enhanced Effects Chain** — Onboard effects modules are rebuilt for higher fidelity and greater routing flexibility.
- **Improved GUI Scaling** — High-DPI display support brings the interface in line with modern screen resolutions.
