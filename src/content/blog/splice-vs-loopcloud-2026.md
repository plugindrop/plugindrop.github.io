## Splice Overview

Splice is one of the most widely used sample subscription platforms. Its library covers hip-hop, electronic, pop, R&B, and beyond — with sounds from established producers and labels.

**Pricing (2026):**
- **Starter** — $7.99/mo — 100 credits/mo
- **Creator** — $13.99/mo — 300 credits/mo
- **Producer** — $39.99/mo — 2,000 credits/mo

Each credit corresponds to one sample download. Credits roll over for up to 3 months. Samples are royalty-free once downloaded.

The feature that makes Splice unique is **rent-to-own plugins**. Subscribers can pay monthly installments toward purchasing full plugins from developers like Output, iZotope, and Arturia. This is a distinct value-add that Loopcloud does not replicate.

The Splice desktop app handles downloading and organizing samples, but previewing is done through the browser or app — not directly inside a DAW session.

## Loopcloud Overview

Loopcloud launched with a focus on workflow speed and has built a DAW-first experience around its desktop plugin.

**Pricing (2026):**
- **Free** — 10 downloads/mo, limited browsing
- **Basic** — $8.99/mo — 30 downloads/mo
- **Pro** — £13.99/mo — 60 downloads/mo (additional packs purchasable)

The standout feature is the **Loopcloud plugin**, which runs inside your DAW. You can preview samples in sync with your project's tempo and key without leaving your session. This is a significant workflow advantage for producers who work primarily inside a DAW.

Loopcloud also offers **AI-powered search**, which can match sounds by mood, genre, or reference audio. The free tier gives producers a genuine way to evaluate the platform before committing to a paid plan.

## Key Differences

**Free tier:** Loopcloud offers one; Splice does not. If budget is a constraint, Loopcloud lets you start without paying.

**DAW integration:** This is Loopcloud's clearest advantage. The desktop plugin lets you drop samples directly into your session at the correct BPM and key. Splice requires browsing outside the DAW, then importing files manually.

**Rent-to-own plugins:** Splice's plugin rental model is unique in the market. If you want to spread the cost of industry-standard plugins over several months, Splice is the only major platform offering this.

**AI search:** Loopcloud's AI-powered search can reduce time spent browsing large libraries. Splice relies on tag-based filtering.

**Pricing parity:** Both platforms sit in the $8–14/mo range for their core tiers, so cost is not a decisive factor at the entry level.

## Which Is Better For...

**Hip-hop producers:** Either works well — both libraries have strong hip-hop coverage. Loopcloud's in-DAW preview is practical for chopping and layering. Splice's rent-to-own can offset the cost of beat-making tools.

**Electronic music producers:** Loopcloud's tempo-synced in-DAW previewing is particularly useful when building arrangements. The AI search is helpful for finding specific textures quickly.

**Film scoring / sound design:** Both platforms lean toward music production samples rather than sound design assets. For sound design work, dedicated libraries (Boom, SoundSnap, Sonniss GDC packs) are more appropriate.

**Beginners:** Loopcloud's free tier removes the barrier to entry. Starting without a subscription commitment makes it the more accessible choice for producers still building their workflow.

## FAQ

**Can I keep samples after canceling my subscription?**
Yes — on both Splice and Loopcloud, samples you have downloaded remain yours after canceling. The royalty-free license stays valid.

**Does Loopcloud work on Mac and Windows?**
Yes. The Loopcloud plugin supports VST3, AU, and AAX formats across Mac and Windows. It is compatible with all major DAWs.

**Is Splice's rent-to-own model actually worth it?**
It depends on which plugins you want. If a plugin retails for $299 and Splice charges $9.99/mo toward it, you own it after ~30 months of payments. For producers already paying for a Splice plan, this can be a practical way to build a plugin collection incrementally.
