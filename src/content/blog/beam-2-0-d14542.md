## What Is BEAM 2.0?

BEAM 2.0 is an audio-cleanup plugin from Accusonus, designed to process recordings captured on multiple microphones at once. Instead of manually muting, gating, and mixing each mic channel, BEAM uses beamforming-style processing to combine the inputs into a single, cleaner signal. It's aimed primarily at video producers, podcasters, and anyone recording interviews or multi-camera setups where mic bleed and inconsistent levels are a problem. It runs as a plugin, so it slots into a DAW or video editor's effects chain rather than requiring a standalone app.

## Key Features

- **Multi-mic combination** — merges audio from several microphones into one processed output, reducing the need for manual channel mixing.
- **Noise and bleed reduction** — targets background noise and cross-talk between mics, a common issue in interview and panel recordings.
- **Plugin format** — installs as an effect you can insert directly on a track, rather than a separate processing app.
- **Designed for video and podcast workflows** — built around the kind of multi-source recording setups common in talking-head video, interviews, and panel discussions.
