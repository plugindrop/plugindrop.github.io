## Inside BEAM 2.0's Modular Effects Playground

Three effect categories — convolution reverb, granular processing, and chorus/phase modulation — share one modular engine in BEAM 2.0, which the feature list calls a modular effects playground. Any Lunacy effect loads directly inside that engine, letting producers sparkle keys, spice up vocals, reshape drums, or launch synths into outer-space textures without leaving the plugin.
