## What Is BEAM 2.0?

According to [Lunacy](https://lunacy.audio/products/beam/), BEAM 2.0 is "a modular effects playground filled with a growing collection of cutting-edge effects." Any Lunacy effect can be loaded into the BEAM engine, and the company describes uses such as adding sparkle to keys, spicing up vocals, reshaping drums and sending synths into outer space. It runs natively on macOS and Windows.

## What Lunacy Says Is Included

- **Standalone effects (new in 2.0)** - every BEAM effect module can be used as a standalone plugin, or combined in patches inside BEAM.
- **Four effect plugins with the core product** - Grains, Space, Haze and Filter.
- **Presets** - 300+ global presets from sound designers (Lunacy names Venus Theory, J.Views, Dash Glitch, Databroth and Alckemy Neuro among them), plus 150+ node presets with sub-preset browsers for effect nodes and modulators.
- **Impulse responses** - 200+ IRs, from halls to synthetic textures.
- **Preset generator** - creates new presets from scratch using what Lunacy calls a proprietary probability cluster algorithm.
- **Expansion** - additional Lunacy effects and preset packs can be added; Lunacy sells a separate BEAM Bundle with all current effect nodes and 450+ presets.

## Technical Requirements (from Lunacy)

- macOS 10.15 or later (AU / VST3 / AAX; Lunacy recommends M1 / M2)
- Windows 10 or later (VST3 / AAX; Lunacy strongly recommends an i7 or better)
- 8 GB RAM required (16 GB recommended); at least 250 MB free disk space
- A stable internet connection is required to download and activate the product
- Lunacy lists a 14-day return policy for software purchases (expansion packs excluded). This applies to Lunacy's own store; check Plugin Boutique's terms if you buy there.

## Who Might Look at It

BEAM is aimed at music production and sound design: people who want to build their own effect chains and modulated patches from one engine rather than buy single-purpose effects. If you only need a single reverb or filter, Lunacy's four standalone effects (Grains, Space, Haze, Filter) are the pieces to look at; Haze and Filter are listed on Lunacy's page as free.
