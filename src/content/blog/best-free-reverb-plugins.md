Plate reverb with a warm, slightly diffused character. Simple interface — size, pre-delay, low/high cut. Sits well in busy mixes without dominating. Low CPU usage makes it practical on every track.

## Which One Should You Grab?

**For ambient and experimental work** — Start with Valhalla Supermassive. The Warp and Hydra modes create textures no other free reverb offers.

**For mixing vocals and instruments** — OrilRiver or TAL-Reverb-4. OrilRiver gives more control; TAL-Reverb-4 gives faster results.

**For realistic room and hall sounds** — Dragonfly Reverb. The separate Room and Hall plugins each focus on what they do best.

**For creative sound design** — CloudSeed and NI RAUM both offer unique textures. RAUM's freeze function is particularly useful for pad creation.
