## What Is Decimort 2?

Decimort 2 is a bit and sample rate reduction plugin developed by D16 Group Audio Software, a Polish developer known for emulating vintage hardware in software form. The plugin is designed to recreate the character of early digital samplers, applying bit depth and sample rate reduction along with filtering to shape a degraded, lo-fi digital tone. It ships as a studio effect for shaping drums, synths, vocals, and full mixes that need grit rather than clean digital precision.

## Key Features

- **Bit depth reduction** — lowers resolution to introduce quantization artifacts associated with early digital hardware.
- **Sample rate reduction** — reduces the sampling frequency to create aliasing and a coarser digital texture.
- **Filtering stage** — shapes the crushed signal before and after the reduction process to control brightness and harshness.
- **Multiple emulation modes** — models different vintage sampler behaviors rather than a single generic bitcrusher curve.
- **Preset library** — includes factory presets aimed at drums, bass, and full-mix processing for faster workflow.
