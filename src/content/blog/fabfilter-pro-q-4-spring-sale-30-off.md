## What Is Pro-Q 4?

FabFilter Pro-Q is a parametric EQ plugin used widely in professional mixing and mastering workflows. It combines a visual spectrum analyzer with up to 24 dynamic EQ bands, mid/side processing, and surround support including Dolby Atmos. Regular price: $189.

## Key Features

- **Up to 24 EQ bands** — Each can switch to dynamic mode with threshold and range controls
- **Spectrum grab** — Click directly on the frequency spectrum to create and adjust bands
- **Linear phase mode** — For mastering applications where phase accuracy matters
- **Mid/Side processing** — Per-band M/S control for stereo work
- **Surround support** — Dolby Atmos and other immersive formats
- **Smart solo** — Audition what each band is doing in isolation
