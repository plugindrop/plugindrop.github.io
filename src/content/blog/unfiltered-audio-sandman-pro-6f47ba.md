## What Is Unfiltered Audio Sandman Pro?

Sandman Pro is a delay and looping plugin from Unfiltered Audio, a developer known for building tools that push sound design well beyond conventional territory. At its core, it pairs tape-style delay with a freeze and granular looping engine, enabling everything from standard echo effects to evolving textural soundscapes. Unfiltered Audio operates under the iZotope/Native Instruments umbrella, which speaks to the development resources behind it. It's built for producers who want a delay unit that doubles as a creative instrument.

## Key Features

- **Freeze/Loop Engine:** Captures incoming audio and holds it in a loop buffer in real time, ideal for building ambient pads and drones from any source material.
- **Granular Playback Modes:** Multiple buffer playback options let you scatter, stutter, and manipulate frozen audio for glitch-style effects.
- **Modulation Matrix:** Assign LFOs and envelopes to key parameters for movement and animation throughout a sound.
- **Tape-Style Delay Core:** Classic delay behavior with feedback and tone controls forms the foundation before the more experimental features kick in.
- **MIDI Triggering:** Freeze and loop functions can be triggered via MIDI, making live performance use a realistic option — check the product page for full MIDI implementation details.
