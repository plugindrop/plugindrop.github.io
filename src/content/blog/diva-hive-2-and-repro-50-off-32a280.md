## What Are Diva, Hive 2, and Repro?

u-he is a German audio software developer known for precision analog modeling and exacting sound quality. This promotion covers three distinct synthesizers, each targeting a different workflow. Together they cover a wide range of synthesis approaches — from CPU-intensive analog emulation to lightweight hybrid design.

- **Diva** (Dinosaur Impersonating Virtual Analogue) is u-he's flagship analog synthesizer emulation, modeling oscillator and filter circuits from several classic hardware synths.
- **Hive 2** is a hybrid synthesizer built for efficiency and speed — lower CPU overhead, fast patching, and a broad factory preset library.
- **Repro** is u-he's vintage circuit-modeling series, focused on component-level emulation of classic analog hardware.

## Key Features

### Diva
- **Multiple oscillator topologies** — selectable circuit models drawn from different vintage hardware, each with distinct tonal character
- **Classic filter models** — several filter architectures available, from ladder to Sallen-Key designs
- **Zero-delay feedback filters** — contributes to the analog-accurate response the plugin is known for
- **Adjustable quality modes** — scale CPU usage against sound quality to suit your machine and workflow

### Hive 2
- **Hybrid synthesis engine** — combines virtual analog oscillators with additional synthesis sources
- **Low CPU footprint** — designed to run multiple instances without overloading modern systems
- **Deep modulation matrix** — flexible routing between sources and destinations for complex motion
- **Large factory preset library** — ships with broad factory content across genres and styles

### Repro
- **Circuit-level signal path modeling** — emulates analog hardware at the component level rather than just behavioral approximation
- **Classic arpeggiator and sequencer** — period-accurate performance tools matching the original hardware
- **Analog drift and instability** — tuned imperfections that replicate the behavior of aging vintage units
