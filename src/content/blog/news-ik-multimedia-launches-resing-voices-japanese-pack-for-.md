## IK Multimedia Launches ReSing Voices Japanese Pack for Vocal Modeling

## What Is the ReSing Voices Japanese Pack?

ReSing is IK Multimedia's vocal modeling software, designed to let producers create and shape synthesized vocal performances without recording a live singer. The newly launched **ReSing Voices Japanese Pack** is an expansion that adds 10 vocal models built specifically for Japanese-language music production and voice creation. IK Multimedia developed these models to cover a broad range of tonal characteristics, making the expansion relevant across different Japanese music styles and production contexts.

## Key Features

- **10 Japanese vocal models** — A dedicated set of voices designed from the ground up for Japanese-language phonetics and expression, rather than retrofitted from English-language sources.
- **Genre-spanning tonal range** — The pack reportedly covers a wide variety of voice characters, offering producers flexibility across J-pop, anime, city pop, and other Japanese-language genres.
- **Native ReSing integration** — Models slot directly into the existing ReSing workflow, meaning no additional routing or third-party tools are required to get started.
- **Voice creation focus** — Beyond simple playback, ReSing's underlying architecture is built for shaping and modeling vocal performance, which the Japanese Pack extends into a new language territory.
