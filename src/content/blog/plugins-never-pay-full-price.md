## Why these prices are effectively fictional

A list price only matters if people actually pay it. For the plugins below, our price-check history shows the "regular" price appearing rarely, if at all, while a deep discount shows up on nearly every check. When a plugin sells at 80–90% off often enough that the discount looks like the baseline, wishlisting it and waiting is the correct move — not a compromise.

### Waves Platinum and Gold Bundles — the $1999 number nobody pays

Waves lists Platinum at $1999, but our tracked checks recorded actual prices of $131/$77, $119/$60, $119/$70, $119/$60, $119/$70, and $1999/$199 across six separate dates from May through July 2026 — automated checks dipped as low as $60, though our confirmed, vetted all-time low sits at $77. Only one of those six checks showed anything close to the nominal $1999 figure, and even then the actual charge was $199. [Check the current live price](https://www.pluginboutique.com/product/81-Bundles/84-Mix-Master/12542-Platinum-Bundle?a_aid=69cb95abe1763) before assuming $1999 is real. Gold Bundle shows the same behavior at a smaller scale — regular $879, but flash sales bring it to $99–$175, with a documented floor of $79.99. Waves runs sales often enough that "waiting for a sale" barely describes the behavior — it's closer to "waiting a few days."

### Plugin Alliance — the "Any Plugin $29.99" club

Six separate Plugin Alliance products in our dataset — bx_console SSL 4000E, bx_console Neve 88RS, Maag EQ4, SPL Vitalizer, Lindell 80 Series, Shadow Hills Mastering Compressor, and Elysia niveau filter — list between $179 and $299 individually, and every one of them shows a recurring $29.99 sale price in our history. That's not a one-off: it's Plugin Alliance's standing "Any Plugin $29.99" promotion, and it recurs often enough that we've recorded it as both the typical sale price and the all-time low for each of these plugins. [See the bx_console SSL 4000E price history](/plugin-prices/plugin-alliance-bx-console-ssl-4000e/) if you want to check whether the promo is live right now.

### iZotope — 50%+ off is the norm, not the exception

iZotope Music Production Suite lists at $905–$999, but our tracked sale price sits at $499 (roughly 45–50% off), with an all-time low of $362 recorded at Best Service in August 2023. Ozone 11 Standard shows the same shape at smaller scale: $199 regular, $99 typical sale, and a documented $70 floor from Black Friday 2024. iZotope runs these discounts often enough — summer, fall, and Black Friday — that a full-price purchase usually means bad timing rather than no alternative.

### UAD 1176 Classic Limiter Collection — $149 down to $29

Universal Audio's storewide "up to 80% off" events bring the [1176 Classic Limiter Collection](https://www.pluginboutique.com/search?search_query=UAD%201176%20Classic%20Limiter%20Collection&a_aid=69cb95abe1763) from $149 down to a common $49, with a documented low of $29 recorded at Plugin Boutique in May 2025. These UA sales run multiple times a year rather than only at Black Friday, which is worth knowing if you assumed BF was your only shot — see the [Black Friday brand-by-brand breakdown](/posts/is-it-worth-waiting-for-black-friday-plugins/) for the full picture.

### Soundtoys — the flagship bundle and the singles both drop hard

The [Soundtoys 5.5 bundle](https://www.pluginboutique.com/product/81-Bundles/89-Complete-Collection/15254-Soundtoys-5-5?a_aid=69cb95abe1763) lists at $659, with a tracked $299 sale price (50% off) confirmed in an April 2026 promotion, and an all-time low around $239. Individual Soundtoys plugins follow the same pattern at smaller scale — Decapitator and EchoBoy both list at $199 and drop to roughly $69 (about 65% off) during Soundtoys' Summer and Black Friday sales. If you only need one or two Soundtoys plugins rather than the whole bundle, the singles discount just as aggressively.

### NI Komplete 15 Ultimate — 50% off, recorded at both Black Friday and PB's own sale events

Komplete 15 Ultimate lists at $1319, and Plugin Boutique's own history shows it running at roughly 50% off ($659) during both Black Friday and other major PB promotions — this isn't a once-a-year event exclusive to any single date.

### Softube — up to 75% off the premium single plugins

Tube-Tech CL 1B, one of Softube's priciest standalone plugins at $399, is documented dropping to $99 (75% off) in Softube's seasonal sales. Trident A-Range ($299) and Weiss DS1-MK3 ($349) show the same discount depth. These aren't isolated incidents — Softube runs several named sale events a year ("Releases Rewind," Summer Sale, Black Friday), and the discount depth is consistent across all of them.

### Sonnox Oxford Inflator — the deepest discount in our entire dataset

Inflator lists at $185, with a typical Summer Sale price of $29 (about 85% off). One documented deal-roundup event pushed it to $16 — over 90% off — during a limited "every Sonnox plugin on sale" promotion through July 2025. Community reporting describes Sonnox as normally staying out of major sales, which makes this pattern more notable, not less: when Sonnox does discount, it discounts harder than almost anything else we track.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Waves Platinum Bundle actually worth $1999?**
A: No one in our tracked history paid anything close to that. Across six separate price checks from May–July 2026, the recorded price ranged from $60 to $199 — never above $199. Treat $1999 as a reference number, not a real price.

**Q: How often does Plugin Alliance run the "Any Plugin $29.99" promo?**
A: It recurs multiple times a year rather than being a single annual event, and our data shows it as both the typical sale price and the all-time low across seven different PA plugins we track.

**Q: What's the single deepest discount in this dataset?**
A: Sonnox Oxford Inflator, documented at $16 — over 90% off its $185 list price — during a limited "every Sonnox plugin on sale" event. Its regular Summer Sale price of $29 (85% off) is already the deepest recurring discount we track.
