TDR Nova is a parallel dynamic equalizer with four fully parametric bands, switchable high-pass and low-pass filters, and a real-time spectrum analyzer built in. What separates it from every other free EQ is the dynamic section — each band can behave as a standard static filter or react to the incoming signal level, giving you de-essing, resonance control, and transparent dynamic correction without reaching for a separate compressor. Voxengo Marvel GEQ is a 16-band linear phase graphic equalizer with the kind of processing quality that explains why Voxengo's free plugins are taken seriously in professional studios. Linear phase filtering means no phase distortion is introduced at any frequency — a critical property for mastering and any situation where you need tonal adjustments that are completely transparent to the stereo image. **Honest limitation:** Linear phase filtering introduces latency and can cause pre-ringing on sharp transients, so it is the wrong choice on individual percussive tracks; keep it on the bus and master.

**Best for:** Mastering engineers and mix bus processing where phase coherence matters as much as the tonal correction itself.

## How to Choose

- **If you want one EQ that handles everything**, install **TDR Nova** first — the dynamic capability makes it useful on every track type, and the spectrum analyzer accelerates your learning curve.
- **If you are mastering or processing the mix bus** and need zero phase distortion, **Voxengo Marvel GEQ** or **LP10** are the right tools; minimum-phase options introduce phase shift that can subtly affect stereo coherence.
- **If your tracks need character rather than just correction** — drums, guitars, mix bus coloring — reach for **Baxter EQ** or **TDR SlickEQ**. These reward a "set it and let it breathe" approach.
- **If you are building a large session** with many EQ instances and need maximum flexibility, **ReaEQ** handles unlimited bands at minimal CPU cost without asking you to compromise.
- **If vintage tone is central to your genre** (lo-fi, soul, classic rock, jazz), **Ignite Amps PTEq-X** gives you the Pultec interaction that producers have relied on for sixty years — for free.
- **When you are ready to invest**, **FabFilter Pro-Q 4** is the upgrade that makes every workflow on this list feel like a warm-up.

**Q: What is linear phase EQ and when should I use it?**
A: Linear phase EQ processes audio without introducing phase shift at any frequency — unlike standard minimum-phase designs. For general mixing, minimum-phase EQ is typically preferred because it responds more naturally to the music and adds no latency.
