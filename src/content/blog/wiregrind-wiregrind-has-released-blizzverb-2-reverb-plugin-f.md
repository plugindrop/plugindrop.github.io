BlizzVerb 2 is a VST3 room reverberation plugin from WireGrind, currently at version 2.1. It focuses on physically-informed acoustic modeling — simulating real room behavior rather than leaning on purely algorithmic approximations. ## Key Features

- **Acoustic Modeling** — A physically-informed wetness slider rebalances the underlying acoustic model for realistic depth.
- **Spectral Decay** — Frequency-dependent decay based on measured room acoustics, so highs and lows decay at different rates naturally.
- **1,000 Room Shapes** — Each shape produces unique early echo patterns and distinct temporal textures.
- **Dual Stereo Models** — Separate algorithms optimized for speaker playback versus headphone monitoring.
- **Three Freeze Modes** — Sustain, Add More, and Replace give you creative control over infinite reverb tails.
- **Echo Fluttering** — Inspired by acoustic flutter phenomena, this creates periodic waves of echoes for experimental sound design.
- **Full Automation** — All parameters can be automated in real-time with minimal artifacts.
