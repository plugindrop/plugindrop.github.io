## What Is Ozone 12 Standard?

Ozone 12 Standard is iZotope's mid-tier mastering suite — the step between the entry-level Elements and the full Advanced version. iZotope has been a dominant name in audio processing software for years, and Ozone is their flagship mastering product. Standard gives producers and engineers an AI-assisted, all-in-one mastering environment with a collection of processors purpose-built for the final stage of production.

---

## Key Features

- **Master Rebalance** — AI-powered stem separation that lets you adjust level balance (vocals, bass, drums) on a full mix without stems
- **Low End Focus** — spectral processing tool designed specifically to tighten and clarify the low frequency range
- **Vintage modules** — includes Vintage EQ, Vintage Limiter, and Vintage Tape emulations modeled on classic analog hardware
- **Match EQ** — analyze a reference track and apply its spectral profile to your master automatically
- **Imager** — multiband stereo width control for managing the stereo field at different frequency ranges
- **Maximizer** — look-ahead peak limiter with IRC (Intelligent Release Control) modes for transparent loudness
