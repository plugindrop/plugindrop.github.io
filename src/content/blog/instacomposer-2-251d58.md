## What you actually get

The vendor lists InstaComposer 2 as an Intelligent MIDI Generation plugin, meaning it writes playable MIDI rather than audio, so the output stays fully editable in your DAW. According to the product listing, it can create melodies, phrases, riffs, and chords, covering most of the idea-generation stages in a typical session.

The listing also notes it can generate 5 tracks simultaneously, which matters if you're sketching a full arrangement rather than one line at a time. Plugin Boutique lists automatic harmonies and chord progression generation, useful for building accompaniment without manual voicing work. It also lists controls for probability, complexity, and variation, giving you a way to dial generative output from sparse to busy without starting over.

Multiple time signatures are supported as well, according to the listing, which is relevant for anyone working outside straight 4/4.

## Where it fits in a session

This sits earliest in the workflow, before sound design or mixing begin. It's suited to the writing stage: sketching a chord progression, auditioning melodic variations, or generating a quick MIDI riff to bounce ideas off.

Because it routes across multiple tracks and channels, it also works as a sketch pad for arranging a full idea across bass, chords, and lead parts at once. From there you drag the generated MIDI into your own instrument tracks and keep editing normally.
