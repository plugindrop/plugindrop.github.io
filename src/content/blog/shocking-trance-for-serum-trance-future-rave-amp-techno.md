## Where it fits in a session

These presets are most useful at the sound-selection stage, before arrangement — pulling up a lead or pluck patch to sketch a hook, or auditioning bass patches against a kick to lock in low-end before the rest of the track is built. They also work well for quickly filling out a template when a deadline is tight and custom patch design isn't realistic.

In a trance or future rave context, that often means layering a preset lead under a supersaw stack, or using a pluck patch as the basis for a melodic arpeggio line. For techno, the same pack can supply darker bass or stab material that gets filtered and automated through the arrangement.
