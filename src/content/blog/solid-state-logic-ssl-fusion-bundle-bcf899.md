Solid State Logic is one of the most recognized names in professional audio — the company behind the consoles that shaped decades of commercial recordings. The SSL Fusion Bundle brings together plugin recreations of the processing modules found in SSL's Fusion hardware stereo processor, a rack unit designed for analog-style color, warmth, and control on mix buses and master chains. ## Key Features

- **Analog-Modeled Processing** — Each plugin in the bundle draws from the circuit topology of the SSL Fusion hardware, targeting the harmonic character and transient response associated with SSL's analog signal path.
- **Stereo Bus Focus** — The Fusion processing suite is designed specifically for stereo and mix bus applications, covering EQ, compression, saturation, and stereo imaging in a cohesive workflow.
- **High-Frequency Shelf EQ** — The Violet EQ module targets the upper frequency range with the smooth, musical lift that SSL's hardware version is known for.
- **HF Compression** — A dedicated high-frequency compressor for taming harshness on the bus without affecting the full-range dynamics.
- **Stereo Image Control** — Mid/side-based width adjustment built into the bundle's processing chain, allowing precise stereo field shaping.
