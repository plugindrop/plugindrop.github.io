## What Is Fluid Chords 2?

Fluid Chords 2 is a MIDI chord and progression generator plugin developed by Devious Machines, designed to help producers and songwriters build chord progressions directly inside their DAW. It works as a MIDI effect that generates and plays chords, which you then route into any virtual instrument. The plugin is aimed at streamlining the songwriting process, whether you're starting from scratch or looking to explore variations on an existing progression.

## Key Features

- **Chord Generator** — Suggests chord options based on scale and key, helping you build progressions without needing deep music theory knowledge.
- **Real-Time Playability** — Lets you trigger and audition chords live, so you can experiment with progressions in the moment rather than programming them note by note.
- **Scale & Key Awareness** — Keeps generated chords within your chosen key and scale, reducing dissonant or out-of-context voicings.
- **MIDI Output/Export** — Generated chords can be sent as MIDI to instruments or exported for further editing in your DAW's piano roll.
