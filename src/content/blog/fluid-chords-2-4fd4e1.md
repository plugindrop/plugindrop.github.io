## What you actually get

Fluid Chords 2 is a chord generation plugin built for producers who want to move between chord ideas quickly inside a DAW. According to the product listing, it runs as VST3, VST, AU, or a standalone app. The deal context frames it around a versatile chord generator at an affordable price point, which is the core pitch here rather than a long feature checklist.

## Where it fits in a session

This is a tool for the writing stage, not the mixing stage. Drop it early in a project when you're sketching progressions and want to audition variations before committing MIDI to a track. It sits upstream of your synths and sample instruments, feeding them chord data rather than processing audio.

- You're on Pro Tools and need AAX — AAX is not listed among the formats here.
