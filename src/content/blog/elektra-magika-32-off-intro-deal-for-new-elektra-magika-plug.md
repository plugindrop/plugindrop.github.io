## What Is Elektra Magika MultiLayerEQ?

MultiLayerEQ is a new equalizer plugin from independent developer Elektra Magika, built around a concept the company calls interval-based band layering. Rather than placing EQ bands at arbitrary Hz values, the plugin structures them around musical intervals — a design choice meant to make EQ decisions feel closer to how you already think about harmony, rather than how a frequency-analyzer graph displays it.

That's a meaningfully different starting point from the parametric EQ model that FabFilter, Waves, and most DAW-stock EQs use. Whether it's better depends entirely on how you work: if you mix by ear and think in scale degrees, an interval-aware layout could remove friction.
