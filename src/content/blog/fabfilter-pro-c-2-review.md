## What Is New in Pro-C 3

FabFilter lists these as the changes over Pro-C 2:

- **Six new compression styles.** Versatile, Smooth, Vari-Mu, El-Op (written "Op-El" in the press release), Upward and TTM (To The Max). That makes 14 styles in total, up from eight.
- **Character mode.** Three flavors of analog saturation, coloration and drift (Tube, Diode and Bright), with adjustable Drive and Pre/Post compression routing.
- **Side chain EQ.** Up to 6 bands, up from 3, with the filter shapes from Pro-Q 4 (including brickwall low-pass and high-pass) and mid-only or side-only processing per band.
- **Immersive audio.** Support for Dolby Atmos surround formats, listed as up to 9.1.6 on the product page, with routing that adapts to the host project.
- **Host tempo triggering.** A tempo-synced internal pulse with adjustable offset and length, for rhythmic ducking without an external trigger.
- **Auto Threshold.** Makes the threshold level-independent. FabFilter mentions dialog tracks as an example.
- **Adjustable maximum lookahead.** Lookahead is optional and goes up to 20 ms, with a customizable maximum to trade latency against lookahead.
- **Instance List.** Pro-C 3 instances can be controlled from the Instance List in Pro-Q 4 (version 4.10 or later), together with Pro-G and Pro-DS.
- **Redesigned interface and preset browser.** New design, plus a new preset browser with search, tags and metadata.
- **Oversampling.** Up to 32x, listed as improved.

## Features Carried Over and Listed on the Product Page

- Variable knee from hard knee to a 72 dB soft knee, and a Hold setting up to 500 ms
- Program-dependent attack and release curves, Auto Release, and intelligent Auto Gain
- External side chain and MIDI triggering
- Variable stereo linking and mid/side processing
- A Range setting and a Mix setting that scales gain change from 0% to 200%
- Level and gain change meters with peak and loudness display (Momentary mode of EBU R128 / ITU-R 1770)
- Audition Triggering, MIDI Learn, undo/redo, A/B comparison and sample-accurate automation
- Four interface sizes: Compact, Small, Medium and Large

## The Compression Styles

The vendor says Pro-C 3 has 14 program-dependent compression styles. The press release names the six new ones like this:

| Style | FabFilter's description |
|-------|--------------------------|
| Versatile | General-purpose algorithm for use on any material |
| Smooth | For smooth gluing at low ratios and longer envelope timings |
| Vari-Mu | Variable-mu compression inspired by classic hardware |
| El-Op / Op-El | Opto-like tube compression inspired by classic hardware |
| Upward | Pumping upward compression |
| TTM | Downward and upward compression combined across multiple bands |

We do not have vendor descriptions of the eight styles carried over from Pro-C 2 on the Pro-C 3 pages, so we do not describe them here.
