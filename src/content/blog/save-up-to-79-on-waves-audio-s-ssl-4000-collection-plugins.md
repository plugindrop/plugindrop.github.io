## What Is the Waves SSL 4000 Collection?

The Waves SSL 4000 Collection is a set of plugins modeled after the Solid State Logic 4000 series consoles — hardware that shaped the sound of countless chart-topping records from the 1980s onward. Developed in collaboration with SSL, the collection brings the character of the 4000 E and G series to your DAW. It's one of Waves' longest-standing and most recognized product lines, built for engineers who want that classic British analog console texture without the hardware price tag.

---

## Key Features

- **SSL E-Channel:** Models the channel strip from the SSL 4000 E Series, including EQ and dynamics drawn from the original hardware circuit.
- **SSL G-Channel:** Captures the slightly different EQ and compression character of the 4000 G Series console.
- **SSL G-Master Buss Compressor:** A dedicated bus compressor modeled after the G Series master bus section — widely associated with the "glue" compression sound on mix buses.
- **SSL G-Equalizer:** A standalone four-band parametric EQ based on the G Series console's filter topology.
- **Waves WaveSystem Toolbar:** Included across all plugins for A/B comparison, preset management, and undo/redo functionality.
- **DAW Compatibility:** Works as VST, AU, and AAX formats — check the product page for the full list of supported hosts and system requirements.
