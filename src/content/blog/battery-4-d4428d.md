## What Is Battery 4?

Battery 4 is a sample-based drum instrument developed by Native Instruments. It arranges sounds in a grid of cells, with each cell hosting its own sample, synthesis parameters, and effects chain, so you can build kits, load third-party sample libraries, or drag in your own audio. It runs as a VST/AU/AAX plugin inside your DAW and ships with a factory sound library on top of the engine itself.

## Key Features

- **Cell-based layout**: Each pad or cell in the grid can hold an independent sample, synth engine, or layered combination, making it suited to building custom kits fast.
- **Built-in sound-shaping tools**: Per-cell modulation and effects let you shape individual hits without leaving the instrument.
- **Sample import and drag-and-drop workflow**: You can load your own one-shots and loops directly into cells alongside the factory content.
- **Kit and library browser**: A built-in browser helps you navigate kits, cells, and presets across the factory library and any imported content.
- **DAW integration**: Runs as a plugin in standard formats, so it slots into existing production setups rather than requiring a standalone workflow.
