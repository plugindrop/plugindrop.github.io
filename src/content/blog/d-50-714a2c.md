## What Is D-50?

D-50 is a software recreation of Roland's original 1987 hardware synthesizer, one of the instruments credited with popularizing digital synthesis in mainstream music production. The original hardware used Roland's LA synthesis approach, layering PCM attack transients with synthesized tones to create sounds that were harder to achieve on purely analog gear at the time. This plugin version brings that engine into a modern VST/AU/AAX workflow, aimed at producers who want the specific character of the D-50 rather than a generic synthesizer replacement.
