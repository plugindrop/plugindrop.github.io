## What Is the Roland D-50?

The Roland D-50 is a software recreation of Roland's legendary 1987 hardware synthesizer — one of the most influential digital instruments ever made. The original hardware combined PCM sample attack transients with digitally synthesized sustain tones, a technique Roland called **Linear Arithmetic (LA) synthesis**. The result was a distinctive, lush sound that defined late-80s pop, new age, and film scores. This plugin brings that architecture to your DAW.

---

## Key Features

- **Linear Arithmetic (LA) Synthesis** — the original hybrid PCM + digital synthesis engine, faithfully recreated in software form
- **Original D-50 Patch Library** — includes the classic factory presets that made the hardware famous
- **Partials-Based Architecture** — combines up to four "partials" per tone, mixing sample and synth layers for the D-50's signature complexity
- **Onboard Effects** — reverb and chorus/delay processing built into the signal path, mirroring the original hardware workflow
- **Full Parameter Control** — all synthesis parameters accessible from the plugin interface, going beyond what was practical on the original hardware panel
