## What Is Spire?

Spire is a polyphonic virtual synthesizer developed by Reveal Sound, built specifically for electronic music production. It has become a go-to instrument for trance, psytrance, EDM, and progressive house producers worldwide. The plugin combines a high-performance sound engine with a deep modulation system, making it capable of everything from cutting supersaw leads to evolving pads and punchy basses. It's available in VST, AU, and AAX formats for both Windows and Mac.

---

## Key Features

- **4 Oscillators with Multiple Waveforms** — each oscillator supports classic waveforms plus more complex wave types, giving a wide palette to work from.
- **4 Filter Types per Voice** — includes state-variable, LP/HP/BP configurations and a zero-delay feedback filter for analog-style character.
- **16-slot Modulation Matrix** — flexible routing lets you assign LFOs, envelopes, and MIDI sources to nearly any parameter.
- **Built-in FX Chain** — chorus, phaser, reverb, delay, and distortion are all integrated, reducing the need for external processing on every patch.
- **Arpeggiator and Step Sequencer** — rhythmic and melodic pattern generation built directly into the synth.
- **Large Factory Preset Library** — ships with hundreds of presets covering the major EDM subgenres, useful as both starting points and finished sounds.
