## Where it fits in a session

Guitar loop packs like this tend to work best in a few specific spots in a lo-fi or jazz-hop session:

- **Foundation layer** — chopping loops into an MPC-style sampler to build a chord progression or main hook.
- **Texture under vocals** — sitting low in the mix behind a rap or sung vocal in chill hip-hop, adding warmth without competing for space.
- **Intro/outro atmosphere** — using a loop solo at the top or tail of a track before drums and bass come in.
- **Layering** — stacking a loop under a keys or Rhodes part to thicken the harmonic bed without adding a second full instrument.

Since these are loops rather than a playable instrument, the workflow is closer to sample-flipping than live performance — pick a phrase, chop it, pitch or time-stretch it to fit the key and tempo of the track.
