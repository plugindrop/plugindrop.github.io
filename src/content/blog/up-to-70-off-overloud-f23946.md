## What Is Overloud?

Overloud is an Italian audio software company known for high-fidelity analog hardware emulations and guitar amp modeling. Their product range spans two main lines: the **TH-U** series of guitar amp simulators and the **Gems** series of studio mixing/mastering plugins. The Gems lineup covers compressors, EQs, tape emulation, modulation, and delay — all modeled after classic analog studio hardware. The TH-U platform, meanwhile, combines amp modeling, cabinet simulation, and effects processing into a single guitar rig environment.

## Key Features

- **Gems Series (15+ plugins)** — Analog-modeled studio tools including the Comp76 (FET compressor), EQP (Pultec-style passive EQ), Comp670 (tube compressor), Tapedesk (console + tape emulation), and more.
- **Customizable Bundles** — The Bundle of 3 and Bundle of 5 let you pick exactly which Gems you want rather than forcing a fixed selection.
- **TH-U Premium Amp Sim** — Integrates modeling, rig capturing, and Fluid IR processing in one plugin, with officially licensed amps from Randall, THD, DV Mark, and Brunetti.
- **Fluid Modeling Technology** — Lets you swap preamp and power tube models inside TH-U, effectively modding any virtual amp with different tube combinations.
- **Artist Signature Editions** — TH-U packs designed with Marty Friedman, Greg Howe, Eric Gales, and others, each curated for specific tonal directions.
- **Cross-Platform** — Available as VST, AU, and AAX, with TH-U also available on iOS.
