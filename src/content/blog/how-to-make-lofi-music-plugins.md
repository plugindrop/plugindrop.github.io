## Vinyl & Noise Simulation

The vinyl layer is lo-fi's defining texture. Its role isn't just to add crackle — it shapes frequency response, adds a noise floor, and communicates the implied "era" of a recording. A useful approach is to treat it as an ambient texture sitting under the mix, not a sound effect sitting on top of it. ### iZotope Vinyl — A Free Vinyl Simulator

- **Developer:** iZotope
- **Price:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS
- **Formats:** VST, AU, AAX

iZotope Vinyl has been free since 2001, and it handles the vinyl fundamentals with precision. Its six modules (Warp, Dust & Scratches, Electrical, Mechanical, Aging, and a Year dial spanning 1930–1990) each target a distinct dimension of vinyl degradation.
