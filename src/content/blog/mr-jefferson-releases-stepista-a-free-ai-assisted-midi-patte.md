## What Is Stepista?

Stepista is a free MIDI pattern generator plugin by Mr. Jefferson that uses AI and music-theory algorithms to create drum patterns, basslines, melodies, and grooves. It runs inside your DAW as an instrument plugin, letting you route its MIDI output to any synth or sampler on another track. Think of it as a creative starting point — load a preset, tweak the parameters, drag the MIDI out, and build from there. Available in VST3 (Windows, macOS, Linux) and AU (macOS) formats.

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered Pattern Generation** — Combines artificial intelligence with music-theory algorithms to produce musically coherent MIDI patterns on the fly.
- **24 Factory Presets** — Covers genres including Ambient, House, Techno, and Trap, giving you instant starting points across styles.
- **9 Musical Modes** — Lock generated patterns to specific scales and keys, keeping everything harmonically in check.
- **13 Adjustable Parameters** — Fine-tune Swing, Octave, Accent, Velocity, and more to shape patterns to your track's feel.
- **Built-In Piano Roll Editor** — Edit generated patterns manually, move notes outside the chosen scale for intentional variation, or build patterns from scratch.
- **Drag-and-Drop MIDI Export** — Drag patterns directly into your DAW's arrangement for further editing and layering.
