## Pigments 7

<!-- role:pick -->

<!-- pick:Pigments 7 -->

Pigments 7 blends six synthesis engines — modal, granular, wavetable, sample, harmonic and virtual analog — along with a utility engine for extra oscillators and sampled noise. These engines can cross-modulate each other, and a dual-filter setup with series and parallel routing, plus twenty effect algorithms, lets you shape the sound further. Four assignable macro controls make even complex patches easy to tweak live. The factory library ships with more than 1,700 presets, so there's a huge range of starting points to explore.

Sonically, the instrument covers a lot of ground: lush pads, slowly evolving textures, razor-edged basses, and punchy leads are all within reach, and the preset library combined with those macro controls keeps intricate sounds playable and quick to customize on the fly.

That versatility makes it a natural fit for today's producers across a wide range of styles — hip hop, trap, bass music, and EDM on one end, house, electro, lo-fi, and electronica in the middle, and cinematic scoring on the other. Whether the goal is a thumping low-end bass patch, a wash of ambient pad, an intricate sequenced pattern, or a performance-ready sound for playing live, the engine and tool set are built to support it.

On the system side, it runs on macOS 11 or later (64-bit, with M1 support) or Windows 10 or later (64-bit only), needs at least 4 GB of RAM, a 4-core CPU around 3.4 GHz (or an M1 chip on Mac), and about 3 GB of free disk space. It's available as a standalone application or as a VST, AAX, Audio Unit, or NKS plugin, though only in 64-bit DAWs.

Arturia's update page lists these changes:

- **Redesigned Play View.** An audio-reactive Play Page that visualizes audio and key parameters, with quick-edit controls and animations per sound type.

- **New filters.** Rage, Ripple and Reverb.

- **New effect.** Corroder, described as a modulation-driven erosion effect.

- **Amplitude envelopes.** Smoother, S-shaped default envelopes that Arturia says reduce clicks.

- **New content.** 150 new factory presets, 50 wavetables, 30 samples and 20 noises, plus in-app tutorials.

- **Modulation and CPU.** Modulation ranges are now shown visually, and Arturia says optimized processing gives extra headroom for complex modulation, layered engines and heavy effect chains.

Arturia also lists Explorations Volume 5 as introduced with Pigments 7: three expansions (Hazy, Dubstep and IDM Explorations) at 150 presets each. These are sold separately in the Arturia sound store.

We do not have a vendor page that compares Pigments 7 feature by feature against Pigments 5 or 6, so the list above covers only what Arturia describes as new in version 7.

- **Engines.** Six synthesis types and a Utility engine. Arturia also lists external audio input and engine cross-modulation.

- **Filters.** Dual filters with series and parallel routing.

- **Effects.** Two insert buses and a send bus, with three effects per bus, chosen from 20 algorithms that include a shimmer reverb, a multiband compressor and a vocoder.

- **Modulation.** Drag-and-drop assignment with envelopes, LFOs, function generators, random generators and four assignable macros. Arturia says connections are unlimited.

- **Sequencer.** A generative sequencer and arpeggiator for polymetric sequences.

- **Interface.** Resizable 4K interface with dark and light themes.

Arturia's own pages disagree on a few details: one describes three engine slots, another lists two engines in parallel, and filter counts differ between pages. We therefore do not state an engine-slot count or an exact filter-type count here. Check the vendor page before buying if either matters to you.

## FAQ

<!-- role:faq -->

Q: How much does Arturia Pigments 7 cost?
A: Pigments 7 is sold as a one-time purchase through Arturia's own store and authorized retailers, with the regular price tracked and displayed on those product pages rather than fixed here. It sits above the free Pigments Play edition, reflecting its full feature set.

Q: What is the current version of Pigments?
A: Pigments 7 is the current release, superseding earlier versions such as Pigments 5.

Q: How many synthesis engines does Pigments have?
A: It includes six synthesis types — Modal, Granular, Wavetable, Sample, Harmonic, and Virtual Analog — plus a separate Utility engine that adds extra oscillators and sampled noise sources.

Q: Is there a free version of Pigments?
A: Yes, a reduced free edition called Pigments Play is available. It includes 100 presets, limited parameter control, and no modulation control, in contrast to the full version's complete parameter and modulation access.

Q: Is Pigments 7 a paid upgrade for owners of Pigments 5 or 6?
A: Pigments 7 is distributed as a free update for people who already own an earlier Pigments license, rather than as a separate paid purchase. Details about exceptions for specific accounts are not spelled out, so anyone relying on this should confirm eligibility through their own account before assuming it applies.

Q: What are the system requirements?
A: The software needs Windows 10 or later (64-bit only) or macOS 11 or later (also 64-bit only, with M1 Macs supported), at least 4 GB of RAM, a 4-core CPU running at 3.4 GHz (or an M1 chip on Mac), and 3 GB of free hard disk space. It runs as a standalone application or as a VST, AAX, Audio Unit, or NKS plugin inside a 64-bit digital audio workstation.
