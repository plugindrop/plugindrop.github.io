## What Is SSL G3 MultiBusComp?

The SSL G3 MultiBusComp is a bus compressor plugin developed by Solid State Logic, the British manufacturer whose 4000-series mixing consoles defined the sound of commercial recording for decades. The plugin is rooted in the G-Series VCA bus compressor — the hardware section responsible for the "glue" compression that has appeared on major releases across pop, rock, and electronic music since the 1980s. Where the hardware unit lived on a single stereo bus, the G3 MultiBusComp extends that topology to handle multiple bus compression scenarios within a single plugin.

## Key Features

- **G-Series VCA circuit emulation** — models the hardware compression behavior that made the SSL 4000G console a studio standard.
- **Multiple bus compression** — designed to manage compression across several buses simultaneously, rather than limiting use to a single stereo mix bus.
- **Attack and release controls** — classic fast/slow options matching the original hardware's character, suited to transient shaping and sustained glue.
- **Auto release mode** — program-dependent release behavior that adapts to material dynamics without manual riding.
- **Ratio selection** — stepped ratio options consistent with the hardware G-Bus circuit design.
- **Makeup gain** — adjustable output gain to compensate for gain reduction at the compression stage.
