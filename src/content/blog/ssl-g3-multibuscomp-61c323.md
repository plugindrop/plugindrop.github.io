## What Is SSL G3 MultiBusComp?

The SSL G3 MultiBusComp is an official plugin from Solid State Logic (SSL), the British manufacturer behind some of the most iconic mixing consoles in recording history. It models the legendary bus compressor circuit from the SSL 4000 G-Series console — the same "glue" compression heard on decades of chart-topping records. As a first-party SSL plugin, it carries the authenticity of the original hardware maker rather than a third-party emulation.

## Key Features

- **G-Series Bus Compressor Circuit** — Models the analog behavior of the SSL 4000 G-Series console's built-in stereo bus compressor.
- **Multi-Bus Capability** — Designed to run across multiple buses in your session, not just the master fader, giving you more routing flexibility.
- **Classic Attack & Release Controls** — The fast/slow attack and release options mirror the hardware's key settings for dialing in glue or transient shaping.
- **Ratio Selection** — Multiple ratio settings available to go from subtle density to more aggressive parallel-style compression.
- **Makeup Gain** — Onboard gain control to compensate for gain reduction without leaving your DAW.
- **Authentic SSL Metering** — VU-style gain reduction metering consistent with the hardware console aesthetic.
