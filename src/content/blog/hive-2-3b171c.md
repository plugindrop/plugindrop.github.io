## What Is Hive 2?

Hive 2 is a wavetable synthesizer plugin developed by u-he, a German software company known for synths like Diva and Zebra. It was designed as a leaner, faster alternative to u-he's flagship synths, aimed at producers who need quick sound design turnaround for electronic music. Hive 2 runs as a VST, AU, and AAX plugin on Windows and macOS.

## Key Features

- **Wavetable oscillators** — Hive 2 uses wavetable synthesis as its core sound engine, giving access to evolving, modern textures common in EDM and electronic genres.
- **Built-in effects chain** — includes onboard effects for shaping the signal without reaching for third-party processors first.
- **Arpeggiator and step sequencer** — useful for building rhythmic, movement-driven patches directly inside the synth.
- **Modulation system** — a routing setup for connecting envelopes, LFOs, and other modulators to synth parameters.
- **Low CPU footprint** — Hive was originally positioned by u-he as a lighter-weight synth compared to Zebra or Diva, aimed at producers stacking many instances in a session.
- **Preset library** — ships with factory presets covering a range of electronic music styles; check the product page for the exact preset count.
