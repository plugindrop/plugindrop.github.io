- **If you produce hip-hop, trap, or drill**, prioritize machine drum packs and construction kits — the hardware-sourced 808 character is not replicable from acoustic recordings.
- **If you mix live bands or produce singer-songwriter content**, studio acoustic kits with multi-mic options give you the room control that processed packs eliminate entirely.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between a one-shot pack and a construction kit?**
One-shots are individual drum hits — a single kick, snare, or hi-hat — that you sequence yourself. Construction kits include pre-arranged loop stems and sometimes full MIDI patterns, giving you a structural starting point. One-shots offer more flexibility; construction kits are faster to use.

**Do I need to buy format-specific packs for my DAW?**
No. Standard WAV files at 24-bit work universally across Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Studio One, and every other major DAW. Format-specific concerns only arise with sampler instruments (Kontakt, Decent Sampler) — check the pack description if you want those.

**How many velocity layers do I need in an acoustic drum pack?**
For realistic groove feel, four velocity layers is the usable minimum. Six to eight layers is better for dynamic passages. Fewer than four layers produces a mechanical repetition artifact — the "machine gun" effect — that undermines the natural feel you are paying the acoustic premium for.

**Are Loopmasters packs royalty-free for commercial use?**
Yes. Packs purchased through Loopmasters are royalty-free for use in commercial productions. Always read the specific license for a pack before releasing a track based heavily on construction kit loops — some full-loop uses carry additional terms.

**Can I use drum sample packs without a sampler plugin?**
Yes. All WAV-format drum packs work directly in any DAW's built-in sampler or drum rack — Ableton's Drum Rack, FL Studio's FPC, Logic's Ultrabeat, or Simpler. You do not need a third-party sampler plugin unless the pack was specifically designed for one like Kontakt or Battery.
