## What Is Scaler 3?

Scaler 3 is a music theory and composition tool developed by Scaler Music (formerly Plugin Boutique's in-house brand). It's designed to help producers discover chords, progressions, and melodies — even without deep theory knowledge. The plugin detects what key and scale you're working in, suggests chord progressions that fit, and provides performance tools to turn those ideas into full arrangements. Scaler has earned multiple awards since its original release and has become one of the most recognized theory tools in the plugin market.

Scaler EQ and DC Snares round out this sale. Scaler EQ brings a musically-informed approach to equalization, while DC Snares is a dedicated snare construction kit built for layering and shaping snare sounds from scratch.

## Key Features

- **Chord Detection & Suggestion Engine** — Scaler 3 analyzes audio or MIDI input to identify keys and scales, then suggests chords and progressions that work musically.
- **Performance Modes** — Includes arpeggiator, strumming, and humanization tools to turn static chords into expressive, playable parts.
- **Musical EQ Approach (Scaler EQ)** — Scaler EQ ties frequency adjustments to musical notes and harmonics, offering a different workflow from traditional parametric EQs.
- **Snare Layering Engine (DC Snares)** — DC Snares lets producers build snares from individual components — top, body, bottom, transient — with dedicated processing per layer.
- **DAW Integration** — Scaler 3 routes MIDI output to other instruments in your session, functioning as a composition hub within your DAW.

## Who Is It For?

- **Bedroom producers & beatmakers** — Scaler 3 is designed for creators who want professional-sounding progressions without years of theory training.
- **Singer-songwriters** — The chord suggestion engine helps break out of repetitive patterns and discover new harmonic directions.
- **Sound designers & drum programmers** — DC Snares is built specifically for producers who want granular control over snare construction rather than relying on one-shot samples.
- **Mixing engineers exploring musical EQ** — Scaler EQ offers a note-based EQ workflow that may appeal to those who think in terms of pitch rather than raw frequency values.
